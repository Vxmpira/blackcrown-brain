# Watcher Skeptics, the angle doc

For Herald. How to sell Korvus to traders who distrust every tool pitch. Written 10-30, grounded in brand law and the first 7d of real traffic (53 visits, baseline week).

## Who the skeptic is
Index futures trader, burned by guru products, allergic to profit promises. Will not pay on a claim. Will try something free if it costs nothing and asks nothing.

## The frame that works
The watcher. Korvus is not a signal seller, it is surveillance. It watches the wire 24/7, scores headlines, reads cross index SMT, and explains why price moves. It never predicts, never promises. That restraint IS the pitch to a skeptic.

## The proof move
Observer tier, free, no card. The ask is never buy, it is judge the output yourself. Every skeptic post ends at the free door, not the paywall.

## Rules per post
- One claim per post, never stacked.
- A number beats an adjective (24/7, 9.99, free, no card).
- Trader voice, no hype, two hashtags max on X.
- Not-advice disclaimer on any product claim about market reads.
- Never promise profit, never predict price.
