# Every Headline, Scored. No More Guessing Why ES Just Moved.

You know the drill. Twelve tabs open. Three news sites. A Twitter feed moving too fast to read. ES drops six points and you have no idea which headline did it.

That is the problem Korvus was built to fix.

The terminal pulls in market news as it breaks. Then it scores every headline for market impact. Not vibes. Not someone's hot take. A score, so you can see at a glance what matters and what is noise.

A Fed speaker clears their throat? Scored. CPI prints hot? Scored. Some company you have never heard of misses earnings? Scored, and probably low, because it does not move index futures.

The result is simple. When ES or NQ moves, you look at one screen and see the likely reason. No digging. No asking a Discord full of strangers what just happened.

We are not going to tell you this makes you profitable. It does not trade for you and it does not predict anything. It does one job: it tells you what the market is reacting to, right now, in plain terms.

That is it. News in. Scores out. Context when you need it.
