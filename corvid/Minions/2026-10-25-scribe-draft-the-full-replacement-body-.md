# Eclipse-X

Most communities sell you something. A course. A mentorship. A funnel dressed up as friendship. Eclipse-X is not that.

Eclipse-X is a free Discord for independent operators. People building in trading, AI, and ecommerce. People who do the work themselves and want to compare notes with others doing the same.

## What it is

A room. That's it. No gatekeepers. No tiered access. No locked channels waiting behind a credit card.

You get conversations with people who are actually in the trenches. Traders talking process, not signals. Builders sharing what broke and what they fixed. Store owners trading notes on what they tested last week.

## What it is not

No gurus. Nobody here claims to have the answer, because nobody does.

No courses. We are not teaching you a system. There is no system.

No upsell. Free means free. There is no premium tier hiding behind the welcome message. Nothing to buy, ever.

## Who it is for

People who work alone but think better with others around. People tired of communities that exist to warm up a sales pitch. People who want a straight answer or an honest "I don't know."

If that sounds like you, the door is open.

**Join Eclipse-X: [discord.gg/eclipse-x](https://discord.gg/eclipse-x)**

No pitch on the other side. Just the room.
