**Finding:** The strongest long tail angles are the ones Korvus already computes (session times, SMT divergence, prior day levels), because the free page can show a live example of the thing it explains. Search volume is not instrumented yet, so this ranking rests on fit and intent, not measured demand.

**Ranked angles**

1. **Futures session times in ET** (`/p/futures-session-times-et`)
   - Intent: utility lookup. Users want open and close times they can bookmark, so they return.
   - Title: "Futures Session Times in ET: Globex Hours Guide" (47 chars)
   - Pitch: Session clocks in ET, live on your Korvus board, free to start so you never miss an open.

2. **What is SMT divergence** (`/p/what-is-smt-divergence`)
   - Intent: informational, definition-seeking. Beginners want the concept explained in plain terms.
   - Title: "What Is SMT Divergence? ES, NQ, YM Explained" (44 chars)
   - Pitch: Watch cross index SMT divergence on the Korvus board as you learn what the pair is showing, free with an Observer account.

3. **Prior day high and low levels** (`/p/prior-day-high-low-levels`)
   - Intent: reference and education. The query is commonly phrased as "strategy," but the page should explain the levels, not prescribe a play.
   - Title: "Prior Day High and Low: Levels to Know" (38 chars)
   - Pitch: See the prior day high and low marked against today's session, free on Observer, and learn what traders watch them for.

4. **Premium and discount zones** (`/p/premium-discount-zones-explained`)
   - Intent: conceptual, mid-level. Readers are often already in ICT material and want a clear definition.
   - Title: "Premium and Discount Zones Explained for Futures" (48 chars)
   - Pitch: A plain English primer on premium and discount ranges, with the live board so you can see the concept on current price, free to start.

5. **Why is ES moving today** (`/p/why-is-es-moving-today`)
   - Intent: news-driven and time sensitive, likely the highest raw volume of the five, but the weakest fit. Honest answers invite direction calls, which Korvus cannot make.
   - Title: "Why Is ES Moving Today? What the Wire Shows" (43 chars)
   - Pitch: The scored headlines and USD agenda behind today's tape, shown with impact and confidence, free on Observer. Context, not calls.
