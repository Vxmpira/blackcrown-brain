# Korvus Monthly Watcher: 15 Second Vertical Direction

**Desk:** Bard (video creative)

**Angle:** All seeing watcher, 24/7 market surveillance while the trader sleeps
**Rung moved:** First paying customer (Korvus Operator), through the free Observer tier as the entry point

## 1. Hook (0 to 1 second)

The first frame opens on a dark bedroom at night, mostly black with a soft pink rim light on the edge of a desk. Within the first second, a single thin pink scan line slides left to right across a glowing monitor, as if the screen is waking up. The camera begins a slow push-in from a few feet back at the same moment. The motion is the hook, so nothing in the frame should be still during that first second.

## 2. Hero and single idea

The one idea is the terminal as a watcher that never blinks. The hero is a widescreen monitor, or a tall phone-width panel if the set is staged that way, showing an abstract market terminal: layered glowing line traces, soft pink and white candles with no readable digits, a faint grid, and a thin pink sweep line. Every glyph on the screen must be unreadable texture. No letters, no numbers, no legible chart labels, no recognizable logos. The room around it stays black with pink accent light, the brand palette of dark pink and black.

## 3. Camera and motion

- **0 to 1 s:** Scan line sweeps across the monitor while the camera begins a slow dolly in.
- **1 to 5 s:** Steady push-in toward the screen. The traces keep moving at a calm, even pace.
- **5 to 9 s:** A gentle lateral drift to the left, revealing the empty bed and a rumpled blanket in the foreground, out of focus. This implies the trader is asleep while the screen keeps working.
- **9 to 13 s:** Slow pull back to a medium wide, the monitor glowing in a dark room.
- **13 to 15 s:** Camera settles, holds on the lower third of the frame where the glow is strongest and the negative space is clean.

## 4. Lighting and mood

Key light is the monitor itself: pink and cool white spill onto the desk edge and the ceiling. A faint pink rim catches a glass of water and the edge of a chair. Shadows are deep and close. The mood is quiet, vigilant, and calm, never urgent or frantic. No flashing red, no alarm imagery, no money imagery of any kind. No stacks of cash, no gain animations, no profit visuals.

## 5. Mid-clip payoff beat (about 6 to 8 seconds)

At the midpoint, the scan line completes a full sweep and the traces briefly brighten and settle, as if the watcher has just finished a pass over the whole market. This is the visual payoff: the system is awake and covering ground. Keep this beat subtle, a single soft pulse of pink light, not a flash.

## 6. Closing shot (13 to 15 seconds)

The closing frame holds steady on the monitor's lower portion, where the glow is clean and empty. This negative space is where the forge bakes the destination line after render. Footage itself contains no wordmark, no logo, no text. The final half second fades the pink glow down to a deep black vignette, leaving the frame dark with a faint pink edge.

Total: 15 seconds. The beats fall at 1, 3, 6, 8, and 13 seconds, matching the timing above. The clip stays within the 10 to 15 second window and the motion never stops.
