# Crown Vertical Clip Direction: "The Room Thinks"

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 12 seconds, 4K source, graded for phone screens
**Subject:** Crown's Deep-Logic room, shown as a glowing terminal in a dark room
**One idea:** Real reasoning takes real compute, and you can watch it happen.
**Brand fit:** Private, serious, operator-grade. No guru theater, no hype, no claims beyond the product.

---

## Palette and Look

- **Base:** Near-black with a faint violet undertone, around #0A0008. Shadows stay crushed.
- **Primary glow:** Deep pink, around #FF2A8A, used only for light sources: screen glow, rim lights, streaming particles.
- **Highlights:** Pale pink-white at the hottest points, around #FFD6EA, used sparingly.
- **Grade:** Low saturation outside the pink, soft bloom on highlights, slight halation. No warm skin tones, no bright whites anywhere.
- **Contrast:** High. Most of the frame should be black with pink light carving out shapes.

---

## Sound Design (Suggested)

- Near silence at the open. A single soft mechanical tick on the hook frame.
- Low synth pulse fades in at 0.8 seconds and builds gently through the middle.
- The pulse quickens slightly during the reasoning stream, then drops out on the final hold.
- No vocal track. No trending audio that clashes with the brand's serious tone.

## Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: Hook (0.00 to 1.00)

**Motion in the first second:** A hard pink rim light sweeps left to right across a black glass surface, and the camera pushes in on the sweep in the same beat.

- **Subject:** The edge of a dark glass screen, mostly out of frame at first.
- **Camera:** Starts static at frame 0, then a fast push-in (roughly 1.5x speed of the later dolly) that lands on the glass edge by 1.00.
- **Lighting:** A single hard pink key from camera-left, flaring as it crosses the glass. Everything else stays black.
- **Pacing:** One sharp cut from black at frame 0. The motion is already moving at frame 1 so the hook lands before the viewer can scroll.

### Scene 2: The Terminal Wakes (1.00 to 4.00)

- **Subject:** A glowing terminal-style panel filling the frame. Its interface is rendered as soft, unreadable horizontal bars of pink light. Do not generate any readable characters, glyphs, or symbols. Keep the bars blurred and abstract.
- **Camera:** Slow, steady dolly in, eased. Shallow depth of field, focus pulling gently from the panel edge to its center.
- **Motion:** Light bars resolve from scattered to aligned, like a system coming online. Subtle scan-line shimmer across the glass.
- **Lighting:** Panel glow is the only light source. Faint bloom around the bars. The room stays black beyond the panel.
- **Mood:** Quiet focus. The machine is ready.

### Scene 3: The Reasoning Stream (4.00 to 8.00)

This is the money shot. The product's Deep-Logic mode is visibly working.

- **Subject:** The same terminal panel, now active. Thin streams of pink particles flow from the bottom of the frame up into the panel, densifying over the scene. The light bars pulse in rhythm with the stream.
- **Camera:** Dolly continues, slightly slower, then a subtle rack focus from the panel's upper edge to a bright cluster of particles at mid-frame around 6.00.
- **Motion:** Particle density increases through the scene. The stream reads as heavier at about 6.50, which visually signals that deeper reasoning is consuming more compute.
- **Lighting:** Glow intensifies gradually. The brightest moment lands around 6.50 with a pale pink-white hot spot, then settles.
- **Pacing:** Build, not a jump. The increase should feel like a machine working harder, not a flash effect.

**Illustration note (not on screen):** The particle density is an illustration of Deep-Logic's token burn, showing that deeper reasoning uses more compute. It is a visual metaphor only. No token counts, meters, or numbers appear on screen.

### Scene 4: The Answer Settles (8.00 to 11.00)

- **Subject:** The panel calms. Particles thin out and settle into a steady, even glow.
- **Camera:** Slow pull back from the panel, revealing that the surrounding room is entirely black. The panel becomes one light source in a dark space.
- **Motion:** Particle flow slows and stops. Light bars return to a calm, aligned resting state.
- **Lighting:** Glow drops to a steady mid-level pink. Rim light on the glass edge persists as a single line.
- **Mood:** Composed. The work is done and it is precise.

### Scene 5: Final Hold (11.00 to 12.00)

- **Subject:** The single pink rim light on the glass edge, against black.
- **Camera:** Locked off. Completely still.
- **Motion:** Minimal. A very slow breathing pulse in the glow, then a fade to black on the final frame.
- **Lighting:** One pink line. Everything else is black.
- **Pacing:** Hold long enough for the frame to register before the loop or end card. Leave the upper and lower thirds clean for Bard's overlay lines.
