# Wire E: Full Shot Direction

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds, no emblem, no crown, no raven mark
**Concept:** A pale pink perception sweep moves across a near-black trading floor. The camera is the watcher. It never cuts fast, and it never shows a readable screen.
**Mood:** Cold, watchful, patient. Quiet confidence, no hype.
**Motion rule:** Motion begins inside the first second, at frame zero.

## Overlay Schedule (baked on after render, not in the footage)

| Overlay | Start | Duration | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook: "The tape never sleeps" | 0.0s | 3.0s | Upper third, centered |
| Payoff: "Watching so you don't have to" | 5.0s | 4.0s | Upper third, centered, Shot 3 keeps this zone quiet |
| Destination: "korvus.industries" | 10.0s | 3.0s | Lower third, centered, Shot 5 keeps this zone quiet |

Overlay text is forged onto the video in post. The footage itself contains no words, numbers, or symbols.

## Shot List

### Shot 1: Wake (0.0s to 2.5s)
- **Overlay:** Hook, "The tape never sleeps," runs 0.0s to 3.0s.
- **Subject:** An empty trading floor, rows of dark desks receding into shadow. One chair sits at the center, angled toward the glow.
- **Motion:** At frame zero, a pale pink perception sweep rakes left to right across the floor like a slow searchlight. The camera begins a gentle forward push at 0.4s.
- **Camera:** Low, roughly knee height. Slow dolly forward, steady, no handheld shake.
- **Lighting:** Near-black ambient. The only color is the pale pink sweep, which lights edges of desks and the chair back. Faint cool haze in the air.
- **Pacing:** Slow. The sweep crosses the frame in about 2 seconds.
- **Framing note:** Keep the upper third darkest for the hook overlay.

### Shot 2: Attention (2.5s to 5.0s)
- **Overlay:** None. Hook is still on until 3.0s, then the frame breathes.
- **Subject:** A bank of dark monitors, blank-faced, glowing with soft pink light that ripples across their surfaces. No readable content on any screen; the glow is abstract, like light moving through water.
- **Motion:** Camera tilts slowly down from the ceiling to the monitor bank. The pink ripple travels across the screens from right to left.
- **Camera:** Starts high, tilts down about 30 degrees over 2.5 seconds. Smooth gimbal feel.
- **Lighting:** Pink glow from the monitors is the key light. Deep shadow between the screens. Slight bloom on the brightest pink points.
- **Pacing:** Steady and unhurried. The ripple moves at about half the speed of Shot 1's sweep.

### Shot 3: Payoff Room (5.0s to 8.0s)
- **Overlay:** Payoff, "Watching so you don't have to," runs 5.0s to 9.0s. This shot is built for it.
- **Subject:** A long wall of dark glass along the edge of the floor, reflecting a soft pale pink gradient. A single silhouette of an empty chair sits in the reflection, not occupied.
- **Motion:** Camera moves laterally along the glass wall, left to right. The pink reflection slides across the surface in the same direction, very slowly.
- **Camera:** Slow lateral dolly, about 1 meter over 3 seconds. Locked level.
- **Lighting:** Soft pink reflection across the glass, deep black beyond it. Light falls off toward the bottom of the frame.
- **Pacing:** The slowest shot in the piece. Hold the rhythm.
- **Framing note:** The upper 35% of the frame stays nearly empty dark glass so the payoff line reads cleanly. Subject sits in the lower two thirds.

### Shot 4: The Sweep (8.0s to 10.5s)
- **Overlay:** Payoff continues until 9.0s, then the frame clears. Destination begins at 10.0s.
- **Subject:** A broad pale pink arc of light sweeps across the entire floor from a point far back in the room, like a watcher's gaze moving through the space.
- **Motion:** The arc expands outward from the back of the room toward the lens. Camera pulls back slightly, opening the frame to show the full floor.
- **Camera:** Slow pullback, about 1.5 meters over 2.5 seconds. Slight upward tilt to reveal more ceiling.
- **Lighting:** The sweep is the primary light, bright at its leading edge and fading behind it. Floor reflects the pink in a long soft streak.
- **Pacing:** Accelerates slightly as the arc widens, then settles at the end of the shot.

### Shot 5: Close Watch (10.5s to 13.0s)
- **Overlay:** Destination, "korvus.industries," runs 10.0s to 13.0s. This shot keeps the lower third quiet.
- **Subject:** Extreme close macro of a dark glass panel edge. The pale pink light traces a thin line along the edge, pulsing gently.
- **Motion:** Camera drifts slowly upward along the glass edge. The pink line brightens and dims in a slow, even breath.
- **Camera:** Macro-close, very slow vertical move, about 0.5 meters over 2.5 seconds. Shallow depth of field with the background soft.
- **Lighting:** Single pink rim light on the glass edge. Everything else is near-black.
- **Pacing:** Still and watchful. The pulse runs about one cycle per 1.5 seconds.
- **Framing note:** Keep the lower third dark so the destination line reads cleanly.

### Shot 6: Hold (13.0s to 15.0s)
- **Overlay:** None. Clean ending.
- **Subject:** Return to the empty trading floor, wide, with the single chair from Shot 1 now lit only by a fading pink residue.
- **Motion:** The pink light dims slowly to near black. The camera stops its movement at 13.5s and holds still for the final seconds.
- **Camera:** Locked wide. No movement after 13.5s.
- **Lighting:** Pink fades from the edges inward until only a faint trace remains on the chair back. Ends in near darkness.
- **Pacing:** Final fade over about 2 seconds. Hold the last frame for the final beat.
