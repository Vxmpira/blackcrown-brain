**Director Brief: Korvus, "The Watch Never Sleeps" (11-10, 15 seconds, 9:16)**

**Opening line:** A single unbroken push-in down a dark hallway, already moving on frame one, toward a half-open door where pink light spills across the floor.

**Concept:** One idea: the market never sleeps, and something is watching it for you. The watcher is never shown as a person or a creature. The viewer feels the gaze through camera movement and light.

**Subject:** A sleeping trader's bedroom at night. The trader is a soft silhouette under a blanket, face turned away, never identifiable. The desk holds several monitors whose screens glow pink and show only slow, abstract waveforms and soft scan bands. No readable marks, no charts with values, no green candles, no money imagery of any kind.

**Shot breakdown:**

- **0.0 to 1.0s:** The camera is already moving. A slow dolly-in down the hallway. A thin pink scanline crosses the frame from left to right on the first beat.
- **1.0 to 4.0s:** The camera enters the room through the door. Low, steady push toward the desk. The monitors pulse in a slow rhythm, waveforms rising and settling, like breathing.
- **4.0 to 8.0s:** A faint grid of pink scan light sweeps across the room, passing over the sleeping silhouette and the bed, then pausing on the monitors. This is the watcher's gaze. It moves deliberately, never frantically.
- **8.0 to 12.0s:** The camera rises toward the ceiling, looking down on the bed and desk as the room shrinks. Through the window, the city lights blur softly and pulse at a slow, uneven rhythm.
- **12.0 to 15.0s:** The camera pulls back through the window into the night sky. A pink edge glow hangs at the frame border. The image fades to black with one final slow pulse of light.

**Lighting:** Near-total darkness. Magenta-pink rim light on edges, soft pink glow from the monitors, a faint accent glow from the city outside. Heavy edge vignette. Light film grain. No hard highlights.

**Pacing:** Slow and deliberate, under one major move per beat. Nothing cuts. Every motion is continuous, so the viewer feels a single unbroken watch.

**Mood:** Quiet vigilance, not menace. Calm, patient, a little eerie in its stillness. The feeling is "someone is awake so you don't have to be," with no hype and no urgency.

**Honesty check:** No profit imagery, no implied returns, no trading signals. The screens show only abstract motion, so nothing in the frame reads as a claim.

**Overlay:** Any words that appear on screen come from the creative's overlay field and are baked on after render. None are generated in the footage.

**Repeat of opening line:** A single unbroken push-in down a dark hallway, already moving on frame one, toward a half-open door where pink light spills across the floor.
