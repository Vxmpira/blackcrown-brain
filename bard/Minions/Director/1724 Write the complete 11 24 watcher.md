**KORVUS, WATCHER TOWER (11-24)**
Monolith format · 15 seconds · 9:16 vertical · 4 shots of about 3.75 seconds each

**Concept:** A single brutalist watchtower rises out of a dark, sleeping city. It never closes its eyes. The clip is the all-seeing watcher idea: the market is being observed around the clock while the trader sleeps. Cold monochrome, one soft pink accent, nothing else.

**Shared look for all four shots:** Cold monochrome palette of charcoal, slate, and near-black. Film grain throughout. A faint vertical grid of light falls across the concrete. Soft pink accent appears only as a thin glow from the tower's upper windows and the horizon line. No flat black fields; keep the air hazy with depth. Slow drift on every shot, never a static frame. Tiny lone silhouettes appear in each shot to show scale.

---

**SHOT 1 (0.00 to 3.75 seconds): The Tower Rises**

Motion starts in the first frame. The camera begins low at street level, angled up, and drifts slowly upward along the face of a massive brutalist tower made of stacked concrete blocks. The tower fills the frame edge to edge and disappears into a dark sky above. Night fog moves slowly across the base, catching faint cold light.

Lighting: a single cold source from a distant streetlamp rakes across the concrete, creating hard shadows in the ridges of the blocks. A soft pink glow rises from a narrow slit window near the top of the tower, the only warm color in the frame.

Subject: one tiny silhouette of a person stands at the base of the tower, far below, facing the building. The figure is almost lost in scale.

Pacing: steady, deliberate, heavy. The upward drift has the weight of something enormous waking.

Mood: silent, vast, watchful. The tower feels inhabited without anything moving inside it.

---

**SHOT 2 (3.75 to 7.50 seconds): The Watch Begins**

Motion starts in the first frame. The camera pushes slowly forward toward the tower's upper floors, closing distance as the fog thins. The frame moves from the broad facade toward a grid of identical rectangular windows, most dark, a few catching faint reflections.

Lighting: cold blue-gray ambient light fills the windows. One window in the center of the grid glows with the soft pink accent, steady and unblinking.

Subject: a second tiny silhouette stands in the opening of a distant rooftop across an empty plaza. It holds still, looking up at the tower, mirroring the watch.

Pacing: the push forward accelerates very slightly, then settles, giving the sense of focus narrowing.

Mood: attention sharpening. The tower is watching, and so is something across the street.

---

**SHOT 3 (7.50 to 11.25 seconds): Surveillance Sweep**

Motion starts in the first frame. The camera tracks laterally across the tower's midsection in a smooth horizontal sweep, as if the tower's gaze is moving across the city. Thin horizontal bands of light slide slowly across the concrete, like scanning lines passing over a surface, without any text or graphics.

Lighting: the bands of cold light are gray-white, with the soft pink accent appearing briefly at the leading edge of each band and fading as it passes. The shadows deepen behind each band.

Subject: a small cluster of three silhouettes walks along a dim street far below, moving in and out of the light bands. Their scale keeps them small and anonymous.

Pacing: the sweep is even and unhurried, with a constant rhythm, like a patient pulse.

Mood: relentless, tireless, and calm. Nothing is missed, and nothing is rushed.

---

**SHOT 4 (11.25 to 15.00 seconds): The Watch Holds**

Motion starts in the first frame. The camera pulls back and upward in one slow motion, revealing the full height of the tower against an enormous dark sky, with the city's lights spread thinly across the bottom of the frame. The drift ends with the tower centered and still, its top dissolving into fog.

Lighting: the sky is near-black with a faint cold haze. The soft pink glow from the upper window returns, now the clearest point of warmth in the entire clip, holding steady as the frame settles.

Subject: the lone silhouette from Shot 1 reappears at the base, now seen small against the full structure, standing still while the tower keeps watch above.

Pacing: the pullback slows to near stillness at the final frame, then holds for the last beat of the clip.

Mood: quiet, resolved, watchful. The tower keeps watching while the city sleeps. The message is the brand's edge, stated without any voice: the market is observed around the clock, and so is the trader's edge.
