# Korvus Watcher: Video Direction

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 12 seconds (acceptable range 10 to 15)
**Angle:** The all-seeing watcher. The market is surveilled around the clock while the trader sleeps.
**Creative owner:** Bard (studio desk)
**Mode:** Single continuous idea. One dark room, one glowing terminal, one slow build from stillness to a held final frame.

---

## Concept in one breath

A quiet bedroom at night. The trader is asleep, or the room is simply empty of him. The only light comes from a terminal screen that never stops working. The camera slowly pushes toward the glow, and the screen behaves like a watcher: alert, patient, aware of everything moving through the market while the room stays still. The emotional read is reassurance through vigilance, not hype and not promise. Nothing on screen claims a result.

---

## Shot list

### Shot 1: Hook (0.0 to 1.0 s)
- **Content:** Pure darkness. Within the first frame, a single thin band of pink light sweeps across the black room from left to right, as if a scanning line has just woken up.
- **Motion:** The scanning light is the motion. It must be visible inside the first second.
- **Camera:** Locked off on a low, slightly off-center angle, roughly at pillow height looking across the bed toward the desk. No camera movement yet.
- **Lighting:** Only the scanning band. Everything else falls to black.
- **Color:** Deep black with a hot pink edge, roughly the saturation of a neon tube, never pastel.

### Shot 2: Awakening (1.0 to 4.0 s)
- **Content:** The terminal screen comes up in stages. First a wash of soft pink, then slow abstract line movement across the glass, a dense field of moving traces that suggests constant motion without depicting anything legible.
- **Motion:** The camera begins a slow, steady dolly-in toward the desk, about 10 percent of frame width over three seconds. The screen's internal movement is continuous and unhurried.
- **Camera:** Smooth gimbal or slider move, no handheld shake, no snap zooms.
- **Lighting:** The terminal is the key light. It casts a cool pink spill across the desk edge, a chair back, and the wall. The rest of the room stays in shadow.
- **Color:** Pink glow, black falloff, a faint dusty haze in the air caught by the light beam for depth.

### Shot 3: The Watcher (4.0 to 8.0 s)
- **Content:** The hero beat. Tight framing on the screen surface. Abstract glowing traces pulse in soft waves, slowly building density as if the system is taking in more and more of the market. No readable figures, no axis labels, no recognizable chart grids. Shapes only: flowing lines, soft nodes of light, gentle ripples.
- **Motion:** The internal screen motion slowly intensifies, then settles into a steady rhythm. The camera holds the frame with a very slow drift toward the center of the glow.
- **Camera:** Tight, near-static. Ends slightly closer than where it started.
- **Lighting:** The screen remains the source. A subtle bloom softens the edges of the bright traces. Shadows stay deep so the screen reads as the only thing awake in the room.
- **Color:** Pink highlights with black surround. One accent of cooler pink-white at the brightest nodes. No other hues.

### Shot 4: Closing Beat (8.0 to 11.0 s)
- **Content:** The camera pulls back slowly from the screen, revealing the empty bed in the background, neatly made, untouched by the light. The room is calm. The watcher is still working.
- **Motion:** A slow, steady pull-back. The screen's ripples slow to a near stillness as the frame widens.
- **Camera:** Gentle dolly out, about 25 percent of frame width over three seconds, ending on a wide, centered composition.
- **Lighting:** The screen light holds across the room. The pink spill softens across the bedding and the far wall.
- **Color:** Same palette, slightly darker overall to create a settling mood.

### Shot 5: Held Frame (11.0 to 12.0 s)
- **Content:** The final frame holds. The screen glows steadily in the dark room. The composition is deliberately open, with generous negative space in the upper third and lower third of the frame so the end card can sit cleanly over it.
- **Motion:** Near stillness. A single, very slow breath of light across the screen, then nothing.
- **Camera:** Locked off. No movement.
- **Lighting:** Steady, same as the end of Shot 4.
- **Color:** Unchanged. Pink on black, no fade to white, no flash.

## Pacing

- Opening second is the only fast moment. Everything after is slow, deliberate, and even.
- Cut count is minimal: the piece should read as one continuous take, or at most three visible transitions. Prefer a smooth continuous camera move over hard cuts.
- Shot durations are roughly 1 s, 3 s, 4 s, 3 s, 1 s. The hero shot is the longest so the watcher idea has time to register.
- No rhythm cuts to music beats. The visual pulse follows the screen's own slow waves.

---

## Mood

Vigilant, calm, unhurried. The feeling is that something is watching carefully on your behalf while the room is quiet. No urgency, no fear, no wealth imagery, no money, no charts that imply gains, no people celebrating, no screens showing profit. The watcher is a companion to the trader's sleep, not a sales pitch.

---

## Sound design

- **Opening (0.0 to 1.0 s):** A single soft, deep sweep that matches the scanning band of light. Think a low electronic tone rising quickly and settling.
- **Omniscient perception tone (1.0 to 8.0 s):** A layered, slightly ethereal tone that reads as attentive awareness. It should feel like a presence that is paying attention, with subtle, slowly evolving harmonics. The tone may echo the anime-style omniscient perception sting that has worked on TikTok, but keep it original and restrained, never a recognizable copied clip.
- **Texture:** A faint room tone beneath everything: quiet air, a very soft electrical hum from the screen. No traffic, no voices, no notification pings.
- **Peak (around 6.0 to 7.5 s):** The tone swells gently with the screen's brightest pulse, then pulls back.
- **Closing (8.0 to 12.0 s):** The tone thins into a steady, low note that holds under the final frame and fades out in the last half second. No hard stop, no crescendo into the end card.
- **Mix:** Nothing above moderate loudness. No sudden hits. Dynamic range stays gentle so the piece feels calm on a phone speaker.

---

## Visual rules

- Everything on screen is abstract or atmospheric. The terminal shows motion, density, and glow only.
- No readable figures of any kind. Any numbers that might appear in the terminal must be rendered as unreadable light shapes, never as legible digits, and must never be presented as real market data.
- Any on-screen data is illustrative and must never be described as real. This direction does not depict actual prices, returns, or results.
- No brand marks, device logos, or interface branding visible anywhere in the frame.
- No faces, no hands on the keyboard, no person in the frame beyond the empty bed.
- Vertical framing keeps the screen and bed in the lower two-thirds, leaving the upper third open for later placement.

---

## Compliance notes

- This piece makes no performance claims, no profit implications, and no trading advice.
- Product claims in any companion copy must keep the standard not-advice disclaimer.
- The end card and any overlay text are handled by a separate desk and are not part of this direction.
