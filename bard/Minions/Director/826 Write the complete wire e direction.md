# Wire E: Perception Sweep (Director Direction)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds (owner ruling 10-29)
**Palette:** Near-black trading floor, pale pink perception sweep, no emblem, no crown, no raven mark
**Pacing:** Motion begins inside the first second and never fully stops. Slow, watchful, unhurried.
**Mood:** Quiet vigilance. The market is always being watched while the trader sleeps. No urgency theater.

## Shot 1: 0.0s to 3.5s, Opening Sweep

Motion starts in the first second. A single pale pink luminous band enters from frame right and crosses a near-black trading floor at a steady, low speed. The camera sits low and static at desk height, looking down a row of empty trading desks. Monitors glow faintly in the dark, showing only soft, abstract light bars with no readable marks of any kind. The sweep passes over the desk surfaces and leaves a thin trailing haze of pink light behind it.

- **Lighting:** Pale pink key light from the sweep itself, cool near-black ambient, a faint edge vignette.
- **Camera:** Locked low angle, with a very slight drift forward (under 5% push) so the motion feels alive without shaking.
- **Overlay:** Hook line appears at t0 and holds for 3 seconds (see Overlay Field below).

## Shot 2: 3.5s to 7.5s, The Sweep Travels the Floor

The sweep continues across the floor and widens into a soft arc, lighting rows of chairs and desks one after another, like a slow scan. The camera begins a gentle dolly forward down the center aisle. Chairs remain empty. No people are shown with faces. The pink light reveals outlines of equipment and the faint grid of the room, then falls back to darkness behind it.

- **Lighting:** Pink arc acts as a moving practical light source. Shadows stretch long and sharp across the floor, then collapse as the arc passes.
- **Camera:** Smooth dolly forward at a constant, unhurried speed. No handheld movement.
- **Pacing:** The arc takes about 4 seconds to cross the frame. Hold the rhythm steady.

## Shot 3: 7.5s to 11.5s, Convergence on the Wall

The sweep curves and converges toward a dark monitor wall at the far end of the floor. The screens brighten gradually, displaying only soft, abstract luminous bars that rise and fall without any legible marks, digits, or symbols. The pink light gathers in the center of the wall and pulses once, slowly, like an eye opening. The camera continues a slow push toward the wall and tightens the frame.

- **Lighting:** Central pink glow as the dominant source. The monitor bars act as secondary, cooler fill. Deep black falloff at the edges.
- **Camera:** Slow push-in, ending at a medium-tight framing of the wall. Subtle depth-of-field pull so the center is sharp and the edges soften.
- **Overlay:** Payoff line appears at t5 and holds for 4 seconds (see Overlay Field below). Destination line appears at t10.

## Shot 4: 11.5s to 15.0s, Settle and Clear Space

The pink pulse fades back into the wall and the sweep dissolves into clean, dark negative space. The camera slowly eases to a stop, leaving open dark space across the lower third of the frame for the destination line to sit in. The monitors dim to a faint glow. The final second holds near-black with a trace of pink haze at the edges.

- **Lighting:** Pink fades to near-black. Only the faint edge vignette and a thin rim of pale pink remain at the final frame.
- **Camera:** Decelerates to a full stop by about 14.0s, then holds completely still for the last second.
- **Pacing:** Settling, not ending abruptly. The last frame should feel like a watch that never stops.
