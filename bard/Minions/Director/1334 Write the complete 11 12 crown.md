- Motion: slow dolly in, figure stationary, haze moving behind.
- Light: soft diffuse blue-gray wrapping the slab.

**Shot 4 (9.0 to 12.0s): Private Ascent**
Camera rises up the face of the megastructure, passing sealed windows, revealing the full height of the building. Fog thickens at mid-height, cutting the top off the frame. Only the ember remains visible through the haze.
- Motion: vertical crane up, steady, no shake.
- Light: deeper blue-gray, haze softening edges.

**Shot 5 (12.0 to 15.0s): The Ember Holds**
Tight, slow push toward the pink ember in a single window, with the building's dark mass surrounding it. Grain increases slightly. The ember pulses once, very softly, then holds steady to the final frame.
- Motion: slow push in, minimal camera movement, one soft pulse of light.
- Light: pink ember as the sole color source, everything else cold monochrome.
