**Wire Direction B: Vertical 9:16, 12 Seconds, Three Shots**

**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 12 seconds, three shots, dark pink and black palette, cinematic and restrained.

**Shot 1, 0 to 4 seconds: The Desk**
- **Subject:** A dark desk in a quiet room. A wide monitor glows deep pink and shows a faint, slowly scrolling line chart rendered as abstract trace lines only, with no axes, no scale marks, and no readable detail.
- **Camera:** Motion begins on the first frame. A slow push-in toward the monitor, steady and unhurried.
- **Lighting:** The pink glow from the screen is the main light source, spilling softly across the desk edge and the keyboard. Everything beyond the desk falls into near black.
- **Pacing:** The room holds still. Only the chart trace drifts and the camera closes in.
- **Mood:** Watchful, patient, the market moving while the room waits.

**Shot 2, 4 to 8 seconds: The Terminal**
- **Subject:** A glowing terminal panel rendered as shapes, bars, and soft geometry with no legible characters.
- **Camera:** A lateral tracking move that follows along the length of the glowing panel at a constant, smooth speed.
- **Lighting:** One soft sweep of pink light crosses the screen from left to right, lifting the surface and then fading back into the dark. This is the only light event in the shot.
- **Pacing:** Steady and deliberate. The sweep is the single beat of the shot.
- **Mood:** Focused and precise, a live system doing its work.

**Shot 3, 8 to 12 seconds: The Empty Chair**
- **Subject:** An empty chair in a dark room, pulled back from the desk. Beyond the large glass window, city lights blur into soft bokeh.
- **Camera:** A slow pull-back that widens the frame, revealing the room and the window.
- **Lighting:** Low key. Faint pink from the monitor remains as a rim on the chair and the glass. The city lights provide the only other color, blurred and distant.
- **Pacing:** Slow to the final frame. The movement settles as it ends.
- **Mood:** The watcher is away, the market keeps running, the room stays on watch.

- Screens show abstract light and motion only. No interface chrome that could pass for legible text.
