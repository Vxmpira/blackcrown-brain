# Crown Ownership Clip: Direction

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 12 seconds, single continuous move with one soft push-in
**Claim:** Ownership only. Your context stays yours.
**Mood:** Private, calm, watchful. Dark room, one warm-pink source, nothing shouting.

## Concept

A pink filament of light leaves a phone screen and gets pulled into a sealed black sphere at the center of the frame. Nothing leaks back out. The whole clip is one idea: what goes in stays inside.

## Shot Breakdown

**0.0 to 1.0s, Hook (motion in the first second):** Close on a phone screen in a dark hand. A thin pink filament whips off the glass in a fast arc, camera tracking sideways with it. It snaps taut toward the center of frame and begins to coil.

**1.0 to 4.0s, Product as hero:** The camera settles into a slow push-in on the phone held in a dim room. The screen glows soft pink-white with blurred chat-bubble shapes and abstract glow. Pink light spills across the palm and fingers. Shallow depth of field, the screen sharp, the room falling into black.

**4.0 to 7.0s, The seal:** The filament draws out of the screen as a stream of fine pink particles, flowing inward in one direction only. They pour into a matte black sphere floating above the phone, which closes around them with a soft pulse. Camera orbits about 15 degrees around the sphere, steady, no shake.

**7.0 to 10.0s, Hold:** Camera stops. The sphere sits calm and dark, a faint pink rim light tracing its edge. Beneath it, a faint grid fades in across the floor and the edges darken into a vignette. This is the longest held frame, so the viewer can read the overlay.

**10.0 to 12.0s, Release:** Slow pull back. The hand lowers the phone out of frame, the screen dims to a single pink point, and the sphere holds its glow for the final second before fading to black.

## Lighting and Look

- Key: a single warm-pink source from the phone screen, roughly 3200K tinted toward magenta.
- Fill: almost none. Black is the base. Never flat black, always a faint pink haze in the air.
- Accent: a soft pink glow around the sphere edge, no hard highlights, no lens flare.
- Grid and vignette: faint, behind the sphere and at the frame edges.

## Pacing

- One continuous move, two cuts maximum (none is preferred).
- The hook is fast, 0 to 1s. Everything after slows down, ending in stillness.
- No music cues in the footage direction. Sound is handled separately.
