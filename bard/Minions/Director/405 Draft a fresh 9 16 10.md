**Korvus Operator B: "The Watch" (9:16, 12 seconds)**

**Concept:** The market never sleeps, and neither does the watch. A dark room, a single glowing terminal, and a gaze that sweeps the empty space while the trader rests.

**Subject:** A dark, sparsely furnished bedroom at night. A wide monitor on a desk glows with the Korvus terminal in deep pink against black. The screen shows abstract, unreadable chart structure: thin rising and falling pink bars, faint horizontal level lines, and a slow vertical scan line. No legible content.

**Scene breakdown:**

- **0.0 to 1.0s (hook):** Total darkness. Within the first second, a hard pink scan line flashes across the monitor and the screen blooms into light. The camera is already moving, starting a slow push toward the glow.
- **1.0 to 4.0s (hero reveal):** The camera settles on the monitor in a medium close framing. Pink bars rise and fall in a steady rhythm across the screen. The scan line travels left to right, then resets. The desk, chair, and edges of the room stay in shadow.
- **4.0 to 8.0s (the watch):** The camera pulls back and drifts slowly across the room. The scan line from the screen spills faint pink light onto the walls, the rumpled edge of an unmade bed, and an empty chair. The room is never fully lit. The light moves like a slow gaze that checks every corner while nobody is there to watch.
- **8.0 to 12.0s (settle):** The camera drifts back to the monitor and holds a steady, centered frame. The scan line slows, pauses on the screen's glow, and begins to dim. The pink bars hold a last quiet pulse, then the screen fades to black and the room returns to darkness.

**Camera:** Slow, continuous dolly and gentle lateral drift. No handheld shake, no whip pans, no jump cuts. One smooth move per scene. Vertical framing throughout, with the monitor placed in the upper-middle third.

**Lighting:** Practical lighting only. The monitor is the single key source, casting deep pink with a restrained bloom. Surrounding surfaces stay near black with a thin cool rim on the chair and desk edge. Light haze in the air catches the pink for depth. High contrast, low ambient.

**Motion and pacing:** Motion begins in the first second. The pace stays calm and deliberate, with rhythmic pulses from the screen as the only repeating beat. Movement slows toward the end, so the final frame feels like a held breath.

**Mood:** Quiet vigil. Confident, watchful, a little eerie, never loud or hyped. The feeling is a market under constant observation while the trader rests. No celebration, no money imagery, no urgency.

**Constraints:** The screen shows only abstract shapes and light. No readable interface details, no real price or profit visuals, no promised outcomes, no people's faces. The hero is the glowing terminal.

No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
