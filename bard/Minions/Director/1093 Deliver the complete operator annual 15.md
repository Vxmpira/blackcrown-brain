# Operator Annual: 15 Second Vertical Clip Direction

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15.0 seconds, one continuous dark space.
**Idea:** The watcher never sleeps. One terminal keeps vigil in the dark while the world is off.
**Overlay:** The server adds any overlay lines after render. The footage carries none.

## Look and Environment

- Deep black void with no horizon and no visible walls.
- Pink accent light only, soft and saturated, never neon-bright.
- A soft pink glow sits behind the monitor, slightly off center, to give the subject depth.
- A faint grid lies on the floor and fades into the black before it reaches the frame edges.
- Edge vignette on all four sides, heavier at the top and bottom.
- No images, no props, no people, no reflections of anything legible.

## Hero

- One wide terminal monitor floats in the black void at roughly eye level, slightly below center frame.
- The glass is lit pink from within, with a thin pink rim light along its edges.
- Screen content is abstract light only: rising trace lines, soft horizontal bands, and gentle pulses of brightness.
- No readable characters, no axis marks, no buttons, no icons, no cursor.

## Motion Rule

Motion begins in the first second and nothing is static for longer than about one second. Every movement is slow and continuous. No cuts, no handheld shake, no whip pans.

## Shot by Shot

**Shot 1 | 0.0 to 3.0 seconds | Arrival**
- The camera is already moving at frame one, a slow dolly in toward the monitor at about 3 percent of frame width per second.
- Within the first second, a single trace line begins rising across the screen from the lower left.
- At 0.8 seconds the pink glow behind the monitor takes one soft pulse, brightening about 15 percent and settling.
- By 3.0 seconds the monitor fills roughly 45 percent of frame width.

**Shot 2 | 3.0 to 7.0 seconds | Vigil**
- The dolly continues but eases to a near stop by 4.5 seconds.
- A gentle orbit begins at 3.0 seconds, the camera drifting about 8 degrees to the left around the monitor.
- Soft horizontal bands drift upward across the screen at a steady, unhurried pace.
- Trace lines multiply quietly, several rising at different speeds.
- The screen pulses every 1.5 seconds, each pulse slightly softer than the last.
- The floor grid comes into view as the camera lowers slightly.

**Shot 3 | 7.0 to 11.0 seconds | Convergence**
- A slow push toward the glass edge begins at 7.0 seconds, closing to about 70 percent of frame width by 11.0 seconds.
- The separate trace lines converge into one rising line near the center of the screen.
- Focus shifts gradually from the glass rim to the faint floor grid at about 9.5 seconds, then back to the glass by 11.0 seconds.
- The pink rim light strengthens slightly as the camera closes in.

**Shot 4 | 11.0 to 13.5 seconds | Pull Back**
- The camera reverses into a slow pull back, returning to the wide framing of Shot 1 by 13.5 seconds.
- The single rising line dissolves back into soft horizontal bands.
- The pink glow behind the monitor widens and brightens about 10 percent, then holds.

**Shot 5 | 13.5 to 15.0 seconds | Settle**
- The camera locks off and holds completely still for the final beat.
- The screen dims gradually to its lowest brightness, with one final faint pulse at about 14.2 seconds.
- The floor grid and the glow fade into the black over the last 0.8 seconds.
- The final frame is the monitor glow alone in deep black, with the vignette holding the edges.
