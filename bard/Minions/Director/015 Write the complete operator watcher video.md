**Director Brief: Korvus Operator, "The Watcher" (vertical 9:16, 12 seconds)**

**Concept:** One idea: the market is watched while the trader sleeps. The terminal is the only light in a dark room, and it never stops watching.

**Palette:** Near black room (#0a0a0c), deep magenta and pink glow (#d1407a to #ff6fa3) as the only saturated color, a cool grey fill at very low level. No other hues.

**Lens and framing:** 50mm equivalent, vertical 9:16, shallow depth of field, subject sharp and background falling away. Slight grain, restrained bloom on highlights. No lens flare streaks.

---

**Shot 1, 0:00 to 0:01. Hook (motion from frame one)**
- Camera: a slow push-in begins on the first frame toward a dark monitor edge.
- Lighting: a single thin band of pink light crosses the glass from left to right within the first second, as if a line of data just refreshed.
- Motion: the band moves steadily, no cut, no whip.
- Subject: the monitor bezel and the edge of a dark desk, nothing else readable.
- Palette: black with a sharp pink highlight line.
- Data: abstract glow only. Labeled Illustration.

**Shot 2, 0:01 to 0:04. The terminal as hero**
- Camera: the push-in continues and settles, framing the glowing terminal centered in the upper two thirds of the frame.
- Lighting: the screen is the key light. Pink wash falls across the desk surface and a faint edge of the trader's chair. Everything else sits in shadow.
- Motion: soft, undulating glow lines rise and fall like a slow waveform. Smooth, unhurried, no hard edges.
- Subject: the terminal, abstract. No readable values, no charts with numerals, no legible labels.
- Palette: pink glow on near black, grain visible in the shadows.
- Data: Illustration. Abstract glow and motion only.

**Shot 3, 0:04 to 0:08. Payoff, the watch**
- Camera: holds still. A very slight drift, almost imperceptible, adds life without distraction.
- Lighting: a single vertical scan line of light moves slowly across the terminal from right to left, then passes through the room, lighting the empty chair for a moment.
- Motion: the scan line is the only fast-feeling element and it stays controlled. Once it passes, a soft pulse blooms and fades, as if something was checked and found in order.
- Subject: the screen and the empty chair. The watcher is the screen, not a person.
- Palette: pink scan line, deep black shadows, a faint cool fill on the chair.
- Data: Illustration, abstract glow only.

**Shot 4, 0:08 to 0:11. The room while the trader sleeps**
- Camera: a slow pull-back, dolly out, revealing more of the dark room: a bed edge or pillow at the far edge of frame, very soft focus, nothing legible.
- Lighting: the screen remains the only source. Its glow dims by a small degree as the frame widens, then holds.
- Motion: the pull-back is the slowest move in the clip. The terminal stays lit, steady, watching.
- Subject: the room in stillness, a sense of night passing.
- Palette: black, muted magenta from the screen, faint cool shadow detail.
- Data: Illustration, the glow continues without readable detail.

**Shot 5, 0:11 to 0:12. Closing frame with end card space**
- Camera: the move ends and the frame locks off. The subject sits low and off-center with clear negative space in the upper third and across the top half, reserved for Herald's overlay and the end card.
- Lighting: the screen glow eases to a steady low pulse and holds.
- Motion: minimal. One last slow breath of glow, then stillness for the final beat.
- Subject: terminal glow, dark room, empty desk.
- Palette: near black with the pink glow held low.
- Data: Illustration, abstract, steady.

---

**Pacing:** Slow after the first second. One idea, one light source, one restrained payoff. No quick cuts, no zooms that feel like urgency. The energy comes from the scan line and the sense of time passing, not from speed.

**Mood:** Cinematic, quiet, a little lonely in a good way. The message is that the watch continues. No promised outcomes, no implied returns, no trading action on screen.

**Exclusions for the generator:** No people's faces. No keyboard keycaps, phone screens, book spines, price tags, clocks, or signage that could carry legible characters. No logos, no crown or raven marks, no profit imagery, no money, no charts with axis labels. Any screen content stays abstract glow and motion, labeled Illustration.

**Handoff:** Herald supplies the overlay lines and the end card copy, baked on at render. This direction carries no on-screen wording.
