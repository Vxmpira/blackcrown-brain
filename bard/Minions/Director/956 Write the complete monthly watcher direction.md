# Korvus Monthly Watcher: Video Direction

**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 15 seconds, single continuous shot sequence
**Desk:** Bard, video creative
**Subject:** A dark trading terminal glowing in an empty room while the market moves

## Concept

The market keeps moving while the room sleeps, and something keeps watching. The terminal is the watcher. It never blinks, never leaves the room, and never tells the viewer what to do. The traces are the market doing what markets do: moving, widening, settling. Nothing here promises a result or celebrates a gain.

## Subject and Set

- A single wide monitor on a dark desk, screen glowing cool cyan in an otherwise unlit room.
- The screen shows abstract, unlabeled line traces only. No readable interface, no panels with recognizable layout, no price scales.
- Surrounding props stay minimal: a chair pushed back, a mug going cold, a faint reflection of the screen on the desk surface.
- No people in frame. The room is empty on purpose.

## Camera

- **Opening (0 to 1 second):** Motion from the first frame. A slow push toward the screen, shallow depth of field, the traces already moving as the camera starts.
- **Middle (1 to 11 seconds):** A gentle lateral drift across the monitor face. The camera stays steady and unhurried. Handheld feel is not wanted.
- **Final stretch (11 to 15 seconds):** A slow fade-in of the camera's move toward the monitor, easing to a stop. The frame settles.

## Beat Sheet

**Beat 1, Hook (0 to 1 second):** Motion in the first second. Cyan traces are already climbing across the screen as the camera begins its slow push. Soft haze drifts through the light beam from the monitor.

**Beat 2, Movement (1 to 8 seconds):** The traces widen as the market moves. Lines branch and spread across the screen in fluid, layered motion. Crown gold accent light catches the edges of the desk and the rim of the mug. Depth of field keeps the foreground soft and the screen sharp.

**Beat 3, Watch (8 to 11 seconds):** The motion holds steady. The room stays still while the screen keeps working. A faint shift in the haze suggests time passing.

**Beat 4, Settle (11 to 15 seconds):** The camera fades toward the monitor and stops. The traces gradually resolve into one steady rising arc across the screen. The arc holds on the final frame.

## Lighting and Palette

- **Base:** Near black. The room is never fully lit.
- **Primary accent:** Electric cyan from the screen, the main light source.
- **Secondary accent:** Crown gold, used sparingly on desk edges and surface highlights. Gold appears as a warm rim, never as a shape or emblem.
- **Atmosphere:** Soft haze in the light beam. Gentle bloom on the brightest trace points.
- **Contrast:** High, but never crushed. Shadows keep some texture.

## Pacing and Mood

- Slow and deliberate throughout. No quick cuts, no sudden motion bursts after the opening second.
- Mood is calm, watchful, and a little lonely in a good way. The viewer should feel the room keeping watch.
- One idea only: the market keeps moving, and something keeps watching.

## Hard Rules

- No raven, no crown, no logo, no emblem, and no brand mark of any kind anywhere in the frame. This includes faint edge silhouettes, reflections, and decorative shapes in the haze or on the desk.
- No money imagery: no currency symbols, no coin stacks, no green gain celebrations, no fast upward spikes framed as wins.
- No readable screens, charts with labels, or interface text.
- Overlay lines are added later by the server through the forge, not generated. Leave clean space in the upper third of the frame for those overlays to sit.

- Critic gate: confirm the final frame contains no brand mark, no typography, and no implied profit before filing.
