**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 12 seconds, single continuous idea

## Concept

A single thread of pink light is your context. It starts loose in the dark, gets drawn into a private chamber, and is sealed by a ring of light that closes around it. Nothing else enters the frame. The thread stays whole and stays inside.

## Palette and Light

Black base with deep pink rim light. Key light is low and pink from the left, with black fill on the right. Faint haze catches the light. No bright whites, no gold, no currency tones, no charts.

## Shot List

**1. 0 to 2s: Opening motion.** Macro shot of a dark surface. Camera pushes in fast in the first second as a single pink filament lifts off the black and begins to coil. Shallow depth of field, the filament sharp, the background dissolving into black.

**2. 2 to 5s: Into the chamber.** Dolly right, slow and steady. The filament threads through a dark glass lattice, a translucent private structure with soft pink edge glow. Light passes through the lattice and fractures into thin lines on the glass. The filament stays one unbroken line.

**3. 5 to 8s: The seal.** Camera orbits about 30 degrees around the chamber at low angle. A ring of pink light forms around the filament and closes like a lock, a soft pulse rather than a snap. Behind frosted glass, a faint raven silhouette crosses slowly and leaves. No claws, no aggression.

**4. 8 to 11s: Held and owned.** Slow pull back. The filament rests centered inside the sealed ring, glowing steadily. A faint crown-shaped halo settles above it, barely visible. Negative space around the chamber stays empty. No other lines, particles, or figures reach it.

**5. 11 to 12s: Hold.** Camera stops. The ring dims to a steady breathing pulse and one ember of pink stays lit at the center. Cut to black on the final beat.

## Mood and Pacing

Calm, sovereign, private. Operator to operator, not a sales pitch. Motion is deliberate with one fast opening push, then everything slows. Cuts are minimal, ideally one continuous move with a single dissolve at the end. The viewer should feel the thread is safe, not that anything is being sold.

## Avoid

Other AI logos or interfaces, screens with interface elements, competitor imagery, money or profit visuals, dramatic storm or combat energy, any framing that implies other systems lose or forget data.

No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
