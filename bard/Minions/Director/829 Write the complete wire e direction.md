## SHOT 1: The sweep wakes the floor
**Time:** 0.0 to 3.0 (3.0 seconds)
**Overlay cue:** Hook line "The tape never sleeps" lands at 0.0 and holds through 3.0.

**Framing:** Medium close on an empty trading desk. Three dark monitor edges sit out of focus at the frame's lower third. Negative space fills the upper two thirds.

**Subject:** A single pale pink perception sweep, a soft band of light, crosses the dark floor from left to right like a slow searchlight passing over a sleeping room. The band carries no shapes or marks of any kind, only a smooth luminous gradient with a faint haze.

**Camera:** Slow lateral dolly right, about 3 percent of frame width per second. The motion is already present on frame one, so the viewer never sees a still opening.

**Lighting:** Only the sweep and the faint blue-white bleed from off-screen monitors. The rest of the floor stays near black. The sweep brightens as it passes the center of frame and dims as it leaves.

**Pacing:** Unhurried. The sweep takes the full three seconds to cross the middle of the frame.

**Mood:** The room is awake and watching, but nobody is in it.
