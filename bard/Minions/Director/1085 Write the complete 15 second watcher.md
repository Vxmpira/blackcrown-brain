# Korvus Watcher: 15 Second Shot List

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds, 6 shots
**Concept:** Korvus as an all seeing watcher. The market is observed 24/7 while the trader sleeps.
**Palette:** Dark pink and black. Soft pink rim light, glowing terminal glass, a faint grid, an edge vignette.
**Data:** Every chart and readout in the footage is abstract glow. Any price line is Illustration only and shows no real market data.

---

**SHOT 1 | 0:00 to 0:01 | Open in motion**
- **Subject:** Edge of a black glass monitor on a dark desk, dust motes drifting in the air.
- **Camera:** Slow dolly push toward the glass, already moving on frame one.
- **Lighting:** A single pink light sweep crosses the glass from left to right.
- **Pacing:** Fast entry, the sweep resolves inside the first second.
- **Mood:** Quiet, something is awake in the room.
- **Overlay:** None.

**SHOT 2 | 0:01 to 0:04 | Product UI as hero**
- **Subject:** A glowing terminal fills the frame. Pink line traces rise and fall across a dark grid. A thin scanning beam moves slowly from top to bottom. The UI uses no readable digits or words, only shapes and light.
- **Camera:** Slow push in on the screen, steady and unhurried.
- **Lighting:** The terminal is the only light source. Its glow bleeds onto the black surround.
- **Pacing:** Calm, the scan line sets the rhythm.
- **Mood:** Attentive, precise, always on.
- **Overlay (hook, appears at 0:02 and holds to 0:04):** `Someone is still watching.`

**SHOT 3 | 0:04 to 0:07 | The sleeping trader**
- **Subject:** Overhead view of a bed in a dark room. A figure sleeps. A phone sized screen on the nightstand throws a slow pink pulse onto the ceiling, like a heartbeat.
- **Camera:** Slow crane down from the ceiling toward the bed, stopping at a three quarter angle.
- **Lighting:** Only the nightstand pulse and the faint pink wash it casts on the walls.
- **Pacing:** Slow, almost still. The pulse is the only motion besides the crane.
- **Mood:** Trust. The trader can rest because something else is keeping watch.
- **Overlay:** None.

**SHOT 4 | 0:07 to 0:10 | The watcher's eye**
- **Subject:** A large lens iris made of concentric pink light rings opens slowly in total darkness. Thin pink lines spread outward from its center across a black grid, like a network of watch paths. One node brightens and pulses once.
- **Camera:** Slow orbit around the iris, keeping it centered.
- **Lighting:** All light comes from the iris and its traces. Edge vignette deepens as the orbit goes on.
- **Pacing:** Builds gradually. The single node pulse lands near the end of the shot, on the beat.
- **Mood:** Omniscient but calm. No alarm, no urgency.
- **Overlay:** None.

**SHOT 5 | 0:10 to 0:13 | The morning handoff**
- **Subject:** The terminal on the nightstand brightens softly as a hand reaches for it. The screen glow stays steady and warm, showing the watch is still running.
- **Camera:** Tight, smooth handheld on the hand and the screen, gentle drift to the right.
- **Lighting:** Pink glow grows slightly brighter across the skin of the hand and the sheets.
- **Pacing:** Steady, unhurried, a little slower than shot 2.
- **Mood:** Relief and continuity. The market was watched, and the trader is informed.
- **Overlay:** None.

**SHOT 6 | 0:13 to 0:15 | Closing hold**
- **Subject:** The terminal glow dims to one pink point held on black. The iris from shot 4 fades in faintly behind it and then settles.
- **Camera:** Locked off, no movement. The frame holds still for the close.
- **Lighting:** One pink point on black, edge vignette at full strength.
- **Pacing:** The shot holds through the last two seconds with nothing added.
- **Mood:** Settled, confident, honest.
- **Overlay (appears at 0:13, holds to 0:15):**
  - Line 1: `Korvus watches the market while you sleep.`
  - Line 2: `korvus.industries`
  - Line 3 (disclaimer, smaller type, bottom of frame): `Market intelligence, not financial advice.`
