# Korvus Monthly Watcher: Shot Direction

**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 14 seconds
**Series:** Korvus monthly watcher
**Filed by:** Director, for Bard
**Rung moved:** First paying customer (Korvus Operator), through brand recall of the 24/7 watch

## Concept

A dark trading terminal glows in an empty room while the market keeps moving. Nobody is at the desk. The screen does the watching. The idea is one line: the market does not stop when you do. Korvus keeps the watch.

## Subject

- A single dark desk in an empty room, lit only by the terminal.
- The terminal shows an abstract chart: candles and price lines drawn in soft pink light on black. No axis labels, no readable marks, no glyphs of any kind.
- A thin pink horizon of light across the desk surface.
- Nothing else in frame: no people, no mascots, no props with shapes that read as a logo.

**Absolute rule:** no brand mark of any kind. No raven, no bird, no crown, no silhouette that reads as a logo or emblem. Chart shapes must stay generic candles and lines, never arranged into a symbol.

## Shot Sequence

**0.0 to 2.0 seconds: Motion open.** Extreme close on the chart as a fresh candle forms on the right edge and the price line rises through the frame. The motion starts on frame one. Slow push in on the glowing glass.

**2.0 to 5.0 seconds: The room.** Camera eases back and tilts slightly down, revealing the empty chair, the dark desk, and the room falling away into black. The terminal is the only light source. Candles keep forming in the background of the glass.

**5.0 to 9.0 seconds: The watch.** Slow lateral dolly across the desk, parallax between the glowing screen and the dark room. A pulse of pink light travels along the price line, showing activity without any readable number. The camera drifts past the empty chair.

**9.0 to 12.0 seconds: The turn.** Camera settles into a locked, quiet frame on the screen. Candles continue forming in an unbroken rhythm. One soft flicker in the room's far corner, then stillness.

**12.0 to 14.0 seconds: Hold.** Static frame. The chart keeps moving on the glass. The room stays dark. Hold to the end.

## Camera

- Single continuous take conceptually, one flowing sequence, no hard cuts.
- Slow push in, then eased pull back, then slow lateral dolly, then locked frame.
- Smooth gimbal feel. No handheld shake, no whip pans, no zooms.
- Shallow depth of field on the screen; the room softens into black at the edges.

## Lighting

- Single practical source: the terminal glow.
- Deep pink light as the key on the desk surface and the chair edge.
- Cool hint of blue-pink in the highlights of the glass, nothing warmer than pink.
- Edge vignette, falloff to near black at the frame corners.

## Palette

- Base: near black, never flat. Faint pink haze in the room air.
- Accent: dark pink, rose, and a soft magenta glow on the chart.
- Chart candles: pink for rising, deep muted rose for falling. No green, no red, no bright white.
- No gold, no silver, no metallic highlights.

## Pacing

- Slow and deliberate after the first second. The motion open is the only fast-feeling beat.
- Candle formation sets a steady rhythm of roughly one new candle every 1.5 to 2 seconds, consistent through the clip.
- The room shots breathe. Let each move settle before the next.
- The last two seconds are still. Do not add a final flourish.

## Mood

Quiet, watchful, calm confidence. Lonely in the good sense: the trader is away and the watch continues. No urgency, no hype, no money imagery, no fast cash energy. The feeling is an honest tool doing honest work in an empty room.

## Audio

- Low ambient room tone with a faint electrical hum from the screen.
- A soft, original pulse under the candle rhythm, no licensed or recognizable tracks.
- No voiceover in the footage.

## Closing Beat (verbatim)

Closing beat, quoted verbatim: **"The screen keeps watching. The room stays dark."**

Staged as a visual beat, not on-screen text: the final two seconds hold on the glowing chart in the empty room with the candle rhythm still running, and the pulse under the audio fades out with the light.

## Negative Constraints

- No people, no hands, no faces.
- No brand mark of any kind: no raven, bird, crown, emblem, or logo-like silhouette.
- No readable chart labels, prices, dates, or symbols.
- No profit imagery: no cash, no coins, no rising money stacks, no gains displays.
- No trading advice implied through arrows, buy or sell cues, or target lines.
- No stock footage of real trading floors.

No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
