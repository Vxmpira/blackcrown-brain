**Director Brief: Monthly Watcher, 9:16, 15 seconds**
*Korvus Operator, monthly plan. Feeling: the market never sleeps, and something is always watching it for you.*

**Concept**
One idea: a lone watcher keeps vigil over a city of light while the trader sleeps. The city is abstract market architecture, not a real exchange, not a chart with a visible price, and nothing suggests a gain or a loss. The watcher is the calm, all seeing presence of Korvus. The message is attention, not promises.

**Hook, 0 to 1s**
Motion from the first frame. A slow vertical push descends through black fog into a dark pink haze, revealing a single crown-shaped silhouette of light cutting across the frame. A thin pulse of pink light travels once along the silhouette's edge and stops. No cuts, no text, no faces.

**Palette**
- Base: near black, slightly cool (#07060A to #0E0A12)
- Primary accent: deep dark pink (#B3176B) for glows, rim light, and the watcher's outline
- Highlight: soft rose (#FF6FA8) used sparingly, only on the pulse and the closing light
- Haze: desaturated violet-black for depth, never a flat background
- Forbidden: greens, reds, gold coins, cash, rising arrows, or any color that reads as money

**Shot List**

*Shot 1, 0 to 3s: The Watch Begins*
- Subject: a tall, faceless watcher silhouette standing on a high ledge above a dark city of softly glowing towers.
- Camera: slow vertical push-in from ground level toward the watcher's back, 1 to 2 degrees of drift per second.
- Lighting: single rim light in dark pink along the shoulders, fog catching the light.
- Detail: thin lines of light connect the towers like a quiet network, pulsing faintly.

*Shot 2, 3 to 6s: The City Stays Awake*
- Subject: a wide view of the city at night, streets and towers pulsing in slow rhythm, as if breathing.
- Camera: slow lateral dolly left to right, low angle, shallow focus drifting from foreground fog to far towers.
- Lighting: soft pink bloom from the lines of light, deep shadow between towers.
- Detail: a few towers brighten and dim in sequence, suggesting continuous activity without any readable data.

*Shot 3, 6 to 9s: Eyes Across the Night*

- Camera: slow orbit around the silhouette, about 30 degrees over the shot, with a gentle push in.

- Detail: a single pink pulse travels from the crown down through the city lines, the same pulse as the hook.

*Shot 4, 9 to 12s: The Trader Sleeps, the Watch Continues*

- Lighting: low, warm-neutral fill inside the room kept very dark, pink from outside.

*Shot 5, 12 to 15s: Closing Light*

- Camera: slow final push, settling into a hold on the crown silhouette as the frame reaches its last beat.

- Detail: fog drifts across the frame and the city blurs into a clean dark field with a faint pink glow at the bottom edge, leaving negative space for the house overlay.

- Shots 1 to 3 move at a steady, deliberate speed. No quick cuts.
- Shot 4 slows noticeably, the only moment of stillness, to carry the "while you sleep" feeling.
- Shot 5 settles. Motion decays to near stillness by the final frame.
- Pulses are the only rhythmic elements: one at 0 to 1s, one around 6 to 7s, one at 13s.
- Audio, if used: low ambient hum with a soft single tone on each pulse. No music with lyrics, no voice.
