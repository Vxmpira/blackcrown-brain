# Korvus Watcher, Monthly Direction

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds, four shots, no cut shorter than 2 seconds. Cuts land at 0:00, 0:03.75, 0:07.5, and 0:11.25.
**Concept:** The all-seeing watcher. While the trader sleeps, something keeps watch over the market. Korvus never blinks.
**Brand fit:** Dark pink and black BlackCrown world. The raven and the crown are the marks. No guru theater, no person speaking to camera.

## Mood and Look

- Near-black base with a restrained dark pink glow (deep magenta, not neon pink).
- Soft accent glows and a faint grid texture in the air, an edge vignette, never flat black.
- Calm, watchful, slightly ominous in a precise way. Nothing frantic.
- No money imagery of any kind: no coins, no cash, no stacks, no rising green arrows, no celebrating faces. Market context is conveyed only through abstract light, never through readable data or implied returns.
- Monitors in frame show soft abstract light traces and glow only. No legible charts, no numbers, no interface text.

## Pacing

- Shot 1 opens with motion inside the first second.
- Overall tempo is slow and deliberate, with each shot holding its own movement rather than relying on fast cuts.
- Every shot has one idea and one camera move.

## Shot List

**Shot 1 (0:00 to 0:03.75): The Perch**
- Subject: a single raven on a dark iron ledge high above a sleeping city at night, its silhouette cut against a pink horizon glow.
- Motion in the first second: the raven's head turns slowly toward the lens as its wings settle.
- Camera: slow vertical push-in from the wide silhouette toward the bird's head, shallow depth of field, city lights out of focus below.
- Lighting: low key. Pink rim light on the raven's edge, deep black shadow everywhere else.
- Mood: the watch begins.

**Shot 2 (0:03.75 to 0:07.5): The Empty Desk**
- Subject: a dark trading desk in an unlit room. The chair is empty, pushed back slightly. Two monitors glow with soft, abstract pink light traces that drift without forming any readable content.
- Motion in frame: the light traces slowly flow across the screens. A faint dust mote drifts through a beam of pink light.
- Camera: slow lateral dolly across the desk, stopping on the empty chair.
- Lighting: monitor glow as the only key source. Grid texture faintly visible in the haze.
- Mood: the trader is asleep, and the desk is still being watched.

**Shot 3 (0:07.5 to 0:11.25): The Sleepless City**
- Subject: an aerial view of a city grid at night, streets threaded with soft pink light, the market's pulse rendered as slow glowing currents across the map.
- Motion in frame: the camera descends gradually, and the light currents pulse softly along the streets.
- Camera: steady, slow downward drift with a subtle push. No whip, no fast movement.
- Lighting: deep navy-black base with pink accent glow along the road lines.
- Mood: the world sleeps, and the watch does not.

**Shot 4 (0:11.25 to 0:15.00): The Watcher's Eye**
- Subject: an extreme close-up of the raven's eye, reflecting the pink glow of the city below. As the camera pulls back, the reflection resolves into the negative space of a crown silhouette.
- Motion in frame: a slow pull back from the eye, the crown shape forming in the reflected light, then settling into stillness.
- Camera: slow pull-back, ending on a held, quiet frame.
- Lighting: single pink source reflected in the eye, everything else black.
- Mood: vigilant, composed, the watch held.
