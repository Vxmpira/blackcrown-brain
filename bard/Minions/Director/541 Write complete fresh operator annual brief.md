## Shot Direction

### Shot 1: The Watch (0 to 5 seconds)
- **Motion in the first second:** The glowing terminal's abstract price ladder is already scrolling as the frame opens. The camera begins a slow push-in on the screen from the first frame.
- **Subject:** A dark trading desk at night. One empty chair sits in shadow. The terminal is the only light source and the hero of the frame.
- **Camera:** Slow dolly-in, shallow depth of field, vertical framing. Steady and unhurried.
- **Lighting:** A single pink key light from the screen spills across the desk edge. Everything else falls to black. A thin rim light traces the chair back.
- **Pacing:** Still and watchful. Let the stillness carry the first beat.
- **Mood:** Quiet vigilance. The screen is the eye that never closes.

### Shot 2: The Wire (5 to 10 seconds)
- **Motion in the first second:** A column of news panels refreshes on the terminal the moment the shot cuts in.
- **Subject:** Over-shoulder view of the terminal. Stacked panels of abstract glowing bars and tiles update one after another, with soft pink highlights pulsing as each new item lands.
- **Camera:** Slow lateral slide across the monitor, following the rising panels.
- **Lighting:** Pink glow from the screen with deep black falloff. Faint cool edge light on the desk surface.
- **Pacing:** Slightly quicker than shot 1. The information flow picks up, then holds.
- **Mood:** Attentive and precise. Calm competence, no urgency theater.

### Shot 3: The Hero Frame (10 to 15 seconds)
- **Motion in the first second:** The screen dims slightly as the camera begins a slow pull-back, revealing the desk.
- **Subject:** The terminal centered in frame, its glow the single focal point. The empty chair remains in shadow.
- **Camera:** Slow pull-back from tight on the screen to a wider desk composition. Settle into a held final frame for the last two seconds.
- **Lighting:** The pink glow dominates. Black negative space surrounds the screen.
- **Pacing:** Settles into stillness. The final hold gives room for the overlay and voiceover to land.
- **Mood:** Confident and understated. The watcher is still on duty.
