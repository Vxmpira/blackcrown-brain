## HOOK MOTION (0.0 to 1.0 seconds)

The frame opens on near total black. Within the first second, a thin hot pink line sweeps horizontally across the lower third of the screen, as if a monitor has just woken in a dark room. ## SHOT LIST

**Shot 1, 0.0 to 2.5 seconds: Wake**
- Hook motion as described above, resolving into a dark bedroom. Only the monitor provides light.
- Foreground: a rumpled pillow and the edge of a sleeping silhouette, out of focus, no face shown.
- Camera: slow push in on the monitor, 2 percent per second, steady.
- Screen content: a dark terminal layout of stacked abstract panels in pink and black. Panels contain glowing bars, a few thin level lines, and soft gradient wells. No readable text, no numbers, no icons that resemble logos.

**Shot 2, 2.5 to 5.0 seconds: The watch**
- Macro close on the monitor surface. Pink bars rise and fall in a steady rhythm, as if the market is moving under the glass.
- A thin horizontal level line brightens when a bar touches it, then dims. This is the watcher noticing.
- Camera: slow lateral drift left to right, 3 percent per second.
- Light: pink key from the screen, deep black falloff, faint haze in the air catching the glow.

**Shot 3, 5.0 to 8.5 seconds: Payoff**
- Wider frame of the room. The terminal dominates the composition. A second panel slides into view at the edge of the screen, showing a column of indicator lines drawn in pink, reading as the LodeStone layer stacked above the chart panels.
- Camera: slow dolly in, ending on the terminal.
- Pacing: the rhythm of bars quickens slightly, then settles, so the shot feels alive without feeling frantic.

**Shot 4, 8.5 to 12.0 seconds: Destination**
- Slow pull back from the monitor. The room returns to darkness, the screen glow narrows to a pink edge on the desk and the pillow.
- The screen content dims to a near black field with a soft pink gradient at the center, leaving calm negative space in the middle third for the destination overlay.
- Camera: slow pull back, decelerating into a stop at the final frame.
- Final frame holds for the last 1 second so the destination reads.
