# Crown Night Shift: Creative Brief

**Desk:** Bard (Director drafts, Bard files)
**Product:** Crown, BlackCrown Intelligence, the private AI workspace
**Rung moved:** First paying customer. The brief pushes viewers toward the free tier (10,000 tokens, no card) and, for those who want more, Crowned at 49.99 a month with 5 million tokens.

## Concept

The workspace keeps working while the city sleeps. One idea only: Crown answers in the dark. The product interface is the hero. It appears as a calm, glowing workspace, not a gadget or a sci-fi prop.

## Format

- Vertical 9:16
- 10 to 15 seconds total
- Three shots, one continuous idea, no cuts that break the flow

## Look and Mood

- Near black room, matte surfaces, a single soft pink glow from the screen edge
- Soft bloom, shallow depth of field, no lens flare
- Quiet, focused, after-hours confidence. No hype, no urgency, no guru energy.
- No people, no faces, no hands, no animals, no birds, no mascots.

## Shots

**Shot 1 (0 to 4 seconds):** Dark interface panel fills the frame, pink glow rising in the first second as a prompt field lights and the camera drifts in slowly toward the cursor.

**Shot 2 (4 to 8 seconds):** A long answer streams down the panel as thin pink light traces each line. The camera slides sideways in one smooth move, focus shifting across the workspace.

**Shot 3 (8 to 12 seconds):** The camera pulls back and tilts up over a black desk, screen glow spilling onto matte surfaces. The panel dims to a steady ember pink while city light blurs beyond the glass.

## Pacing

Motion starts in the first second. Slow, even build with ease in and ease out on every move. Each shot holds about 4 seconds. The final 1.5 seconds hold still on the dimmed glow so the clip ends calmly. Optional: a low, slow pulse underneath, never a beat drop.

## Honesty Notes

- Any interface on screen is an illustrative mock, not live account data. Mark it Illustration in the render package.
- Do not show token counts, prices, or plan names in the footage. Anything readable would break the no text law. Any words the video carries come only through the creative's overlay field, handled by Bard's forge.
- Never imply unlimited usage. Never imply financial results or trading outcomes.
