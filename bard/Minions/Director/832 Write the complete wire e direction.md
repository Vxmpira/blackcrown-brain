## Shot List

### Shot 1: Opening Sweep (0.0 to 3.0)
- **Subject:** Empty trading floor. Rows of desks, dark monitors, a faint haze of floor dust.
- **Motion from frame 1:** The pale pink perception sweep enters from the left edge as a thin, luminous horizontal band and crosses the frame over about 2.5 seconds. Camera begins a slow dolly-in from a low angle, roughly 3 percent push across the full shot.
- **Lighting:** Near-black ambient. The sweep is the only significant light source, pale pink (warm-cool balanced, not neon), with soft falloff on the desk edges.
- **Screens:** Monitors show abstract candlestick silhouettes and wave shapes only. No axis labels, no numbers, no legible glyphs. Screens are soft-focus. Illustration.
- **Lens:** 35mm equivalent, shallow depth of field, focus on the nearest desk edge.
- **Overlay:** Hook line 0.0 to 3.0.

### Shot 2: Sweep Across the Rows (3.0 to 6.0)
- **Subject:** The sweep reaches the monitor row mid-frame. Each monitor catches the light in sequence, left to right, like a line of attention waking up.
- **Motion:** Continuous. Sweep speed holds steady. Camera pulls a small lateral track, about 4 percent, parallel to the monitor row, so the sweep appears to pace the camera.
- **Lighting:** Pink rim light on monitor bezels. Desk surfaces stay in deep shadow. Keep the black floor black.
- **Focus pull:** Rack focus from floor dust motes in the foreground (0.5 seconds into the shot) to the center monitor.
- **Overlay:** Payoff line begins at 5.0 (this shot, second 2 of the shot).

### Shot 3: The Watched Monitor (6.0 to 9.0)
- **Subject:** A single central monitor, dark except for a pale candlestick silhouette. The silhouette has no labels. Pink edge light traces the top of each bar.
- **Motion:** Slow orbit of about 12 degrees around the monitor, camera drifting clockwise. A second, narrower sweep passes across the screen face from right to left at about 7.0.
- **Lighting:** Monitor is the key light. Pink spill on the nearest desk edge. Background falls to near-black.
- **Mood:** This is the quiet center of the piece. Hold the orbit steady, no sudden moves.
- **Overlay:** Payoff line continues through 9.0.

### Shot 4: The Watcher Pulls Back (9.0 to 12.0)
- **Subject:** The room, now almost empty in the frame. The sweep reverses direction and travels top to bottom, a vertical band, like a gaze moving down the screen wall.
- **Motion:** Camera pulls back, a dolly-out of about 10 percent over three seconds. The vertical sweep moves at a constant pace through the whole shot.
- **Lighting:** Ambient drops further. The sweep becomes the dominant light, still pale pink, slightly dimmer than Shot 1 to build toward the close.
- **Screens:** Screens dim one by one as the sweep passes, then go dark. No numbers, no text.
- **Overlay:** Destination line begins at 12.0.

### Shot 5: Convergence and Hold (12.0 to 15.0)
- **Subject:** The sweep narrows into a single pale pink vertical line at frame center, over a near-black field.
- **Motion through 15.0:** Motion does not stop. The line holds position but a very slow push-in continues, about 2 percent over three seconds, and the line keeps a faint shimmer. No freeze frame at the end, no fade to black before 15.0.
- **Lighting:** Only the line emits light. Edge vignette keeps the frame dark. Faint floor texture is visible at the bottom of frame, low contrast.
- **Overlay:** Destination line holds 12.0 to 15.0, placed in the lower safe zone.

- Five shots, no hard cuts within the clip. Shots 1 to 4 are continuous moves; Shot 5 is a continuation of Shot 4's motion.

- Grade: near-black shadows, pale pink highlights, low saturation outside the sweep. Film grain light, 2 percent.
