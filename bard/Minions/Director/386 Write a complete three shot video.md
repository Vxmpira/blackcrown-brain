**Korvus Operator, Variant B: The Watcher**
Vertical 9:16, 12 seconds, three shots. Dark pink and black palette, deep shadows, one hard rim of magenta light.

**Shot 1: The Hook (0.0s to 3.5s)**
- **Motion from the first frame:** A single pink light streak sweeps left to right across black at 0.0s, already moving. The motion never stops.
- **Camera:** Slow push-in on a tight close-up of a dark eye-level surface, shot on a long lens so the background compresses into a soft wall of dark. Slight handheld drift, nearly imperceptible.
- **Lighting:** Near-total darkness. Only the moving pink streak and a faint cool glow spilling from the lower edge of frame.
- **Subject:** An unlit, darkened room. In the foreground, the edge of a desk and the silhouette of a raven-shaped shadow cast on a wall, held still.
- **Motion:** The pink streak travels across the wall and the shadow, then dissolves into a sheet of abstract glowing lines that ripple outward, like a surveillance sweep activating in the dark.
- **Mood:** Watchful, patient, a little ominous. The sense that something is awake while everything else sleeps.

**Shot 2: The Payoff, Terminal as Hero (3.5s to 8.5s)**
- **Camera:** Slow dolly forward toward a large glowing terminal screen, locked off on a gentle arc of about 15 degrees. The arc reveals the room around the screen as the camera moves.
- **Lighting:** The screen is the dominant source. Soft pink and cool blue glow wraps the desk, the trader's empty chair, and the edges of a dark ceiling. Key light is the screen; fill is almost zero.
- **Subject:** The glowing terminal, shown as abstract luminous panels: flowing line charts rendered as soft light streaks, blurred rectangular panels pulsing gently, a slow rhythmic wave of pink and white rising through the frame. Nothing resolves into legible detail; every element stays a glow or a shape.
- **Motion:** Light waves move steadily upward through the panels, one continuous rhythm, as if the market is being watched in real time. Faint particles of light drift in the air around the screen.
- **Pacing:** Steady and unhurried. One slow breath across the five seconds.
- **Mood:** Calm, controlled, vigilant. The screen is working while the room is still. Honest and quiet, not theatrical.

**Shot 3: The Destination Beat (8.5s to 12.0s)**
- **Camera:** Slow pull back from the screen into a clean, dark frame. The camera settles at a centered, static composition.
- **Lighting:** The screen glow fades to a low, steady pink ember at the lower third. Upper two-thirds of the frame stay deep black, open and uncluttered.
- **Subject:** Empty negative space. The dim glow of the terminal sits below center. No figures, no props, no visible detail beyond a soft gradient.
- **Motion:** The last light waves drift down and fade out. The frame holds still for the final second so an end card can sit cleanly over the black area.
- **Layout note:** Keep the upper two-thirds of the frame free of strong detail, so the overlay text can be placed without competing with the image.
- **Mood:** Settled, confident, quiet. The watch continues after the frame goes dark.

**Direction notes**
- One idea throughout: the market is watched while the trader sleeps.
- No profit imagery, no promised outcomes, no implied trading advice. The footage shows a watch, not a result.
- Screens are abstract only: glowing charts, light streaks, blurred panels.
- No prices, no product names rendered on screen, no logos in the footage.

No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
