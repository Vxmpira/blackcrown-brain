# Wire E: Video Direction

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15.0 seconds
**Concept:** A pale pink perception sweep crosses a near-black trading floor and finds nothing asleep.
**Mood:** Watchful, calm, relentless. Quiet confidence, no hype.
**Subject rule:** No emblem, no crown, no raven. The sweep is the only hero.

## Palette and Light
- Background: near-black, cool undertone, never flat. Faint edge vignette.
- Sweep: pale pink key light, soft-edged, with a slight bloom. It should read as perception, not neon.
- Monitors: glowing blank panes in pale pink and deep blue-black. No legible content anywhere.
- Haze: thin volumetric dust catching the sweep.

## Camera and Lens
- Lens feel: 35mm, mild anamorphic streak on the sweep only.
- Shallow depth of field. Foreground desks soft, sweep sharp.
- Low, slightly off-center camera height. Movement is slow and deliberate, except where the sweep itself moves fast.
- Grain: light, filmic.

## Shot List

**Shot 1, 0.0 to 3.0 seconds: Sweep Opens**
Motion begins on frame one. A pale pink perception sweep travels left to right across an empty trading floor, lighting rows of dark desks in sequence. Camera dollies in slowly at low height. The sweep is the first thing the eye catches.
*Overlay: hook line runs 0 to 3 (server-baked).*

**Shot 2, 3.0 to 6.0 seconds: Sweep Crosses the Bank**
The sweep moves across a wall of blank glowing monitors. Each screen takes the pink wash and holds it for a beat, showing no content. Camera tracks laterally, matching the sweep's speed. A cut lands on the sweep's leading edge at 3.0.
*Overlay: payoff begins at 5.0 (server-baked).*

**Shot 3, 6.0 to 9.0 seconds: Haze Catches the Light**
Macro view. The sweep passes through floating dust and haze, each particle lit for an instant. Camera pushes in very slowly. The rhythm of the sweep pulses gently, like a watch that never stops.
*Overlay: payoff runs through 9.0 (server-baked).*

**Shot 4, 9.0 to 12.0 seconds: The Line Resolves**
The sweep narrows into a single pale pink line tracing across the floor's far edge. It holds for one second, then the camera pulls back to a wide frame: the whole floor is visible, lit and watching, no people present.
*Overlay: destination line runs 10.0 to 13.0 (server-baked).*

**Shot 5, 12.0 to 15.0 seconds: Sweep Exits, Haze Keeps Moving**
The line sweeps off frame right by 14.0. For the final second the haze keeps drifting slowly through the remaining pink light, so the frame never goes static. Vignette deepens at the edges through 15.0.
*No overlay in this shot beyond the destination line already running.*

## Pacing Notes
- Cuts land on the sweep's position at 3.0 and 9.0 only. Between cuts, motion is continuous.
- No dead tail: the final second carries drifting haze and a moving light.
- No cut to black before 15.0.

## Honesty Rules
- No profit imagery, no cash, no rising charts, no money of any kind.
- No screens showing readable data. Monitors are light only.
- Nothing implies a guaranteed outcome. The footage shows watching, not winning.
