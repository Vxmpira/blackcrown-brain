# Watcher: 7-Shot Direction

**Clip:** 15 seconds, 9:16 vertical, 24 fps
**Concept:** The all seeing watcher. The market never sleeps, and something keeps watch while the trader does.
**Mood:** Quiet, vigilant, honest. Calm confidence, not drama. No money imagery, no rising arrows, no celebration.
**Palette:** Near black, deep pink accent light, cool blue-white only where the watcher's data light touches.
**Pacing:** Slow and deliberate overall, with the cut rhythm tightening slightly in the middle shots, then settling back to stillness for the close.

---

### Shot 1: The City Never Shuts Its Eyes (0:00 to 0:02, 48 frames)

**Subject:** A sleeping city seen from high above at night, its grid of lights thinned out by the hour.

**Motion (first second):** The camera is already moving from frame one. A slow vertical descent of about 3 percent over the shot, drifting downward toward the streets. No warm-up. The first frame should feel like the watcher has been circling for hours.

**Light:** Deep pink haze rising from the horizon behind the skyline, acting as a soft backlight. The city lights are sparse and dim. Heavy edge vignette.

**Pacing:** Slow. The eye should settle before the first cut.

---

### Shot 2: The Sleeper (0:02 to 0:04, 48 frames)

**Subject:** A dark bedroom. A phone lies face down on a nightstand, its back faintly glowing pink. A human shape sleeps, out of focus.

**Motion:** Rack focus. The camera begins on the blurred sleeper, then pulls focus to the phone's dim glow. A slow lateral slide of about 2 percent, left to right.

**Light:** Only the phone's pink glow and a thin strip of pink from the window. Everything else falls to black.

**Pacing:** Stillness with one controlled focus pull. The shot should feel like breath held.

---

### Shot 3: The Wall of Watchers (0:04 to 0:06, 48 frames)

**Subject:** A tall dark wall covered in rows of dim, unlit screens. As the camera moves, the screens wake one by one with abstract, unlabeled light patterns.

**Motion:** A lateral dolly along the wall, moving at a steady pace. The screens light in a slow sequential ripple from the left edge toward the right.

**Light:** Cool blue-white from the screens, mixed with pink ambient spill from the floor. The patterns are abstract shapes and pulses with no readable detail.

**Data note:** All light patterns on the screens are Illustration. They are abstract, unlabeled, and do not represent any real market, price, or chart.

**Pacing:** Steady, rhythmic. The ripple sets a heartbeat for the sequence.

---

### Shot 4: The Iris (0:06 to 0:08.5, 60 frames)

**Subject:** A single large circular form in the dark, built from concentric rings of pink and white light. It reads as an eye that never closes.

**Motion:** The camera pushes slowly toward the center of the iris, about 6 percent over the shot. The inner rings rotate in opposite directions at a slow, hypnotic speed.

**Light:** The rings are the only source. Pink outer rings, white-pink core. Everything outside the iris stays black.

**Data note:** The ring movement is abstract light pattern, filed as Illustration. It carries no data, labels, or numbers.

**Pacing:** This is the longest shot and the peak of attention. Hold the push without rushing it.

---

### Shot 5: Data Without Promises (0:08.5 to 0:10.5, 48 frames)

**Subject:** Abstract ribbons of light flowing through a black void, like currents of information moving continuously.

**Motion:** The ribbons move left to right across frame at a steady flow. The camera tracks slightly with them, adding a gentle parallax drift.

**Light:** Thin pink and cool white lines against black. No color peaks, no spikes that could suggest gains or losses.

**Data note:** Filed as Illustration. The ribbons are decorative abstraction, not a chart, feed, or any real market output.

**Pacing:** Smooth and continuous. Let it breathe so the viewer reads it as ambient flow, not a dashboard.

---

### Shot 6: Watched, Not Worried (0:10.5 to 0:12.5, 48 frames)

**Subject:** The sleeper's face, lit faintly by the pink glow from the phone. Calm, eyes closed, resting.

**Motion:** A very slow push-in, about 3 percent. The face stays in soft focus until the final half second, when the focus settles on the eyelids.

**Light:** Pink rim light on the cheek and brow. Warm shadow on the rest of the face. Nothing harsh.

**Pacing:** Quiet and human. This shot is the emotional reason the watcher exists: someone can rest because something is watching.

---

### Shot 7: The Watcher Holds (0:12.5 to 0:15, 60 frames)

**Subject:** Pull back from the sleeper's room to the dark city skyline, where a single pink point of light remains lit high on a tower, steady and unblinking.

**Motion:** A long, slow pull-back of about 8 percent over the shot, widening from close to wide. The single light holds its position in frame the whole time.

**Light:** One pink point, sharp and steady. Thin horizon haze behind it. The vignette tightens as the frame goes to black at the final frames.

**Pacing:** The slowest shot. Let the stillness land. The last second should be near silence visually, with the pink point as the final image before the fade.

---

**Production reminders:** Keep every shot free of readable detail, including screens, signs, and labels. Light patterns and ribbons are Illustration only. No brand mark appears on screen.
