# Wire C Video Direction: The Watcher

**Brand:** Korvus
**Slot:** Tomorrow, 11:00 ET
**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 13 seconds, one idea
**Idea:** The market is watched 24/7 while the trader sleeps.
**Overlay:** Written separately by quill and baked on after render. Leave the negative space described below open.

---

## Opening Frame (0.00 to 1.00 s): The Hook

The first frame is near black, with the room barely visible. Within the first 0.2 seconds, a thin pink light line rises from the bottom edge of the frame and climbs about a third of the way up, like a sensor waking. At 0.4 seconds the room's darkness breathes once: a soft magenta bloom spreads from the right side of frame and fades back. By 0.8 seconds the camera has begun a slow forward push, and by 1.0 second the glow of an unseen screen is fully lit.

**Camera:** Locked-off at the start, then a steady gimbal push-in beginning at 0.6 seconds. Lens equivalent around 35mm. No shake.

**Lighting:** Almost total darkness. The only source is the pink line and the bloom.

**Color:** Deep black with a saturated pink-magenta accent. No other hues.

**Motion:** The rising line and the bloom give the hook its movement inside the first second.

---

## Beat Two (1.00 to 4.00 s): The Room

The camera continues forward through a dark bedroom or home office at night. A desk sits in the foreground, holding a cold mug and a closed notebook. The chair is empty. No person appears at any point, and no faces appear in any frame. A window to the left reflects the pink glow, and the dark glass shows a faint double image of the desk.

Dust motes drift slowly through the light beam from the screen. This small detail anchors the scene in a lived-in space.

**Camera:** Continued push-in at a steady pace. The move should feel patient, not rushed.

**Lighting:** Single motivated source from the screen, with a faint rim of magenta on the desk edge and the window frame. Shadows stay deep and close to black.

**Color:** Black, charcoal, and pink-magenta only. The window reflection is desaturated and cool, but still reads as dark.

**Motion:** Slow and continuous. The push-in carries the viewer toward the glow. Dust drifts at a crawl.

---

## Beat Three (4.00 to 8.00 s): The Money Shot

The terminal fills the center of the frame. The product interface appears as abstract luminous structure: soft pink light bands, glowing grid lines, and vertical strokes that rise and settle slowly. Nothing on the screen resolves into readable text, numerals, or recognizable chart labels. The interface should read as a living instrument, not as a set of figures.

At 4.0 seconds, the camera begins a gentle orbit of about 15 degrees to the left around the terminal. At 5.0 seconds, a rack focus moves from the window reflection to the glowing interface, so the screen sharpens while the window softens.

At 6.0 seconds, a single pale sweep line crosses the interface from left to right, as if the system is scanning the whole market at once. It is the only moment of fast motion in this beat, and it lasts under half a second.

**Camera:** Orbit from 4.0 to 8.0 seconds, followed by a rack focus at 5.0 seconds. The orbit stays smooth and unhurried.

**Lighting:** The screen is now the key light. Pink spills onto the desk, the mug, and the edge of the chair. Blacks remain dense.

**Color:** Pink-magenta dominates the interface. The sweep line is a near-white pink. Background stays black.

**Motion:** Light bands rise and settle at a steady, breathing rhythm. The sweep at 6.0 seconds is the single accent. No graphs with implied values, no price movement, no profit cues.

---

## Beat Four (8.00 to 11.00 s): The Watch Continues

The camera pulls back slowly through the window. Outside, a dark city sits quietly under a night sky. A few distant lights are scattered across the buildings. A faint pink horizon glow sits low on the skyline, suggesting that the screen's light reaches beyond the room. Nothing moves in the city except perhaps a single distant light that flickers and steadies.

This beat holds the watcher idea: the desk is empty, the room is quiet, and the system keeps watching while the city sleeps.

**Camera:** Pull back and slightly upward, moving from interior to window to exterior. Pace slows as it moves outward.

**Lighting:** Exterior is nighttime and very low key. The horizon glow is the faintest pink, almost a suggestion. Interior light fades behind the glass.

**Color:** Black and deep navy-black, with pink only at the horizon. Keep saturation low outside.

**Motion:** Very slow pull-back. The single distant light is the only movement in the city.

---

## Beat Five (11.00 to 13.00 s): Return and Settle

The camera pushes back toward the terminal, moving inward to the glow. The sweep line crosses the interface once more at 11.6 seconds, then the interface dims by degrees. The light bands settle, and the pink bloom contracts toward a single point of light at the center of the screen.

Between 12.0 and 13.0 seconds, the frame empties into black. The composition should leave the upper-middle and lower third of the frame open, with the dark room and a faint pink ambient glow there, so quill's overlay has clean space to sit.

**Camera:** Slow push back toward the screen, then a hold. The final second is a locked-off hold.

**Lighting:** The screen dims from full to a single soft point. The room goes almost fully dark.

**Color:** Pink-magenta contracts to a small glow, then black. The final frame is near black with a faint pink point.

**Motion:** Light bands settle and fade. The push ends in a still hold.

---

## Final Frame (13.00 s): Hold

The last frame is near black, with a faint pink point of light at the center of the screen and a soft ambient magenta haze around it. No objects are sharp enough to read as text. The hold lasts the final half second, leaving clean space for the overlay.

**Camera:** Locked off.

**Lighting:** Single faint pink point against black.

**Color:** Black with pink-magenta accent only.

**Motion:** None. Hold.

---

## Production Rules

- No real people's faces. The chair stays empty and no hands or bodies appear.
- No logos, no crown or raven marks, no brand wordmarks anywhere in frame.
- No readable screens. The interface is abstract light only.
- No illustrative data, numbers, chart values, or price movement shown or implied.
- No profit imagery of any kind: no money, no gains, no celebratory cues.
- No on-screen text written into the direction. Overlay is quill's, baked on after render.
- Palette is locked to black, charcoal, and pink-magenta, with near-white only on the sweep line.
- Pacing is slow and deliberate after the hook. The only fast motion is the opening rise and the 6.0 second sweep.
- Tone is calm vigilance: the watcher is awake, the room is quiet, and nothing is sold in the frame.

No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
