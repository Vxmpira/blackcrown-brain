# Watcher: Wordless 15-Second Clip Direction

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds, one continuous shot, no cuts, no text of any kind.
**Subject:** One raven, perched alone on a stone ledge inside a dark room.
**Market:** Not shown as data. The market is a field of slow pink light ribbons moving across the back wall, reading as a living current, never as a chart.
**Idea:** The market keeps moving while the trader sleeps, and something is still watching.

## Palette and Light
- Black base, about 85% of the frame, deep and warm-black rather than flat.
- Dark pink light ribbons (deep magenta to rose, low saturation at the edges) as the only real light source.
- Ribbons cast a soft pink rim along the raven's feathers and a faint reflection in its eye.
- One cool edge of distant city bokeh, dim and out of focus, far in the background, so the room feels like it sits above a sleeping city.
- Subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, slight halation around the brightest ribbons. No lens flare streaks.

## Camera
- Locked to a slow, continuous push-in along the ledge, starting at a low three-quarter angle and easing toward the raven's head.
- Movement is smooth and mechanical, like a dolly on a rail, never handheld shake.
- Total push: roughly 20 percent of frame height across the full 15 seconds.
- One focus pull (rack) from the ribbons to the raven's eye at about second 8, then back to the ribbons at about second 12.

## Shot Breakdown (one continuous take)

**0.0 to 1.0 seconds: Motion starts immediately.**
- The first frame already carries movement: a pink ribbon surges left to right across the wall while the raven's head snaps toward the lens.
- Wings half-open, one flick, then settle.
- The push-in begins on the same frame the ribbon crosses center, so the first second feels like something has just noticed the viewer.

**1.0 to 4.0 seconds: Watch.**
- The push-in continues steadily.
- The ribbons rise and fall in a slow, readable rhythm, like a tide with no start or end.
- The raven holds still, eyes tracking one ribbon at a time.
- Light rim on the feathers brightens and dims with each ribbon pass.

**4.0 to 8.0 seconds: The room darkens.**
- The ribbons thin and drift toward the upper frame, leaving the lower wall dimmer.
- The raven blinks once, slowly, with the lids closing from the bottom up.
- Background city bokeh shifts very slightly, as if traffic lights changed far below.

**8.0 to 12.0 seconds: The pulse.**
- At about second 8, the focus pulls from the ribbons to the raven's eye, reflecting one bright pink sweep.
- A second, stronger ribbon surge crosses the frame from right to left at about second 10, the largest motion beat of the clip.
- The raven lowers its head a few degrees, then raises it again.

**12.0 to 15.0 seconds: Hold and fade.**
- Focus returns to the ribbons.
- The push-in slows to a near stop by second 14.
- Ribbons keep drifting at low speed through the last frame, so the market is still moving when the clip ends.
- Final half second: the room edges fall to black, the pink light holds on the raven's rim, then the frame stops.

## Pacing
- Three beats of motion: the opening surge at 0 to 1 second, the rhythmic tide at 1 to 8 seconds, and the stronger pulse at 10 seconds.
- Between beats, the camera and light move slowly, like breathing.
- No sudden moves except the opening snap and the second-10 surge.
- Motion never fully stops until the final half second, so the clip reads as continuous watching.

## Frame Headroom for Overlay Lines
- Keep the top 30 percent of the frame dark and low detail. Ribbons may drift through it, but they stay dim there so a server-applied overlay line can sit on top and read clearly.
- Raven placement: lower-middle third, head near the horizontal center, body anchored to the ledge in the bottom 35 percent.
- Keep the bottom 18 percent of the frame free of bright ribbon peaks, because platform interface elements sit there on phones.
- Side margins stay dark so nothing bright touches the edges.
- No element in the frame should be shaped or sized to suggest text or characters. Ribbons are soft, unbroken curves, never segmented or glyph-like.

## Mood
- Quiet, patient, slightly ominous in a calm way.
- The watcher is not hunting anything and is not promising anything. It is simply awake while the world is asleep.
- No celebration, no money imagery, no green, no rising arrows, no triumph. The feeling is vigilance, not reward.

## Avoid
- Any visible screen, monitor, or device with readable content.
- Candlestick charts, axes, price lines, or anything resembling a financial graph.
- Coins, cash, gold, or wealth symbols.
- Green light or any color that signals gains.
- Cuts, split screens, or a second subject.
- Human faces or hands.
- Handheld shake or fast whip motion.

No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
