# Watcher Shot List: 15 Seconds, 9:16 Vertical

**Concept:** The all seeing watcher. The market never sleeps, and something keeps watch while the trader does. Camera moves from a single eye, out over a sleeping city, and back to the eye.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15.0 seconds, 24 fps, 7 shots.
**Palette:** Near black base, dark pink light sources (deep magenta to muted rose), no bright white, no gold.
**Data rule:** Any light pattern that reads as market data is abstract and unlabeled. Filed as Illustration.

## Shot List

**Shot 1 | 0.0 to 1.8s | Eye Open**
- **Subject:** Extreme close-up of a raven's eye, dark iris, a thin rim of dark pink light.
- **Motion (starts frame one):** Slow push-in, 3% per second. The eyelid begins to lift in the first 0.5 seconds.
- **Light:** Single rim light from the upper left in dark pink. Faint scrolling reflections of thin horizontal light bands sit in the cornea, unreadable.
- **Lens:** 85mm equivalent, shallow depth of field, focus on the iris.
- **Mood:** Stillness with intent. The first image sets the watcher.

**Shot 2 | 1.8 to 3.8s | The Watched City**
- **Subject:** Night skyline from a high vantage, dense rooftops, most windows dark, a few lit in muted rose.
- **Motion:** Slow vertical dolly down the face of a tower, 6% per second, ending at one lit window.
- **Light:** Faint haze in dark pink, soft glow from the windows, a faint grid overlay in the air at low opacity.
- **Lens:** 35mm equivalent, deep focus.
- **Mood:** Scale and quiet. Thousands of people sleeping, one vigil running.

**Shot 3 | 3.8 to 6.0s | The Sleeper**
- **Subject:** Dim bedroom. A figure asleep under a sheet, a phone face down on the nightstand glowing faint pink through the fabric.
- **Motion:** Slow orbit around the bed, 10 degrees over the shot.
- **Light:** Only the phone glow and a faint wash of city light through a blind. Shadows deep and clean.
- **Lens:** 50mm equivalent.
- **Mood:** Rest. The trader is not watching. The watcher is.

**Shot 4 | 6.0 to 8.5s | The Scan**
- **Subject:** A translucent dark glass panel rising from the bottom of frame, carrying abstract pulse lines in dark pink, unlabeled, no axes, no digits.
- **Motion:** Panel rises with a steady pull back. A single scan band sweeps top to bottom once, smooth, no flicker.
- **Light:** Pink emitted from the panel onto black glass reflections. Fill kept below 10 percent.
- **Lens:** 50mm equivalent, slight low angle.
- **Mood:** Work done in the dark. Illustration: the pulse lines are illustrative only and carry no real market values.

**Shot 5 | 8.5 to 11.0s | The Perch**
- **Subject:** Raven silhouette on a ledge against a dark pink sky, wings folded, head turning slowly toward camera.
- **Motion:** Head turn, roughly 40 degrees, taking the full shot. Camera holds still.
- **Light:** Backlit rim in dark pink, the body almost fully black.
- **Lens:** 135mm equivalent, compressed background.
- **Mood:** Patience. The watcher holds its post.

**Shot 6 | 11.0 to 13.5s | The Sweep**
- **Subject:** Wide view of the city, a slow horizontal wave of pale rose light crossing the rooftops left to right, like a watch lamp passing over sleeping streets.
- **Motion:** Camera drifts right at 4% per second. The wave moves in the opposite direction, creating a counter sweep.
- **Light:** The wave is the only bright element. Everything else stays in shadow.
- **Lens:** 35mm equivalent.
- **Mood:** Coverage. Nothing is missed.

**Shot 7 | 13.5 to 15.0s | Closing Eye**
- **Subject:** Return to the eye from shot 1, now open fully, a single pink pulse reflected in the cornea.
- **Motion:** Push in slowly and hold the last 0.4 seconds. Fade to black over the final 0.3 seconds, a thin pink edge lingering a moment longer than the black.
- **Light:** Identical to shot 1 for continuity, a touch brighter in the reflected pulse.
- **Lens:** 85mm equivalent.
- **Mood:** The watcher never blinks. End on the eye.
