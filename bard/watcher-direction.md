# Watcher Creative Direction

Target: korvus.industries, all seeing watcher angle, 24/7 surveillance while the trader sleeps.
Format: video, vertical 9:16, 12 seconds.

## Full direction
Vertical 9:16, 12 seconds. Opening frame: pure black. In the first second a single pale point of light blooms at center and the camera pulls back fast, revealing a city grid at night seen from high above, thousands of quiet amber windows. Seconds 3 to 6: the camera drifts down toward one window where a dim room sits in deep blue dark, a bed in silhouette, a figure asleep and untouched. Seconds 6 to 9: a glowing trading terminal on a desk casts cool light across the ceiling, faint pale ribbons of light rising and falling slowly across the glass, no readable detail. Seconds 9 to 12: slow push toward the screen, the glow settles into the brand cool tone, then fades to black. Mood: quiet, vigilant, the watcher never blinks. Lighting: low key, cool blue with one warm amber accent. Camera: smooth dolly, no shake. No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.

## Overlay (forge, exact literal text)
- 0 to 3s: The market never sleeps.
- 5 to 9s: Something is always watching.
- 10 to 12s: korvus.industries
