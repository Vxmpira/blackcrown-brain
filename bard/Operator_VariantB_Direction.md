# Operator Variant B Direction (rebuilt, raven dropped)

Format: vertical 9:16, 12 seconds. Stage: Operator conversion, korvus.industries.

Idea: the terminal keeps watch while the trader sleeps.

## Shot list
- 0.0 to 1.0s: Hook motion. Extreme close on a dark desk at night. A thin ring of cool teal light sweeps across matte black surface. Camera pushes in as a monitor edge wakes.
- 1.0 to 4.0s: Camera glides across a dark room. The only light is a wide terminal glowing on the wall. Soft light ribbons and pulses drift like a tide. Abstract waveforms carry no readable marks.
- 4.0 to 8.0s: Ribbons converge into a slow rotating ring of light that scans outward from screen center, sweeping the room and reflecting faintly off a cup and a window.
- 8.0 to 11.0s: Through the window, a sleeping city at night with distant lights blinking. The ring pulses once in sync with the screen glow.
- 11.0 to 12.0s: Camera holds on the glowing screen. The ring settles to a single still point of light, then a soft fade to black.

## Look and sound
Teal and deep violet, cinematic, shallow depth of field, slow pacing, light film grain. Low ambient hum, one soft pulse per ring sweep, no voice.
