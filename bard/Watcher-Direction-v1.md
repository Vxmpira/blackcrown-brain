Shot list:
1. (0 to 1s) Hook. Black frame, then a single cold blue light blooms in a dark room. Camera pushes in fast toward a wide glowing monitor. Motion inside the first second.
2. (1 to 4s) The monitor shows an abstract dense field of luminous vertical bars and slow flowing lines, pulsing in rhythm. Empty chair in front of it, city lights through a rain streaked window behind.
3. (4 to 8s) A translucent raven silhouette made of soft light perches on the top edge of the screen, head tilting slowly, watching the field below. Faint crown shaped halo above it.
4. (8 to 11s) Camera orbits the glowing terminal, the field of lines rises and tightens into a steady pattern, the room stays dark. Single hard highlight on the raven's eye.
5. (11 to 12s) Hold on the dark room, the terminal glow the only light. Clean fade to black.
