## What the numbers teach, as law for future creatives

1. The first second IS the video. 9 out of 10 viewers came from For You cold; the opening frame must open mid-motion with the sharpest visual, never a slow fade or a static screen grid. The hook overlay line lands at t=0, not t=2.
2. The on-screen text in this clip was AI-generated footage text and it misspelled "tabs" as "tabes" on screen. 324 strangers saw a typo. This is exactly why the NO TEXT LAW and the forge exist; the law is vindicated by platform data, never relax it.
3. 324 views produced 0 followers and no measurable site clicks. A view without a reason to stay is worth nothing; every clip needs one concrete payoff moment mid-video (a number, a before-after, a reveal), because retention past second 5 is what the algorithm pays with more reach.
4. Nobody finds us by search yet. Captions should carry one searchable phrase a trader would actually type ("futures news terminal", "trading open checklist") instead of brand-only wording.
5. 13 likes on 324 views (4 percent) is decent for cold traffic; the content angle (too many tabs, one screen) resonates. Keep the angle, fix the first second and the typo-free overlay.
