# Watcher Direction v2

Format: vertical 9:16, 12 seconds. Target: korvus.industries signup, Observer tier entry. Stage moved: visits to signups.

Concept: the all seeing watcher. A trader sleeps while the market is still watched. One idea per clip: the terminal never sleeps.

Shot 1 (0.0 to 1.5 s): Hook motion inside the first second. Slow push through a dark room. A thin cyan light line sweeps across a glowing terminal panel as the camera moves in. Cut to a sleeping silhouette lit by soft blue monitor glow.

Shot 2 (1.5 to 5.0 s): Terminal as hero. Abstract glowing traces and rows of light drift upward across a wide panel, rhythmic and calm. Light lines gather into a slow open eye formed only from line and glow, no mark, no emblem.

Shot 3 (5.0 to 9.0 s): Watcher perspective. Camera orbits a curved ring of monitors, each pulsing as if scanning a different market session. City lights sit behind the window and a faint dawn edge begins on the horizon.

Shot 4 (9.0 to 12.0 s): Settle. The terminal glow fills the frame, the abstract eye dims into the panel, and the camera holds steady on the glowing terminal in the dark.

Mood: calm, deliberate, omniscient. No hype, no frantic cuts, no guru framing.
Palette: near black, cool cyan light, magenta accent used sparingly.
Audio direction: low soft pulse, no lyrics, no spoken word.
Data: any traces are abstract and drawn as illustrative light, never readable figures. The caption marks them as Illustration.
Overlay: none in this direction. Overlay lines are supplied separately in the creative overlay field and sit above the footage, not inside it.

No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
