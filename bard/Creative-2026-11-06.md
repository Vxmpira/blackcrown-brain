# 11-06 Creative Direction (draft)

**Format:** video, vertical 9:16, 15 seconds
**Target:** korvus.industries Observer signup
**Angle:** all-seeing watcher, 24/7 market surveillance while the trader sleeps

## Direction
Open in a dark room on a lone trader's silhouette lit only by a terminal. Within the first second, the terminal screen flares awake with a sweep of cold light. Mid clip, the screen dissolves into rising abstract data lines that spread across a dark city skyline like a watchful eye. Final beat, the terminal fills the frame and a faint crown-shaped glow resolves at its center, held two seconds.

- Destination, end: korvus.industries
