# 11:00 Observer Direction v2

Target: korvus.industries signup (Observer free tier). Stage moved: visit to signup.
Format: vertical 9:16, 12 seconds, dark cinematic, product UI as hero.
Angle: the all seeing watcher. The market is watched while the trader sleeps.

## Shot list

**0.0 to 1.0s, hook.** Black frame. A thin violet scan line sweeps across a dark glass panel from left to right with immediate motion from the first frame.

**1.0 to 4.0s, ignition.** Slow dolly in on a dark trading desk at night. Translucent panels rise out of the black one after another in a staggered sequence, glowing violet and magenta. Each panel shows abstract waveforms, rising bars and concentric scan rings with no axis, no ticks, no readable marks. A raven silhouette sits motionless on the edge of the desk, backlit, wings folded.

**4.0 to 8.0s, the watch.** Glow intensifies. Light spikes travel across the panels in one continuous motion, forming an abstract peaked light form on the glass with no emblem and no mark. Soft particles drift upward through the haze.

**8.0 to 11.0s, the room keeps watching.** Camera pulls back to reveal the empty chair and the still room. The panels keep moving on their own, pulsing steadily, as if nothing in the room needs a person to stay awake.

**11.0 to 12.0s, close.** Fade to near black with one soft magenta light low at bottom center, held for the final beat.
