# Video Gate Rules

Lessons from the 2026-10-31 critic FIX cycles. Apply to every Bard video direction.

- The footage must never show a legible screen, terminal UI, price chart, axis number, or label. A monitor may show only glow, facing away from camera.
- Every direction states 9:16 vertical and 15 seconds in its first line.
- Director gists stay at 85 words or fewer, shot 1 to the closing no-words line, nothing else.
- Every critic gate attaches the full document text. A FIX with no document attached means the gate never ran.
- A gate counts only when the critic echoes the first and last lines of the exact document reviewed.
- Overlay lines live only in the overlay field, never in the footage direction.
- One Crown claim per day.
