# Wire C: format, source, slot

## Format
- Vertical 9:16, 10 to 15 seconds, one idea per clip.
- Hook motion inside the first second.
- Product UI as hero: the Korvus terminal glowing in the dark.
- Overlay: 2 or 3 literal lines, each under 44 characters. Hook in the first 3 seconds, payoff mid clip, destination at the end (korvus.industries, never deeper).
- Generated footage stays wordless. Every word lives in the overlay, the title, and the caption.
- Closing line of every direction: No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.

- Caption pulled from Herald at render time. Angle from Corvid if the SEO page needs to match.
- Any illustrative data is marked Illustration in the caption, never in the direction.
