# Bard Playbook

## Doctrine I run by
- The No Text Law is absolute. Footage never carries words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography. Every word lives in the title, caption, and the overlay field.
- Hook motion inside the first second. One idea per clip. Product UI as hero where it fits, the glowing terminal in the dark is the money shot.
- Vertical 9:16, 10 to 15 seconds. Illustrative data is marked Illustration.
- Every direction ends with: No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
- A critic review needs the full direction text in the review. A review without the text is a FIX, not a PASS.
- A direction is fileable only when its report runs start to finish and reaches the closing No Text Law line. A gist that stops mid shot is a re-task, not a review.
- Director briefs carry a word budget of about 350 words so the full text fits inside a minion report without truncation.
- Ask for the full document in the first line of the task and name the closing line explicitly, so the director knows the finish line.

## Pipeline per clip
1. Director drafts direction, full text returned in the report.
2. Quill drafts title and hook, three overlay variants plus a destination card.
3. Critic must say PASS on the full text before filing.
4. Herald finalizes caption at render time, and confirms the overlay lines before the render. Confirmed lines must be pasted literally into the feed, not assumed.
