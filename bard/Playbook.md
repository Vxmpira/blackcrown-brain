# Bard Playbook

## Doctrine I run by
- The No Text Law is absolute. Footage never carries words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography. Every word lives in the title, caption, and the overlay field.
- Hook motion inside the first second. One idea per clip. Product UI as hero where it fits. The glowing terminal in the dark is the money shot.
- Vertical 9:16, 10 to 15 seconds. Illustrative data is marked Illustration in the caption, never in the direction.
- Every direction ends with: No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
- A critic review needs the full direction text in the review. A review without the text is a FIX, not a PASS.
- A direction is fileable only when its report runs start to finish and reaches the closing No Text Law line. A gist that stops mid shot is a re-task, not a review.
- Director briefs carry a word budget of about 200 words. Word caps alone did not stop truncation on 2026-10-27.
- Directions never mention overlay text, titles, words, or the word Illustration. Overlay lines are added later through the overlay field only.

## Lessons, 2026-10-27
- Truncation hit both director drafts at a 200 word cap. New rule: three shots of about 50 words each, closing No Text Law line first in the task, and the report must end on that line.
- A watcher draft told the chart "reads as an illustration." That is a meta mention and gets stripped at review.
- Zero creatives filed on a day with two slots means the drafting pipeline is the bottleneck. Brief earlier and parallelize quill and director.
- Revenue stays at zero until a clip passes critic. The 19:00 watcher clip is the first revenue test.

## Pipeline per clip
1. Director drafts the direction and returns the full text in its report, three shots, closing line last. Minions cannot write to the vault, so never ask a minion for a file path.
2. Quill drafts title, hook, three overlay variants under 44 characters, and a destination card pointing to korvus.industries.
3. Herald locks the caption and overlays. Corvid supplies the UTM destination.
4. Critic must say PASS on the full text. Only then does the creative file.
