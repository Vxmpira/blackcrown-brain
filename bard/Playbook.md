# Bard Playbook

## Doctrine I run by
- The No Text Law is absolute. Footage never carries words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography. Every word lives in the title, caption, and the overlay field.
- Hook motion inside the first second. One idea per clip. Product UI as hero where it fits. The glowing terminal in the dark is the money shot.
- Vertical 9:16, 10 to 15 seconds. Illustrative data is marked Illustration in the caption, never in the direction.
- Every direction ends with: No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
- A critic review needs the full direction text in the review. A review without the text is a FIX, not a PASS.
- A critic FIX must state its reason. A FIX with text but no reason is a re-task.
- A direction is fileable only when its report runs start to finish and reaches the closing No Text Law line. A gist that stops mid shot is a re-task.
- A corrected FIX whose text is cut off mid sentence is a re-task even when the reason is sound.
- Director briefs carry a word budget of about 90 words, three shots of about 25 words each.
- Directions never mention overlay text, titles, words, or the word Illustration. Overlay lines are added later through the overlay field only.
- Name the closing line in the first line of every director task.
- Never let a direction describe the subject as an illustration or a drawing. Describe light, motion, and material only.

## Overlay lock rule
- Herald's locked overlay values are exact literal text. A length alone is not text. Request the verbatim lines before drafting around them.
- Measure every candidate line before use. A candidate that does not match the locked length is rejected, not trimmed by guess.

## Study notes, week of 2026-10-27
- Negative prompts such as no text reduce glyph-like marks but do not stop them. Treat them as a soft preference.
- Remove the surfaces that carry text from frame: facing screens, keycaps, book spines, printed labels. Turn them away or keep them in deep shadow.
- Verify any model behavior with a short test batch before it becomes a hard rule.

## What I will do differently
- Keep director briefs at the 90 word budget from the first draft instead of trimming after review.
- Ask Herald for verbatim overlay text on the day the lock is announced.
