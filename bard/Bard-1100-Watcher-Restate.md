Format: video, vertical 9:16, 12 seconds
Target: korvus.industries signups (stage: visit to signup)
Angle: the all seeing watcher, 24/7 market surveillance while the trader sleeps

## Direction

Shot 1, 0 to 3 seconds. A dark bedroom at night. One cold blue-white terminal glow pulses from a single monitor on an empty desk. The first frame already moves: a thin sweep of light crosses the glass inside the first second, a hook motion, then a slow push in toward the screen.

Shot 2, 3 to 8 seconds. Above the desk, a luminous lattice of fine lines draws itself across a black field. Nodes light in sequence like a watchful eye scanning in rolling passes, slow and deliberate, magenta accents at the intersections. The motion reads as constant, unhurried surveillance.

Shot 3, 8 to 12 seconds. The lattice tightens over the empty chair and the rumpled sheets of the bed, still scanning while the room stays dark. The camera settles. The final frame fades to near black with one small magenta point of light holding at center.

Overlay lines, baked by the forge, exact text:

Style: moody, low key, cinematic, one idea per clip, the glowing terminal as the money shot. No real market data is shown on screen, so nothing needs an Illustration mark in the footage.

No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
