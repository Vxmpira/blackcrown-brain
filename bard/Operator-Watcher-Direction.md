# Operator Watcher, Direction Draft

Target: korvus.industries, Operator tier (visits to signups)
Format: video, vertical 9:16, 12 seconds
Angle: the all seeing watcher. The market never sleeps and the terminal never blinks.
Hero: the Korvus terminal glowing in a dark room (panels are Illustration only)

## Shot list

**0.0 to 1.0s, hook motion.** Extreme close up of a dark lens made of thin cool light rings. The rings snap into alignment on frame one. Teal rim light, black field.

**1.0 to 4.0s.** Slow pull back from the lens. It resolves into a wall of dark monitors in an empty room at night, city glow through a window. Every screen shows abstract line graphs and scrolling feeds. Camera drifts across the wall at walking pace.

**4.0 to 8.0s, payoff.** One central panel dominates the frame: a dense glowing terminal grid of candles and horizontal level lines (Illustration). A thin ring of light sweeps across it, and the panel pulses as the sweep passes. Small particles drift inward toward the panel, as if data is being gathered.

**8.0 to 11.0s.** Slow orbit around the screen wall while the room stays empty. A chair sits empty facing the screens and a cup on the desk steams gently. The watcher stays awake while the trader sleeps.

**11.0 to 12.0s, destination beat.** Camera settles back on the central lens. The lens iris closes to one bright point of light, which fades into open dark space with room for the overlay.

## Look and motion
- Low key lighting, deep black, cool teal and warm amber from the screens only.
- Slow dolly moves, one clean rack focus from lens to panel.
- No people in frame except the empty chair.

## Audio direction
- Low synth pulse rising across the clip.
- One clean sting at 1.0s.
- Silence beat at 11.0s.
- No vocals, no spoken words.

## Overlay (forge field, literal text, baked by the server)
- 0.0 to 2.8s: The market never sleeps.
- 4.5 to 8.0s: Korvus watches every tick.
- 9.0 to 12.0s: korvus.industries

Each line is under 44 characters. The hook lands in the first 3 seconds, the payoff sits mid clip, the destination closes the clip.
