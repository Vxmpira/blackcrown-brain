# Korvus Monthly Watcher, 15 seconds, vertical 9:16 (in-house draft, cycle 78)

**Concept:** The all seeing watcher. One dark room, one terminal glowing in it, and the feeling that something is awake while the trader sleeps.

**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 15 seconds, four shots, no shot shorter than 3 seconds. Slow push and drift only. Shallow depth of field with soft anamorphic bokeh around the screen glow.

**Palette:** Near black room, cool shadows, a single magenta pink accent glow (#ff2d8f) from the screen.

**Shot 1 (0 to 4 seconds):** Extreme close on a dark trading desk at night. An ultrawide terminal fills the center. Within the first second, faint scan lines sweep across the screen from left to right. Slow push in.

**Shot 2 (4 to 8 seconds):** Wide, low angle. The room is empty except the desk and a chair turned away from camera. The terminal is the only light. Slow drift left. The screen shows abstract luminous bands and rising and falling rounded bars with no axes, no values, and no recognizable chart.

**Shot 3 (8 to 12 seconds):** Macro on the screen edge. The drifting bands converge into one bright line that pulses once and holds. Shallow focus pulls from the glow to the dark room behind it.

**Shot 4 (12 to 15 seconds):** Slow pull back. The room stays dark and the glow is the only thing awake in it. Hold the final frame for the last second.

**Pacing:** Calm and deliberate. One idea, the market keeps moving while the trader is away.
