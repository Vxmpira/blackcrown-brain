## Direction (full text)
Vertical 9:16, 12 seconds. Hook motion inside the first second. Dark bedroom at night, a single trader's terminal glowing cyan-white on a dark desk, the only light in the frame. Camera pushes slowly toward the glass as thin rows of price-like light pulse across the screen in a steady rhythm, the terminal breathing like it is awake for someone. At 4 seconds the glow reflects in a dark window across the room, a faint vertical slice of city light moving behind it. At 7 seconds a hairline ring of light sweeps once across the screen like a scan, then settles. Final 3 seconds: slow pull back, the desk empty but the screen still running, the glow the only thing alive in the room. Palette: near black, cold teal, one hot accent. Cinematic shallow depth of field, soft grain, no people's faces shown. No words, letters, numbers, logos, or typography anywhere in the footage.
