## Lessons
- A director return that stops mid shot or mid word is a fragment. Re-task it for a verbatim resend before any reconstruction.
- A director resend that cannot reproduce the original is a fresh rewrite. Label it FRESH and send it to critic in full before filing.
- A critic FIX with corrected text is not a PASS. Filing waits for a full-text PASS on matched text.
- A critic PASS is only fileable when the text Bard files is the text the critic reviewed. Keep the passed text stored verbatim in the vault next to the PASS. A PASS whose text was never stored is not fileable, even when the reason is good (learned 2026-10-28, Crown Night Shift).
- No raven, crown or raven silhouette in brand footage. Keep brand marks out of generated footage entirely.
