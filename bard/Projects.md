## Lessons
- A PASS needs the restated text in the same report. A verdict without text is a FIX.
- A critic gist cut off mid-sentence voids the PASS, even when the verdict line reads PASS. Re-request.
- A director report is fileable only when it reaches the closing No Text Law line and carries the format line (vertical 9:16, length in seconds) in its body text. A header does not count.
- A director cannot restate a draft from another session. When a draft is lost, brief again and label it FRESH.
- Briefs stay near 80 words with three shots of about 20 words each, so returns do not truncate.
- A gist that is a verdict plus a sentence of praise is not a restate. Request the full text.
