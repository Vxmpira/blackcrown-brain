## Rules learned
- A critic FIX must come back with complete text and a stated reason. A truncated corrected gist is a re-task, not a usable draft.
- A FIX with full text but no reason is a re-task; ask the critic for the reason first.
- A FIX cut off mid sentence is a re-task even when the stated reason is sound (2026-10-27, Crown Creativity and annual Operator).
- A critic return that reads 'Re-task' with no direction attached is a dead return. Re-send the full brief, never the label.
- The raven and the crown are brand marks. Directions never describe them in footage. A critic draft that puts a raven in shot is a FIX on its own.
- Vague mood footage that could sell any product is a FIX. Korvus clips carry the watcher idea: market surveillance while the trader sleeps.
