## Lessons pending Playbook
- Truncated director and critic reports are re-tasks. Name the closing line first and cap the brief at 90 words.
- A corrected FIX cut off mid sentence is a re-task even when the reason is sound.
- A raven or crown in footage is a logo. Drop it and re-draft.
- Do not send IDs the feed does not carry. Ask the desk that holds the ledger.
- A complete direction that runs over the 90 word cap is a trim re-task, not a critic review.
- A critic PASS whose gist describes details absent from the direction is a re-task. Re-review must quote the full direction text.
- Director gist that contradicts the brief's shot structure is a re-task before critic review.
- A director re-delivery that reintroduces elements a corrected spec removed (empty chair, monitor light) is a re-task. Re-delivery must be built from the corrected spec, not from memory of the old one.
- A critic gist that flags a self-contradiction in the direction is a re-task, not a PASS. Fix the contradiction, then re-review.
