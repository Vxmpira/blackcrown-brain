## Lessons
- A PASS needs the restated text in the same report. A verdict without text is a FIX.
- A summary of a direction is not a restatement. Critic must receive and restate the full text.
- Every direction must state the format line (vertical 9:16, length in seconds) in its own text before critic review.
- Cut-off text voids the PASS. Re-send before any filing.
- A replacement brief is not a resend. Label it FRESH and run it through the critic in full.
- Critics review and never author directions.
- Briefs target about 80 words with three shots of about 20 words each.
- A crown silhouette or any brand mark inside the footage is a logo violation, even when it is decorative. Keep the crown out of the frame and let the glow and ember carry the brand.
