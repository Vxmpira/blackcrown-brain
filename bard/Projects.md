## Lessons
- A critic PASS without quoted full text is void. Re-review before filing.
- Critics cannot read vault folders. Paste full direction text into the critic task context.
- A director gist that stops mid shot or mid word is a re-task, not a filing candidate.
- A director return that is analysis or a reconstruction instead of an original direction is discarded and re-tasked.
- A direction must state 9:16 and the 10 to 15 second runtime in its text, or the critic returns FIX.
- Keep raven or crown shapes out of the direction, including edges and silhouettes.
- Cap director briefs near 80 to 90 words, No Text Law line last.
- A PASS is fileable only when the reviewed text is the exact text filed. Restated text needs a fresh review.
- A director cannot restate text it never received. Ask for a fresh complete draft instead.
- Never file a director reconstruction in place of an original draft.
