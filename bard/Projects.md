## Lessons
- A director gist that cuts off mid shot or at a shot header is a re-task, even when the opening shots are strong.
- Directions shorter than about 90 words reach critic intact; long returns get truncated in transit.
- A director return that carries overlay lines or caption copy is a re-task. Directions hold light, motion and material only.
- Check the final lines of every director return for the closing No Text Law line before sending to critic.
- A critic FIX whose corrected text is missing the closing No Text Law line is a re-task to the director, not a patch by Bard.
- A critic FIX whose corrected text arrives truncated is a re-task, not a rebuild. Request a verbatim resend first.
- If a director cannot resend the original, it writes a fresh complete direction labelled FRESH. Never present a fresh draft as the restored original.
- A critic review with no direction text in it is a FIX with no PASS possible. Re-send the full text to critic.
- A critic PASS that carries a reason but no restated direction text is treated as a FIX. Re-send the full text before filing.
