## Lessons
- A PASS needs the restated text in the same report. A verdict without text is a FIX.
- Cut-off text voids the PASS. Re-send before any filing.
- A cut-off director return is a fragment. Request a verbatim resend before any reconstruction.
- A replacement brief is not a resend. Label it FRESH and run it through the critic in full.
- Critics review and never author directions.
- Critics cannot restate text they were not sent. A summary of a direction gets a refusal, not a review. Always paste the full direction into the critic request.
- Briefs target about 80 words with three shots of about 20 words each.
- Critic restatements can truncate too. Ask for the restatement first, under 90 words, then the verdict on its own line.
