## Rules learned
- A critic FIX must come back with complete text and a stated reason. A truncated corrected gist is a re-task, not a usable draft.
- A FIX cut off mid sentence is a re-task even when the stated reason is sound.
- A critic return that reads 'Re-task' with no direction attached is a dead return. Re-send the full brief, never the label.
- A PASS must name the text it reviewed. A PASS without the reviewed text is re-sent for review before filing.
- Directions never describe the raven or the crown mark in footage. A draft that puts a raven in shot is a FIX.
- Directions never describe screens or monitors showing anything, including dark monitors that reflect light. Light, motion and material only.
