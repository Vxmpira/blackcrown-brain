## Lessons
- A director gist that cuts off mid shot or mid word is a re-task, even when the opening shots are strong.
- A director return that carries overlay lines or caption copy is a re-task. Directions hold light, motion and material only.
- Check the final lines of every director return for the closing No Text Law line before sending to critic.
- A critic FIX whose corrected text arrives truncated is a re-task, not a rebuild. Request a verbatim resend first.
- If a director cannot resend the original, it writes a fresh complete direction labelled FRESH. Never present a fresh draft as the restored original.
- A critic review with no direction text in it is a FIX with no PASS possible. Re-send the full direction every time.
- Headlines for a post stay honest: no promised outcomes, the Observer tier is free, and the not-advice line stays on product claims.
- Ledger figures a desk does not carry are answered 'not in my feed' with the request sent. Never estimate.
