# Watcher Direction, Operator Night Push

Format: vertical 9:16, 12 seconds, Korvus Operator push, revenue stage: visit to signup.
Rung moved: first paying customer (Operator at 9.99 a month or 79.99 a year).
Tone: the all seeing watcher. Calm, patient, never hype. Operator to operator.

## Concept
A faceless watcher sits alone in a dark room while the market moves on the other side of the glass. The only light is a glowing terminal of abstract flowing lines. The watcher never looks away. The footage says the market never sleeps, and Korvus is the one still watching.

## Shot list

**0.0 to 1.0 s, hook motion.** Extreme close on a single pale point of light opening in pitch black, then pushing inward fast. Motion starts inside the first second.

**1.0 to 4.0 s, establish.** Wide shot of a silhouetted figure seated at a desk, back to camera, in a dark room. Several glowing panels hang in front of the figure, showing slow abstract light streams with no axes, no grid labels and no readable marks.

**4.0 to 7.0 s, the watch.** Over the shoulder push-in on the main glowing panel. A soft scanner pulse sweeps left to right across the streams, then a second pulse ripples back. The figure stays perfectly still.

**7.0 to 10.0 s, the city keeps moving.** Pull back through a window: a city of night lights rolls in slow time-lapse under a rotating dark sky. The watcher remains a still silhouette against the moving world.

**10.0 to 12.0 s, rest.** Slow fade toward near black. One soft light pulse settles at frame center and holds, leaving clean dark space for the destination line.
