## Budget airline with strong balance sheet but growth stalling and price down

Ryanair is a low-cost airline operator that grew revenue 43% per year over three years but slowed to just 4% growth in the latest year. The company keeps 6% of revenue as profit, holds more cash than debt, and trades at 14 times earnings. Most analysts rate it a buy.
