## Profitable Indian IT firm losing revenue and share value; debt load is severe

Wipro is a large information technology services company based in India. It generated $10.4 billion in revenue and $1.5 billion in net profit in its last fiscal year. The company is profitable and produces free cash flow, but revenue has flat-lined over three years and fell 3% most recently.

### What is working

- Free cash flow of $1.8B shows the business converts profits into actual cash on hand
- Return on equity of 16% indicates the company generates decent returns on shareholder capital invested

### What to be careful about
- Revenue declined 3% year over year and has grown 0% annually over the past three years
- Earnings per share fell 44% year over year, a sharp drop in per-share profitability
- Debt of $161.8B is 16.7x shareholder equity, an extreme leverage ratio that limits financial flexibility

**Watch next:** Whether revenue stabilizes or returns to growth, since the company has shown no top-line expansion in three years and profitability has already begun to erode.
