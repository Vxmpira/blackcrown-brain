## Luxury automaker with fortress profitability trades at premium valuation

Ferrari is a high-end car manufacturer based in Italy that reported 7.1B EUR in revenue for fiscal 2025. The company converts more than one-fifth of every sales dollar into profit and generates substantial free cash flow. It trades on US exchanges as well as European markets.

### What is working
- Profitability is exceptional: keeps 22% of revenue as net profit and generates 26% free cash flow margin
- Return on equity of 41% shows the company deploys shareholder capital very efficiently
- Price momentum is strong: up 26.3% over the past 6 months and 67% over the past 5 years

### What to be careful about
- Valuation is steep at 40x earnings, meaning you pay 40 dollars for every dollar of annual profit
- Revenue growth is slowing: 7% year-over-year growth trails the 12% three-year average
- Stock is volatile with 31% yearly swings, and sits only 5% below its 52-week high of 436.54

**Watch next:** Whether the recent 7% revenue growth accelerates back toward the 12% three-year pace or continues to decelerate, since growth justifies the high valuation multiple.

**Tends to suit:** Long-term investors who want exposure to ultra-premium consumer goods, can tolerate high valuations, and accept significant year-to-year price swings.
