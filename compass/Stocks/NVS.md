## Profitable giant with slowing growth trades near 52-week high

Novartis is a large Swiss pharmaceutical company generating $14.0B in annual net income and $17.6B in free cash flow. Revenue grew just 2% year over year, while earnings per share jumped 22%, partly through share buybacks reducing the share count by 11% over three years.

### What is working
- Very profitable: keeps 18% of revenue as profit and delivers 30% return on equity
- Strong cash generation: $17.6B free cash flow and $11.4B cash on hand support dividends and buybacks
- Returning capital: 3.7% dividend yield plus ongoing share buybacks as count fell 11% in three years

### What to be careful about
- Sluggish top-line growth: revenue rose only 2% year over year, indicating slowing business momentum
- Expensive valuation: trades at 30x earnings, above many peers, with analysts split on value (44% buy, consensus Hold)
- High debt load: $28.7B debt, though manageable at 0.6x equity; limits financial flexibility

**Watch next:** Whether revenue growth accelerates above 2% in coming quarters, as earnings growth is currently driven by cost cuts and buybacks rather than core business expansion.
