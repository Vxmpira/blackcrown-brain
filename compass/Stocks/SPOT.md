## Streaming leader with strong cash generation but pricy valuation

Spotify is a Swedish audio streaming company generating 17.2B EUR in annual revenue with expanding profitability. It converts 13% of revenue to net income and produces substantial free cash flow of 2.9B EUR yearly. The company carries no reported debt and holds 5.3B EUR in net cash.

### What is working
- Revenue growing 10% year over year with 14% annualized growth over three years
- Free cash flow margin of 17% shows strong cash generation relative to sales
- Return on equity of 27% demonstrates efficient use of shareholder capital

### What to be careful about
- Trades at 33x earnings, which is elevated and leaves little room for disappointment
- Share count has grown 7.5% over three years, diluting existing shareholders
- Stock down 1.7% over the past year despite strong recent momentum gains

**Watch next:** Monitor whether the 10% year-over-year revenue growth can accelerate or stabilize given the company already trades at a premium multiple.
