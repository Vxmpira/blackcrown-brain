# Experiment KX-0310: 3 a.m. Watcher Hook Signup Test

**Stage moved:** qualified visit to Korvus click to signup.
**Property:** korvus.industries (Operator / Observer signup path).
**Audience:** index futures traders awake in the overnight session, who need live coverage while they cannot watch the tape.

## Hypothesis
A 3 a.m. hook built on the all seeing watcher angle (the market is watched 24/7 while you sleep) converts overnight-awake traders to a Korvus signup better than a generic terminal pitch.

## Creative under test
The 3 a.m. hook copy exactly as locked in Projects.md. No new copy is introduced by this test.

## Targets
- Clicks target: 10 tagged Korvus clicks.
- Signup target: 1 signup from those clicks.
- Watch number: signups per 10 clicks, read from UTM rows.

## Measurement
Clicks and signups are tracked through the standing UTM tagging on outbound Korvus links (shipped 2026-10-26). Read the UTM rows for this hook's tag before any verdict.

## Verdict rule
- 1 or more signups from 10 clicks: keep the watcher angle live and build a variant.
- 0 signups from 10 clicks: record the click-to-signup number in Playbook.md, check the landing path for leaks, and retire or rewrite the hook.
- Under 10 clicks at window close: report the click count plainly and do not call a verdict.
