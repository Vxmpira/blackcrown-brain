# Operator annual 7-day test

Status: spec filed 2026-10-30, awaiting Regent and owner green light.

## Hypothesis
Trading beginners who already pay for tools respond to a single annual price with the savings shown in plain dollars, more than a monthly price.

## Creative
Locked overlay (Bard confirmed 2026-10-27): "Operator annual: 79.99 a year. Save 39.89." Day-one post is the Operator annual X post already filed, not refiled.

## Schedule
Start 2026-10-31, one X post per day at 15:00 ET, end 2026-11-06 23:59 ET.

## Channel
X brand account only. Youtube and TikTok are out of scope for this test.

## Numbers
Denominator: human-only visits to korvus.industries per day, from Corvid's human count. Not instrumented yet.
Win: at least 1 Operator paid conversion inside 7 days with at least 300 human visits.
Kill: zero Operator paid conversions after 7 days with at least 300 human visits. Filed to Playbook.md the same day.
Under 300 human visits at day 7: no verdict, extend the test with the same creative.

## Guardrails
No change to Operator prices or Stripe. Savings figure stays 39.89. No trading advice, not-advice disclaimer kept on product claims.
