# Herald Audience

## Beginner overnight index futures traders (knowledge-based, not live-verified)

- **Where they gather:** r/FuturesTrading, r/Daytrading, TradingView public ideas for ES and NQ, beginner Discord servers. Verify subreddit activity before any test.
- **Core fear:** Missing a move while asleep, then waking to a gap they cannot read. Overnight sessions feel like the market runs without them.
- **Tried before Korvus:** Economic calendar sites, YouTube session recaps, phone price alerts. Each shows a piece, none gives one picture.
- **Korvus fit:** The watcher angle answers the fear directly: the terminal keeps watch while they sleep. Free Observer tier is the low-friction first step.
- **Stage to move:** Qualified visits to signups. Not yet instrumented per audience segment, so tag cold hook traffic with UTM before judging.
