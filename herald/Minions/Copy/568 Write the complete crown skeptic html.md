<h2>The five checks</h2>
<p><b>1. Storage.</b> Find out where conversations live and under what jurisdiction. If the policy page does not say, assume the answer is not in your favor.</p>
<p><b>2. Training.</b> Read whether your inputs are used to train models, and whether you can opt out. "We may improve our services" is a yes until proven otherwise.</p>
<p><b>3. Access.</b> Ask who inside the company can view stored content, and under what conditions. Support staff access should be logged and limited.</p>
<p><b>4. Control.</b> You should be able to export your data and delete it. If deletion is a request form with no timeline, treat that as a limit.</p>
<p><b>5. Retention.</b> Know how long data is kept after you delete a chat or close an account.</p>
