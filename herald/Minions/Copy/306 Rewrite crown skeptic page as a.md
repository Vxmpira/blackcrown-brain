<h1>Skeptical of AI with your data? Read this.</h1>
<p>Good. Skepticism is the right starting position. Anyone handing work to an AI should know where their data goes, what the tool does with it, and what it costs to rely on. Here are the questions we think you should ask before trusting any AI with real work, including ours.</p>

<h2>The hard questions to ask first</h2>
<ol>
<li>Where does my input go, and is it stored, logged, or used to train models? Get the answer in writing.</li>
<li>Who can read my conversations, including the company's own staff?</li>
<li>Can I delete my data, and does deletion actually remove it?</li>
<li>What does the tool cost at real volume, not at a demo level? Ask how usage is metered.</li>
<li>Is the usage limit stated plainly, or buried? Any tool that claims unlimited use is hiding a limit somewhere.</li>
<li>Does the reasoning hold up when you check it against a source you already trust?</li>
<li>If the tool is wrong, who is accountable, and how would you find out?</li>
</ol>
