<ul>
<li>What do you store, and for how long?</li>

<li>Who can read your conversations inside the company?</li>
<li>How do you delete what you have stored, and can you show that it happened?</li>
<li>What happens to your data if you stop paying, or never pay at all?</li>
</ul>
<p>Put those questions to us. Read the answers in our privacy terms before you paste anything sensitive. If an answer is vague, treat the vagueness as the answer.</p>
