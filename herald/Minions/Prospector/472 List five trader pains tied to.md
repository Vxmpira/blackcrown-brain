**Five trader pains tied to overnight index futures moves**

1. **Gap at the open.** Price can open well away from the prior close, so a stop may fill far worse than planned or the position is already past the trader's plan by the time they wake.
2. **Thin-liquidity wicks.** Overnight sessions carry less volume, so a level can be swept and reversed in minutes, and the trader wakes to a stop-out that looks like it happened for no reason.
3. **Scheduled news while asleep.** Overseas data releases and central bank remarks land during off hours, and the trader learns about them from a chart that has already moved.
4. **Unresolved overnight exposure.** A position left open overnight leaves the trader unsure whether they are up or down until morning, which cuts into sleep whether they check or not.
5. **Session level sweeps.** Asia and London often take out the levels a US-session trader was watching, so the setup is gone or invalidated before their window opens.

**Where observed:** These are common complaints in general trader communities such as futures subreddits and Discord trade-recap channels.
