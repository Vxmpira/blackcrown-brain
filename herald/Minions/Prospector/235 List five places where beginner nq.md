**Five venues where beginner NQ and ES traders talk about overnight sessions**

1. **Reddit, r/Daytrading and r/futures-style subs.** Forum. Beginners post overnight questions (Globex gaps, Asia range, why price moved at 3am) and get answers from peers at all hours. Self-promo and link rules are strict, and most subs require account history before any link is allowed. **Fit: weak. Only a helpful reply that mentions the tool, if the sub allows it.**

2. **Elite Trader futures forums.** Long-running forum with dedicated futures threads. Serious, detail-oriented traders who ask about session structure and levels. Promotion is restricted and moderated. **Fit: weak without a moderator OK.**

3. **TradingView public ideas and chat on ES and NQ.** Charting community. Overnight charts and session levels get discussed openly. Public links are common, but the platform and community flag promotional spam. **Fit: moderate. Educational posts that show the chart, not a hard sell.**

4. **Futures and day trading Discord servers.** Real-time chat where members talk through the overnight session as it happens. Each server sets its own rules, so the fit depends on the specific server. **Fit: depends on server rules. Check before posting.**

5. **YouTube live comments on overnight futures streams.** Live chat during overnight streams. Beginners ask questions in real time. Links in comments are often auto-flagged as spam. **Fit: poor for links. Use only organic, value-first replies.**

Note: venue rules change and were not checked live. Verify each one before any post goes out.
