# Overnight Index Futures Discussion Map: Watcher Targeting

*Source note: built from general knowledge of where these communities operate, not a live scan. Activity levels and subreddit sizes should be verified before the 11-22 package locks.*

## Ranked communities

1. **futures.io (formerly BigMikeTrading) forums.** Ranked first because its futures threads are long-running and dense, and active traders post pre-market and overnight levels there with the reasoning attached.
2. **r/Daytrading and r/FuturesTrading on Reddit.** Ranked second because they produce high-volume overnight questions from newer traders, which is the audience most likely to want a watcher that never sleeps.
3. **Elite Trader and Trade2Win futures forums.** Ranked third because they skew toward experienced traders who ask sharper questions about session structure, though posting is slower overnight.
4. **TradingView chat and ideas feed (ES, NQ, YM).** Ranked fourth because it is the most visible overnight chart discussion, but much of it is idea posting rather than live questions.
5. **FinTwit on X (futures and index cashtags, pre-market hours).** Ranked fifth because it moves fastest on headlines, but signal-to-noise is low and it is hard to target without drifting into hype.
6. **Private trading Discord servers and YouTube overnight streams.** Ranked sixth because they hold real conversation, but access is closed or the chat is hard to observe without joining in ways that break community norms.

## Questions traders ask overnight

- Is the Asia or Europe move holding into the US pre-market?
- Where are the overnight high and low, and has either been tested?
- Is the gap likely to fill at the cash open? (Market information only, not a forecast.)
- What is on the economic calendar before 9:30 ET, and how has the tape reacted to similar prints?
- Did a headline from China, Europe, or a central bank change the overnight range?
- Is Globex volume thin, and does that change how far a move can run?
- What does the overnight session look like compared with the prior day's close?

## Targeting implication for the Watcher

The overnight audience clusters around level and context questions, not trade calls. Watcher copy should answer "what happened and where are the levels" with Korvus scoring and session structure, and stay clear of anything that reads as a signal. Every product claim keeps the not-advice disclaimer.
