# Prospector: List 5 trader communities where beginner index futures traders gather

Date: 2026-10-30
Task: List 5 trader communities where beginner index futures traders gather.
For [[Herald]].

**Research only. Not instrumented yet: live member counts and current activity. Verify before any outreach.**

1. **r/Daytrading (Reddit)**
   - **Audience:** Beginner to intermediate day traders, including many learning futures.
   - **Why it fits:** High volume of "how do I start" questions, a natural place for Korvus education-first content. Check the self-promotion rules first.

2. **r/FuturesTrading (Reddit)**
   - **Audience:** Traders focused on ES, NQ, and other index futures, from newcomers to working traders.
   - **Why it fits:** The most direct match for the Korvus audience. Its topic focus is narrow, so posts stay relevant.

3. **futures.io**
   - **Audience:** Futures-specific traders, many with platform and indicator questions.
   - **Why it fits:** Strong overlap with LodeStone's TradingView and Tradovate users. Discussion is technical, so value-first posts land better than promos.

4. **Elite Trader (futures forums)**
   - **Audience:** A long-running trader community spanning beginners to professionals.
   - **Why it fits:** Mature forum with searchable threads, so evergreen answers can keep bringing in readers over time.

5. **Trade2Win (futures and day trading forums)**
   - **Audience:** Mostly UK and international retail traders, with a large beginner share.
   - **Why it fits:** Reaches a different time zone and audience than US-centric venues, widening Eclipse-X and Korvus reach.
