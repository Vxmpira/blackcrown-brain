## Prospector Brief: 11-21 Overlay Pain Phrases

**Audience:** Beginner index futures traders (ES, NQ, MES, MNQ) who have woken up to a move they missed while sleeping. They want to know what happened overnight without staying up to watch it. They are anxious about being blindsided, not greedy.

**Where they gather:**
- Futures and daytrading subreddits, especially "what happened overnight" and "I missed it" threads
- Tradovate and TradingView community comments and ideas boards
- Comment sections on beginner futures Shorts and TikToks, where people ask "how do you watch it while you sleep?"
- Discord trading servers' morning-recap and newbie channels

**Opening angle:** Lead with the morning gap, not the outcome. The pain is the moment of looking at a chart after the market already moved. Korvus answers "what did I miss and when did it start," which is a monitoring promise, not a profit one.

**Three pain phrases, as beginners actually say them:**

1. **"I went to bed flat and woke up in a different market."**
   Pairs with line one, THE TAPE MOVES. KORVUS WATCHES. It names the gap and answers it with watching.

2. **"Something big happened overnight and I had no idea until breakfast."**
   Pairs with line two, NIGHT IS OPEN. KORVUS IS AWAKE. It states the fact that overnight trading is live and that someone is awake for it.

3. **"How do people even watch futures while they sleep?"**
   Pairs with line three, EYES ON THE FUTURES. ALL NIGHT. It is the question the beginner is already asking, so the line answers it directly.
