## Five skeptic objections to AI tools, with honest reassurance

**Audience:** Traders who have been burned by hype, and anyone who has asked an AI a market question and gotten a confident wrong answer.

1. **"AI makes up numbers."** Crown can't promise its figures are right, so treat every number it writes as a claim to check against your own source before you act on it.
2. **"It's a black box, I can't see why it says what it says."** Deep-Logic is built for longer reasoning, and the token meter shows the work it spends, so you can judge the answer rather than take it on faith.
3. **"I won't paste my trading data into a company I don't know."** The free tier needs no card, and you should read the privacy terms before sharing anything sensitive, with no account numbers or login details in any prompt.
4. **"It will just tell me what to trade."** Crown is not a trade-call service and nothing it writes is financial advice; the decisions stay with you.
5. **"The free tier is a teaser for a subscription trap."** The free tier gives 10,000 tokens with no card, so you can test it before any payment. Crowned is 49.99 a month for 5 million tokens, a meter that runs out, not unlimited use.

**Opening angle for hook 3:** "Five reasons a sharp trader should doubt an AI tool, and what Crown will and won't claim about each one."

*Not financial advice. Crown is an AI workspace; it does not give trading recommendations.*
