## Five likely reasons cold traffic stops before the click

**1. The viewer can't tell what Korvus is in the first seconds.**
- **Barrier:** Comprehension. A short-form viewer sees a dashboard-style product with no clear label and scrolls past.
- **Testable fix:** Bake an opening overlay line that states the category in plain words, such as "Free 24/7 futures market intelligence terminal." Run it against the current opening on the next batch and compare click rate per 30 views.

**2. The viewer is not the person the product is for.**
- **Barrier:** Audience fit. Much of the cold audience may not trade index futures, so the terminal has no obvious use for them.
- **Testable fix:** Split creatives into two hooks, one aimed at active index futures traders (session timing, watching the open) and one aimed at general market curiosity. Compare clicks by hook. If only one hook converts, the audience is the constraint, not the video.

**3. There is no clear next step.**
- **Barrier:** Call to action. The video ends without telling the viewer what to do, or the link is buried in a bio the viewer never opens.
- **Testable fix:** Add an explicit caption line naming the free tier and where the link is, then compare it against the current caption over the same number of views. Keep the Observer tier as the first ask, since it carries no cost.

**4. The content reads as hype, which the brand's honesty edge is meant to avoid.**
- **Barrier:** Trust. Anything that suggests guaranteed results or "free money" makes a skeptical trader leave, and it works against the brand.
- **Testable fix:** Run one honest framing that describes what the terminal shows (scored news, session context, live levels) with no outcome language, against the current framing. Keep the not-advice disclaimer on any product claim. Judge by clicks, not by reach.

**5. Friction between the click and the signup page.**
- **Barrier:** Landing mismatch. If the page leads with paid pricing or loads slowly on a phone, a viewer who came for a free look leaves.
- **Testable fix:** Open the tagged link on a phone and confirm the Observer path is visible above the fold. Then test whether the free option converts better when it appears first. Korvus prices inject server side, so do not hardcode dollar amounts while testing.

## Measurement gap

- Click data is thin: 2 clicks from 30 visits in 7 days is a small sample, so one result will not separate these reasons.
- Where the viewer drops off (watch time, profile visit, link tap, landing load) is **not instrumented yet**. Filing a request to track the link tap and landing load per video would show which of the five barriers is actually active before tests are run.
