**Three cold audiences for the Observer signup test**

**1. Index futures day traders who miss headlines when they step away from the screen**
*Who needs what:* A free, always-on feed that scores market-moving news for ES and NQ so they know what changed while they were away from the desk.
*Land on:* `/` with the signup call to action leading to `/signup`.
*Why first:* They already have the problem Observer solves, and the no-card free tier removes the friction of trying it.

**2. TradingView users who trade index futures and want session context without building their own levels**
*Who needs what:* A free market intelligence view to check alongside their charts, with the understanding that the LodeStone chart indicator is part of Operator, not Observer.
*Land on:* `/` rather than `/lodestone`. The LodeStone page leads to a paid product, which mismatches a free signup.
*Why second:* Real intent, but the free tier does not include what they most want, so the conversion is softer and the copy must be honest about that.

**3. Traders in overnight and European sessions who want to know what happened while US desks were closed**
*Who needs what:* A way to catch overnight index moves and scored news without staying up for the US open.
*Land on:* `/` with the signup call to action leading to `/signup`.
*Why third:* The need is genuine and fits the 24/7 watch, but this group is less likely to know the product exists, so the reach has to come from where they already gather.
