# Objections to Free AI Market Feeds, Ranked

**Audience:** Beginner and intermediate index futures traders who have been burned by hype.
**Ranking basis:** Most likely to stop a sign-up, from most to least.

1. **"Free means I'm the product, and this is a funnel to sell me a paid plan."**
   Honest answer: Yes, Operator is the paid tier and it is how the company earns. Observer needs no card, and nothing in it expires or locks you into a trial.

2. **"AI feeds make things up or present guesses as facts."**
   Honest answer: The feed reports market information and scored news, with the method and confidence stated on each item. It is not a prediction engine, and users should check any item against their own charts and sources before acting on it.

3. **"It's a signal service with a disclaimer bolted on."**
   Honest answer: Korvus gives no trade calls, entries, exits, or profit claims. The product is information to inform the trader's own decisions, and the not-advice disclaimer stays on every product claim.

**Angle for Herald:** Lead with the objection a skeptic already holds, then answer it in one plain sentence. Show the scored-news format as a real visual so the feed looks like information, not a sales pitch.

**Note:** Conversion by objection is not instrumented yet. Filing a request to tag sign-up source against the objection being addressed would let us measure which answer converts.
