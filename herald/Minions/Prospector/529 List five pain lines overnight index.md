4. **"Every time I set alerts, they fire on noise and miss the one that matters."**
   *Source type:* alert fatigue. Traders describe generic alerts that don't separate signal from noise.

5. **"I'm not chasing a gap at the open because I missed the setup while I slept."**
   *Source type:* the missed-setup regret. Traders describe the decision they can't revisit after the fact.
