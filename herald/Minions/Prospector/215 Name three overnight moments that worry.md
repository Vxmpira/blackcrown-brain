# Prospector: Name three overnight moments that worry index futures traders

Date: 2026-10-27
Task: Name three overnight moments that worry index futures traders
For [[Herald]].

1. **Asia-to-London handoff (roughly 2:00 to 3:00 a.m. ET):** Price can shift direction as European desks open, and traders fear sleeping through a break of the overnight range that sets the tone for the morning.

2. **Overnight economic releases and central bank remarks:** Early data drops, such as international CPI, central bank speeches, or policy statements, can move ES and NQ before the cash open, and traders worry they'll only see the reaction after it's already priced in.

3. **Thin-liquidity spikes and stop runs:** Sudden wicks in low-volume overnight sessions can hit stops or levels in seconds, and traders fear waking up to a fill or a gap they didn't plan for.
