## Three places beginner index futures traders see overnight content

**1. Reddit: r/Daytrading and r/FuturesTrading**
- Why it fits: beginners post "what happened overnight?" questions and read replies from people who already watch the Globex session.
- Convert path: answer the question with substance first, then link Korvus only where a rule-compliant self-promo thread or flair allows it.
- Limits: both subs restrict self-promotion heavily. Lead with education, check each sub's current rules, and do not post links in comments unless the mod policy allows it.

**2. YouTube Shorts and long-form futures education**
- Why it fits: viewers search "ES overnight session" and "what moved the market while I slept." Shorts reach beginners who have not yet picked a tool.
- Convert path: a short that shows the overnight recap, with the Korvus link in the description or pinned comment through the UTM-tagged link.
- Limits: no guaranteed returns or trade calls. Keep the not-advice disclaimer on product claims. Note: this is the only wired publishing platform, so this route is live now.

**3. Eclipse-X Discord, #korvus-industries and #forex-news**
- Why it fits: the Presidential Wire and the daily USD agenda already answer the 3 a.m. question. Members who engage are warm.
- Convert path: invite-based. Eclipse-X is top of funnel, so members cross to the Korvus signup from the channel.
- Limits: community value first, no begging for activity. This is a warm-traffic channel, not cold reach, so it adds to the signup quota only as a conversion step, not new discovery.

**Ranking for cold clicks to free accounts:** YouTube Shorts first (live and wired), Reddit second (high intent, tight rules), Eclipse-X third (warm, but mostly converts people who already found you).
