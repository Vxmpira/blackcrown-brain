### 1. Tool stack consolidation (lead for the annual push)
- **Who it reaches:** Traders already paying for charts, news, or indicators who feel the cost of running several tools.
- **What they need:** One place that covers the chart and the market wire without juggling subscriptions.
- **Honest path:** Operator includes the LodeStone TradingView indicator. LodeStone standalone is listed at 19.99 a month on TradingView. Operator annual is 79.99 a year, against 9.99 a month billed monthly. The comparison is factual and checkable on the pricing section.
- **Sample hook:** "Charts, news, and levels in one terminal. Operator annual includes the LodeStone TradingView indicator. Not financial advice."
- **Why it ranks first:** It is the only angle that makes the annual price the obvious choice, so it moves people from click straight to paid.

### 2. Overnight blind spot (top of funnel)
- **Who it reaches:** Index futures traders who are offline during Asia and London hours and miss what moved.
- **What they need:** Knowing what hit the wire while they were away.
- **Honest path:** Korvus runs 24/7 and scores the wire with impact, direction, and confidence. The Observer tier is free, so the honest CTA is signup, with Operator annual offered once they want the full read and the indicator.
- **Sample hook:** "The market did not stop while you slept. Korvus watches the wire around the clock. Not financial advice."
- **Why it ranks second:** Strongest pain and widest reach, but it converts to the free tier first, so annual is a later step. Good for the Observer quota and a feeder into the annual offer.

### 3. Woke up to a gap
- **Who it reaches:** Traders who have been caught by an unexplained open.
- **What they need:** Context on why the open moved, without predictions.
- **Honest path:** Holds only if framed as scored context, never as a call on direction. Any "what this means for your trade" wording is out of bounds.
- **Sample hook:** "Gapped at the open? See what the wire scored overnight. Context, not calls. Not financial advice."
- **Why it ranks third:** Good visit and click hook, but the honesty risk is the highest and it sells the annual plan the least.
