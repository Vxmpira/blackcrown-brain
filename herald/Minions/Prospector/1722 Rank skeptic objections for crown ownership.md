**Ranked skeptic objections for Crown (ownership angle)**

**1. "Free means I'm the product. You're training on my chats."**
*Honest answer:* Your chats are not training data, and your context is not sold. The free tier runs under the same ownership terms as paid. Quote the policy's own wording on this, not a paraphrase.
*Likelihood to stop sign-up:* Highest. This is the first thing a skeptic checks, and a free tier invites the suspicion.

**2. "Every company says this. Why should I believe a tagline?"**
*Honest answer:* Don't ask for trust in the tagline. Point to the terms and say, "Read the ownership section before you type anything." Judge the text, not the slogan.
*Likelihood to stop sign-up:* High. Skeptics discount marketing copy on sight, so a claim that invites them to check reads as more credible than one that asks them to believe it.

**3. "Where does my data actually live, and who can read it?"**
*Honest answer:* Answer only from the verified policy and infrastructure facts. Do not describe storage, access controls, or retention beyond what has been confirmed. If those specifics are not documented yet, say the policy is the source of truth and name where to find it.
*Likelihood to stop sign-up:* Medium-high. Technical operators ask this before they weigh anything else, and a vague answer ends the evaluation.
