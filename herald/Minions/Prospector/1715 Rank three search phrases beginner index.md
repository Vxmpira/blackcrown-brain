**Prospector: beginner index futures search phrases**

Volume: not instrumented. This ranking is judgment from how beginners describe their problem, not keyword data.

**1. "why is ES futures moving overnight"**
The trader went to bed flat or sized down, woke up to a different price, and has no idea what happened while they slept. The question is about missing information, which is the exact gap the all seeing watcher angle answers.

**2. "what moves S&P futures / what moves ES"**
They know price moves but not what drives it. They have been told to watch economic data, Fed headlines, and earnings, and they cannot tell which of those matter today. The problem is a missing filter, not a missing feed.

**3. "too many tabs for futures news"**
They have a dozen sources open (news sites, Twitter, economic calendars, charts) and feel behind on all of them. The phrase is a complaint more than a query, so it fits best as a hook or caption, not a search term to bid on.

Closing line: Lead with phrase 1 for the overnight angle, use phrase 2 as the broader explainer hook, and treat phrase 3 as copy language rather than a targeted search.
