## Why the Leader

- **It shows a scene, not a property.** "3 a.m." puts the viewer in a moment they recognize. Hook 4 describes coverage in the abstract, which is easier to scroll past.
- **It carries the watcher angle.** The all seeing watcher, working while the trader sleeps, is the creative direction that already lands for Korvus. The leader says it in one line.
- **It matches the house motto.** "Your edge while the world sleeps" is the Korvus line, so the cold hook and the brand speak with one voice.
- **It is already locked.** Changing the hook on a cold push gives up a clean comparison window for no proven gain.

## Hook 4: Keep for Later

Hook 4 is the right backup. Use it as the challenger in a later test, or as a caption or overlay line where a coverage statement fits better than a scene. Do not drop it.
