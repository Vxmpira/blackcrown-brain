# Three hypotheses: 40 visits, 2 clicks

**Goal:** Cold visitors on the Korvus landing click through to signup (Observer) or checkout (Operator). Target is 2 clicks per 40 visits, a 5% click rate. This moves the first paying customer rung and the 50-signup quota.

**Measurement:** Count outbound Korvus clicks by UTM source. UTM tagging is live, so each variant's clicks are separable. Visit denominators per variant must be confirmed before reading results.

---

### H1: The watcher proof

- **Who needs what:** Index futures traders who have been burned by hype. They need to see what the terminal actually does before they give it an email or a card.
- **Single change:** Add one Illustration-labeled sample of the live wire feed (scored items with impact, direction, and confidence) directly under the hero, with the Observer CTA beneath it.
- **Number to watch:** Observer signup clicks per 40 visits. Baseline first, then target 2.

### H2: Annual math for people who already pay for tools

- **Who needs what:** Active traders who already subscribe to charting or data tools. They need the Operator price in context, not a bare monthly figure.
- **Single change:** Make the Operator annual card the primary button in the pricing section, with the computed savings label visible on the card. Keep Observer as a secondary link. Prices stay injected from Stripe; nothing hardcoded.
- **Number to watch:** Operator checkout clicks per 40 visits. Watch Observer clicks too, so a drop there does not get mistaken for a win.

### H3: Honesty near the decision

- **Who needs what:** Skeptics who discount anything that sounds like a promise. They need a plain statement of what Korvus does and does not do before they commit.
- **Single change:** Place a short block directly above the final CTA: what the wire scores mean, that Korvus is market intelligence and not trading advice, and that nothing here promises results. One paragraph, no new claims.
- **Number to watch:** Total clicks per 40 visits, with Operator and Observer split by UTM.
