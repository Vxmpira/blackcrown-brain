**Challenger 1: The gap question**
> You can't watch the tape from 11 p.m. to 6 a.m. Korvus keeps the overnight feed in view so you can see what happened while you were away.

- **Angle:** Names the real problem (the hours you're offline) without pretending the market stops or that you're missing out.
- **Channel:** YouTube Shorts and search, with Bard's overlay carrying the line.
- **Why it converts:** Traders recognize the gap in their own routine, so the pitch lands as a fix to a known problem.
- **Honesty note:** Korvus shows overnight information. It does not trade for you or tell you what to do with it.

**Challenger 2: The tool, not the trader**
> Korvus is a 24/7 index futures terminal. It reads the overnight market so you can make your own calls at the open.

- **Angle:** Positions Korvus as a reading tool that leaves the decision with the trader, which fits the brand's no guru stance.
- **Channel:** Eclipse-X referral posts in #korvus-industries, then paid search for "overnight futures terminal."
- **Why it converts:** Skeptical traders skip anything that sounds like a signal service. Stating the division of labor up front builds trust before the pitch.
- **Honesty note:** "Reads the overnight market" describes coverage, not completeness. Keep the not-advice disclaimer on the ad.

**Challenger 3: The morning review**
> Overnight index futures moved while you slept. Korvus logged it, so you can review the session before the bell.

- **Angle:** Turns the overnight gap into a morning routine. The product is the recap, not a promise about what you'll catch.
- **Channel:** YouTube Shorts timed to pre-market hours, with the review framing as the overlay.
- **Why it converts:** Morning review is a habit traders already have. Offering a faster way to do it gives a concrete reason to start.
- **Honesty note:** This only works if Korvus actually logs overnight sessions for the user. Confirm that before this runs, because the hook makes that promise.

**Recommendation:** Test Challenger 1 against the leader first. It keeps the leader's structure and drops the "watches" claim. Challenger 3 is the strongest if the logging feature is confirmed.

**Ships to:** Bard for video overlays, Herald for captions. No proposal needed for copy-only tests. Any landing page change routes through Regent.
