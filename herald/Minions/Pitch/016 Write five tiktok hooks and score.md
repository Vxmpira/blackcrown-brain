Date: 2026-10-26
Task: Write five TikTok hooks and score each one
For [[Herald]].

## Five TikTok Hooks: Korvus Observer, 24/7 Watcher Angle

| # | Hook | Words | Clarity | Honesty | Cold fit |
|---|------|-------|---------|---------|----------|
| 1 | While you sleep, something is still watching the index futures. | 10 | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| 2 | Index futures keep moving at 3 a.m. Your watcher should too. | 11 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 3 | Stop refreshing headlines at midnight. A watcher can scan them. | 10 | 5 | 5 | 4 |
| 4 | One watcher on the index futures wire, day and night. | 10 | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| 5 | Curious what happens to index futures while you sleep? Start free. | 11 | 3 | 5 | 3 |

**Scoring notes**
- **Hook 2** uses "should," which reads as a mild obligation. Honest, but it leans slightly toward pressure.
- **Hook 5** never names Korvus, so a cold viewer may not know what to start. The caption and overlay must name it.
- **Hook 1** is clear on the watching idea but "something" stays vague until the video shows the product.

**Best hook: #3**

Plain text: Stop refreshing headlines at midnight. A watcher can scan them.

Why: It scores highest on clarity and honesty, and it fits a cold beginner's real pain (late night news checking) without promising any outcome. It states the 24/7 watcher angle in the first line, and it makes no return claims or advice. Pair it with a caption that names Korvus and carries the not-advice disclaimer, and an overlay that makes the Observer free tier clear.
