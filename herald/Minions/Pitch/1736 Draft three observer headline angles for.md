**Observer click test: three headline angles**

*Prospector's objection ranking is not in the vault yet. These angles lead with the two facts a skeptic checks first: cost and coverage. Re-cut them once the ranking files.*

---

**1. Coverage angle**
- **Hook:** "The futures market doesn't close. Korvus watches it while you sleep."
- **Claim:** Korvus monitors the market around the clock.
- **Channel:** YouTube Shorts and TikTok, using the all-seeing watcher visual with a short, sparse overlay.
- **Why it converts:** It answers "why would I need this?" in one line and matches the watcher creative that already fits the brand.

**2. Free, no-card angle**
- **Hook:** "See what Korvus shows you before you pay a cent. No card, no trial clock."
- **Claim:** The Observer tier is free and requires no payment method.
- **Channel:** X, posted direct from the brand account, written like a trader talking to a trader.
- **Why it converts:** Skeptics will not buy a terminal they have not seen. Removing the card removes the cheapest reason to hesitate.

**3. Scored-news angle**
- **Hook:** "Every headline scored for impact and direction, in one terminal."
- **Claim:** Korvus scores incoming news for impact, direction, and confidence, with a written Korvus read.
- **Channel:** Futures trading communities and the Eclipse-X #korvus-industries channel, with a sample Presidential Wire post as the proof.
- **Why it converts:** It shows the product working instead of describing it, and the score is a read on the news, not a forecast.

---

Every headline above needs this line in the body or caption: **Korvus is market information, not financial advice. Trading involves risk.**
