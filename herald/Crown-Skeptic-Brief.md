# Crown Skeptic Style Brief, Part One

Assembled by Herald. Owner ruling 10-28 governs all copy: Crown is sold on ownership, not memory.

## Audience
Traders and operators who have tried general AI tools and doubt privacy and value claims. They judge by evidence, not adjectives. They will test the free tier before they believe anything.

## Core angle (always the lead)
Ownership. Your context stays yours. Never sold, never trained on, your chats are nobody's training data.

## Supporting angles, one per post, never stacked
- Deep logic on purpose: the heavy rooms draw more credit because they think harder.
- Flat 49.99 a month carrying $100 of room credit, no surprise bills.
- Four private rooms: Unfiltered, Deep-Logic, Visual Analysis, Creativity.
- Free tier, no card: judge the output yourself.

## Skeptic tone rules
- State the claim, show the mechanism, invite the test.
- No superlatives, no unlimited, no token numbers, no memory-gap claim, no naming the underlying software.
- Usage always presented as money, never raw counts.
- No em dashes. No emoji in outbound copy.

## Hook frames for Copy to finish (one claim each)
1. You read the privacy page. Here is what it means in practice.
2. Most AI tools learn from what you type. Crown does not learn from yours.
3. Try the free tier with no card and grade the answer yourself.
4. A flat 49.99 a month with $100 of room credit. Here is how the meter works.
5. The heavy rooms cost more credit because they think harder. That is the trade.

## Measurement
- Skeptic-angle click split: not instrumented yet.
- Required before any click test: human-only denominator from Corvid.
- The annual X click test stays staged. Go or hold call is at 18:00 for the human gate.
