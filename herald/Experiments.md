# Herald Experiments

## Live: click-stage hook test (opened 2026-10-28)

**Who needs what:** beginner index futures traders on cold traffic who want the overnight picture without juggling tabs. Korvus is the honest match: one screen, directional read, free account.

**Stage moved:** visits to clicks on the Korvus path (then clicks to signups).

**Creative under test:** the leader cold hook 'The market moves at 3 a.m. Korvus watches index futures while you sleep.' against the alternate 'Index futures don't stop at 3 a.m. Korvus doesn't either.' Both are scored 12 on the fixed rubric. Both are text only, no promise of profit, and both carry the not-advice line where a product claim appears.

**Number to watch:** clicks per 100 visits, tracked through the UTM rows from the existing daily per-click job. Baseline from the last 7 days: 2 clicks over 33 visits, about 6 per 100. Not instrumented per hook yet; the per-click UTM job is the instrument, and the hook must be tagged in the link.

**Decision rule:** a hook that lifts clicks per 100 visits clearly above baseline across at least 60 visits keeps the lead. If neither hook clears baseline, the leak is the landing page, not the hook, and the next experiment moves to the landing.

**Verdict due:** 2026-11-01, filed to Playbook.md.
