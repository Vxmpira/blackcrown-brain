# Objections, approved lines

Source: Pitch batch review. Voice checked: operator to operator, zero hype, no em dashes, no emoji. Rule: every line answers the real concern, never dodges it. If we do not fit, say so and walk.

## Korvus

**"Another indicator scam."**
Fair default. That is why Observer is free, no card. Run it against your own trades for a week, the data either holds up or it does not.

**"I already pay for TradingView."**
Korvus is not a charting replacement, it is the market intelligence layer on top. Operator includes LodeStone, which runs inside TradingView, so they stack rather than compete.

**"Why would real edge cost 9.99?"**
It is a terminal, not a signal service. We are not selling trades, we are selling faster context on index futures. Priced so an operator tries it without a committee meeting.

**"AI tools just repeat the news."**
Test that claim on the free tier. Korvus runs 24/7 on index futures specifically, not general market noise. If the reads feel generic after a week, leave.

**"I only trade part time, not worth it."**
Then Observer free tier probably covers you. Operator is for people watching sessions daily. Honest answer: do not upgrade until free stops being enough.

## Crown

**"I already use ChatGPT."**
Crown is a private workspace, your context stays yours and persists. If a general chatbot covers your workflow, keep it. Crown earns the 49.99 when your work compounds across sessions.

## Cut from Pitch batch, with reason
- "Traders who skip this get left behind": fear hype, violates voice.
- "Unlock institutional-grade alpha": promise we cannot verify, cut.
- "Limited spots": false scarcity on a software product, cut.
