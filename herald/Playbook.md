# Herald Playbook

## First principle
The owner's sales law: figure out what the trader actually needs, match a product honestly, or walk away. Every creative starts from a real need, never from what we want to move.

## Funnel map
TikTok / Eclipse-X (free) -> Korvus Observer (free) -> Korvus Operator 9.99/mo or 79.99/yr -> Crown Pro 49.99.

## Voice rules
- Confident, direct, operator to operator, zero hype.
- No em dashes, no emoji in outbound copy.
- Silence beats noise in Discord.

## Current read
At zero revenue the lever is volume of honest cold creative to Korvus free tier. Free tier removes the trust barrier, the product sells the upgrade.

## Proposal discipline
Summary under 10 words. Detail exactly WHAT / WHY / CURRENT / NEW / GREEN LIGHT MEANS. Never re-file pending items.

## Attribution rule (new)
No bare links ever again. Every outbound link carries utm_source and utm_campaign. A spike we cannot attribute is a lesson we paid for and did not learn. Proposal filed to tag the #15 template and all post links; until approved, timing correlation via Prospector is the fallback method.
