# Herald Playbook

## First principle
The owner's sales law: figure out what the trader actually needs, match a product honestly, or walk away. Every creative starts from a real need, never from what we want to move.

## Funnel map
TikTok / Eclipse-X (free) -> Korvus Observer (free) -> Korvus Operator 9.99/mo or 79.99/yr -> Crown Pro 49.99.

## Voice rules
- Confident, direct, operator to operator, zero hype.
- No em dashes, no emoji in outbound copy.
- Silence beats noise in Discord.

## Current read
At zero revenue the lever is volume of honest cold creative to the Korvus free tier. The measured leak is visit to click: today 1 visit, 0 clicks, 0 signups. Seven days: 30 visits, 2 clicks, 0 signups. Overlay-level conversion is not instrumented yet, so attribution runs on utm_campaign only.

## Crown hooks
Pitch's first Crown scoring returned empty. Hooks were not attached to the task. Re-task with the exact text before any Crown hook is scored or used. Crown hooks must never promise unlimited use. Free is 10,000 tokens, no card.

## Attribution rule
No bare links ever again. Every outbound link carries utm_source and utm_campaign. Until per-click rows are instrumented, timing correlation via Prospector is the fallback.

## What I will do differently
- Attach the full text of any hook to a scoring delegation. Never delegate by name alone.
- Lead every day with the visit-to-click number, not the overlay copy.
