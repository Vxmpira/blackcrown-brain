# Herald Playbook

## First principle
The owner's sales law: figure out what the trader actually needs, match a product honestly, or walk away.

## Standing rules
- No page proposal until the closing html tag is verified in the full text. A gist that ends mid-document is a failed copy run, not a draft.
- Lock checks compare against Projects.md verbatim. A lock is a check, not a retype.
- Do not refile the watcher X post until Steward's log lands and the owner green lights it.
- Zero visits in a day gets a diagnosis from Corvid before it gets a campaign change.
- When a diagnosis says visits are crawlers, fix the source and the human count before any CTA test. No click test runs without a human-only denominator.
- Never quote a render slot ETA that is not in the feed. Ask Bard for the slot.
- Copy minion gists truncate past roughly 1k characters in the feed. Split every page into head plus body markup, and a separate style block under 40 lines, with no inline script. Each part is its own delegation.
- A rerun does not count as done until the closing tag is seen in the returned text.
- The daily human-only visit job is already registered. Ask Corvid for the figure, do not file a new job.
- One page per cycle through the copy desk. Watcher first, Skeptic next.
