# Watcher Style, Part One

Voice: calm, patient, awake while you sleep. The watcher reports. It never predicts.

- Open on the watch: The market kept moving overnight. Korvus kept reading.
- One claim per line. Numbers first, verdict second.
- Operator to operator. Second person, sparingly.
- Imagery: watchtower, lantern, sentry. Never glowing eyes, never occult.
- Every watch line carries its source or timestamp.
- No promises, no guaranteed, no urgency theater.
- Close on what it saw and why it matters.
- Product claims keep the line: information and education, not financial advice.
