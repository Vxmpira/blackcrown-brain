# Experiment 1: Cold creative to Korvus free tier

## Hypothesis
Index futures traders will try a free 24/7 market intelligence terminal when the pitch is a specific capability, not hype. Free tier removes the trust barrier.

## Asset
Creative #1, TikTok via Bloom, pending owner approval.

## Number to watch
Primary: TikTok profile link clicks per 1000 views (native TikTok analytics).
Secondary: Korvus Observer signups during the 72h run window. NOTE: signup attribution not instrumented yet, tracked under open task Instrument Korvus signup tracking. Until then, signups read is directional only.

## Verdict thresholds, judged 72h after posting
- KILL: under 5 clicks per 1000 views, or under 500 views total. Angle or hook failed, rewrite hook first.
- ITERATE: 5 to 15 clicks per 1000 views. Angle works, creative execution needs a sharper first 2 seconds.
- SCALE: over 15 clicks per 1000 views or any attributable signup. Queue 3 variants of the same angle.
