## Operator annual 7-day test spec (for Steward gate, 2026-10-30)

**Hypothesis.** A trader who already watches the market and has seen the Observer tier responds to annual-first framing with the savings label, because one annual payment removes the monthly comparison.

**Who needs it.** An index futures trader who wants the full Operator terminal and the LodeStone indicator without a monthly charge running.

**Creative.** Rotate the three LOCKED annual-first overlay lines from Projects.md across days 1 to 7, one line per day, verbatim:
- Operator annual: 79.99 a year. Save 39.89.
- Annual Operator. LodeStone included.
- One payment a year. Full Operator access.

Each post links to korvus.industries with UTM tagging on, which is standing infrastructure. Copy stays free of em dashes and emoji. Every post carries the not-advice line when it makes a product claim.

**Measures.**
- X impressions and link clicks per day, by line.
- Korvus signups per day, from the UTM feed.
- Operator annual purchases, read by the owner from Stripe. Agents do not touch billing. Per-post purchase attribution is not instrumented yet; the flight scorecard job carries the readout.
- Denominator for click rate: human-only visits, from the crawler-filtered count. Not instrumented as a clean feed yet.

**Win.** Click rate at least 1.5x the flight baseline and at least 3 Korvus signups inside 7 days, the same rule as the click test.

**Kill.** Click rate under 1.2x the baseline at 300 human visits, or zero signups after 7 days. Any kill is filed to Playbook.md the same day.

**Sample honesty.** At the current pace of 59 visits per week, 300 human visits is about five weeks away. A 7-day result is directional only and must be labeled not decisive unless the floor is met. Traffic is the binding constraint, and the fix is distribution volume.

**Render dependency.** The annual render slot is unconfirmed and the feed carries no ETA. No slot date is quoted. If no render lands, the post ships as text only on the same schedule.

**Gate path.** Steward gate, then Regent review, then owner green light in boardroom. Nothing in this flight publishes without that path.
