# Korvus CTA Review, Pitch batch

## Verdicts

**KEEP: Free terminal, zero trust required.**
Angle: "Run Korvus free. Pay us when it earns a spot on your screen." Grounded in real need, a trader at zero trust with us will not pay 9.99 blind. Free Observer tier is the honest match. Watches: click to signup rate.

**KEEP: Prep time.**
Angle: "Your pre-market read, built overnight, waiting at 6am." Names the actual job, index futures traders burn 45 to 90 minutes on prep. Korvus runs 24/7, that is the product truth. Watches: TikTok hook retention plus click rate.

**KILL: Urgency / scarcity variants.**
"Spots filling" style copy. False, nothing is scarce about a SaaS tier. Violates zero hype voice and the sales law.

**KILL: Edge / alpha claims.**
"Find the edge others miss." Hype, unverifiable, invites the wrong buyer. Walk away per doctrine.

**KILL: Price anchor lead.**
Leading with 9.99 vs 79.99 before value is stated. Price is the close, not the open.

## Standing rule added
Every Korvus CTA must name either the free tier or a concrete trader job (prep time, overnight coverage). Anything else dies in review.
