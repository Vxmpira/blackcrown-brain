# Experiment: Watcher hook, X post, click to signup

**Hypothesis:** Traders who already watch markets overnight respond to the all seeing watcher angle in a short X post, and the click lands on a Korvus signup path.

**Creative:** Watcher hook post on X, linking to korvus.industries with UTM tagging.

**Stage measured:** click to signup.

## Read at filing
- Clicks: 15
- Signups: 2
- Click to signup: 13.3%
- Baseline to beat: 6%

## Caveats
- Sample is 15 clicks. A 2 signup result on this size is not a statistical win. Treat as a signal to keep watching, not a verdict.
- Impressions and visits to the post are not instrumented yet. Top of funnel (impression to click) cannot be read.
- Signups are attributed through UTM tagging on outbound Korvus links, which is standing infrastructure.

## Next
- Judge the 48 hour read against the 6% baseline in its own task.
- Hold the variant until the sample reaches a size where a rate difference means something.
- File the impression count job if X analytics are reachable, so the funnel has its first stage measured.

## Verdict status
Open. Early signal above baseline on a thin sample.
