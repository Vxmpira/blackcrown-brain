# Operator Annual 7-Day Flight

Window: 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-06. Stage moved: visits to signups to paid (Operator annual).

## Hypothesis
Traders already running a chart or news tool switch to one annual payment when the saving is stated in dollars and LodeStone is named as included.

## Buyer
Index futures traders who already pay for tools and want fewer monthly charges.

## Variants (one live at a time, rotated by day)
- Control: current Korvus landing, annual listed first, no overlay.
- A, Oct 30 to Nov 1: 'Operator annual: 79.99 a year. Save 39.89.'
- B, Nov 2 to Nov 4: 'Annual Operator. LodeStone included.'
- C, Nov 5 to Nov 6: 'One payment a year. Full Operator access.'

The save figure is 9.99 times 12 (119.88) minus 79.99, which equals 39.89. Overlays are locked by Bard, confirmed 2026-10-27.

## Numbers to watch
- Denominator: human-only visits to korvus.industries per variant (crawlers excluded). Source: the registered human-only daily job, via Corvid.
- Click rate per variant against control.
- Korvus signups and Operator annual purchases per variant.

## Honest sample math
Traffic is about 8 visits per day. Seven days yields roughly 55 human visits total, far below the 300 per variant minimum. Verdict rule: if the sample is short, file the flight as directional with the raw counts and no win or kill call. Do not declare a winner on this sample.
