# Watcher Style Block

Owner: Herald. Scope: Korvus creative and page assembly for the all-seeing watcher angle.

## The promise in one line
Korvus watches the index futures market around the clock so the trader can sleep and still wake up informed.

## Who this speaks to
Index futures traders who already run a session and feel the cost of missing an overnight move. Operator to operator. Never a guru pedestal.

## Voice
- Calm, direct, specific. One claim per line.
- Watcher language is observational: watching, logging, scoring, flagging. Never predicting, never promising profit, never trade calls.
- No hype words, no countdowns, no fear selling.
- No emoji in outbound copy. No em dashes anywhere.
- Include the not-advice line on any product claim.

## Visual language
- Dark ground with soft radial accent glows (pink and violet accents), a faint 56px grid at about 1.5 percent white, and an edge vignette. Never flat black, no background images, nothing animated.
- Accent color: #ff2d8f on crown linework and key highlights.
- Crown SVG in every page header, beside the site name: <svg viewBox="0 0 36 33" fill="none" stroke="#ff2d8f" stroke-width="2.4" stroke-linejoin="round" stroke-linecap="round"><path d="M4 24 L4 9 L12 16 L18 5 L24 16 L32 9 L32 24 Z"/><path d="M8 28 L28 28"/></svg>
- The watcher motif: a single eye or a scanning line rendered as an inline svg figure, or a feature chip row reading Overnight, Scored, Logged. Plain text walls are rejected.
- Any data panel or chart is marked Illustration unless it is live.

## Layout law
- Container about 1060px centered with side padding.
- Headings sized with clamp() so they grow on desktop.
- Prose never wider than about 75 characters a line.
- Tables, figures and data panels span the full container.
- Multi-column grids collapse under 700px.

## Video (Bard owns video)
- Generated footage carries no words, letters, numbers, logos or typography.
- Any on-screen words come only through the overlay field, exact literal lines.
- Clip length follows the owner ruling: 15 seconds.

## Copy guardrails
- Korvus only. Crown memory claims are banned; Crown copy follows the ownership angle, never the memory-gap angle.
- Korvus pricing is never hardcoded in copy; it injects server side. Operator is 9.99 a month or 79.99 a year, annual listed first.
- LodeStone is included with Operator. Standalone is 19.99 a month on TradingView. Never call that price a mistake.
