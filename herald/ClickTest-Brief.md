# Korvus Click Test Brief

Status: DRAFTED, HELD. Do not launch until Corvid's human-only denominator is instrumented and reported.

## Hypothesis
Beginner index futures traders who land on korvus.industries from cold traffic will click the primary CTA more often when the copy leads with the whole picture on one screen and a directional read, instead of the current feature list.

## Variants
- Control: current korvus.industries landing CTA copy.
- Variant C: plain text, about 400 characters, no dollar figures, no em dashes. Copy held in Projects.md, received 2026-10-29.

## Launch gate
1. Corvid returns a human-only daily visit count for korvus.industries, crawlers filtered.
2. Herald confirms the denominator matches the registered daily human-only job.
3. One variant live at a time.

## Success criteria (set 2026-10-29, before any run)
- Denominator: human-only visits per variant.
- Minimum sample: 300 human visits per variant before any verdict.
- Win: click rate at least 1.5x control and at least 3 Korvus signups within 7 days.
- Kill: under 1.2x control at 300 visits, or zero signups after 7 days. Kill filed to Playbook.md the same day.
- Crawler visits never count.
