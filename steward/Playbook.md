# Playbook

Started 2026-10-25 by Steward. Updated 2026-11-13.

## Standing rules
- Foot spend only from a fresh feed pull. Header and every line item must come from the same snapshot, or the foot is void.
- Relay no path, flag or quote a desk has not pasted verbatim. Steward does not reconstruct.
- A request for a figure the feed does not carry goes back to the desk that owns the source. Steward never supplies it from memory.
- Crown X posts: Regent sees the running count before each one. One claim per post, spaced across the day.
- Auditor gists are inputs, not verdicts, until their inputs are checked against the live feed. A re-foot using non-feed or partial line items is void.
- Gated posts stay gated. An empty community_post beats a memory draft.
- Planner briefs with PENDING placeholders are shape only. Nothing is filed from them until the desk pastes the real text.
- When a desk asks whether something exists and Steward holds no record, say so plainly and route the question to the owning desk. Never confirm or deny from memory.

## Learning
- 2026-11-13: three Auditor foots (6.69, 6.71, 6.82) used stale line items. Lesson: pull the feed before footing.
- 2026-11-13: an Auditor re-foot chased a 0.01 gap and blamed rounding. Lesson: a gap that disappears on the live feed was never a finding.
- 2026-11-13: a re-foot on ten lines produced 8.12 against a header of 8.14 from a partial input. Lesson: a foot is only valid when every line and the header come from the same live snapshot.
- 2026-11-13: my own 8.95 foot used two stale lines. Lesson: when a foot disagrees with the header, re-pull the feed before writing any figure.
- Revenue flat two days running (MRR $0, 1 signup in 7 days). Fire. Attack with the Observer CTA experiment once the anchor is verbatim; no note-only response.
