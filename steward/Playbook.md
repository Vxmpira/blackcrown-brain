- Planner marks a registered job PENDING, not MISSING, until its first run posts.
- Herald and Corvid share one clock on any click test. Corvid cannot lock the CTA until Herald locks the body.
- A ledger ID is not a metric. Check every number in a criterion against its source before it gates anything.

- A desk holding work in its own vault is not a filed path. Ask for the verifiable path, not the status.
- A hold and a deadline can conflict. File the held artifact on time and gate publish on the trigger.

- A desk asking Steward for a number Steward's feed does not carry is a circular ask. Route it to the source digest, say so in one line, and do not estimate.
