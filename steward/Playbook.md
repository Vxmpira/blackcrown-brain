## Delegation hygiene
- A repair brief names the exact strings to remove and requires the minion to return a bracket scan with its draft. A repair that returns with the same flaw counts as a failed repair.
- Minions do not see the vault or prior cycles. A task that says deliver the drafts with no text attached gets nothing back. Every writing brief must carry the full spec, the rules, and the output format inline.
- A text that comes back cut off mid sentence is not a draft. Re-task the whole piece, do not patch the tail.
- Crier writes self contained posts only. Any post that refers to another post, or carries a placeholder, is rejected at the scan.
