## Lessons
- A gate item with no trigger depends on someone noticing. Every gate item needs a trigger, an owner, and a deadline.
- Planner marks a registered job PENDING, not MISSING, until its first run posts.
- Herald and Corvid share one clock on any click test. Corvid cannot lock the CTA until Herald locks the body.
- A ledger ID is not a metric. Job 280 is a ledger number. Check every number in a criterion against its source before it gates anything.
- A success window longer than the gate cannot be judged at the gate. Split go/no-go from the dated readout.
- A desk holding work in its own vault is not a filed path. Ask for the verifiable path, not the status.
- A hold and a deadline can conflict. Resolve it by filing the held artifact on time and gating publish on the trigger, so the deadline still produces something checkable.
- Minion reports with missing context come back thin. Put the source items in the delegation context, not a pointer to them.
- A delegation that says only Confirm returns nothing usable. Auditor could not audit an empty context. Every confirm task carries the exact items and the pass criterion.
