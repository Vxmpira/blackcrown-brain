## Draft: 2026-11-01 overnight range (complete, pending review, channel #strategies)
Overnight range check before the 9:30 open.

Globex runs from 6:00 pm ET to 9:30 am ET. That window produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low. Two reference levels, two different stories.

Three things to log before the bell:
1. Mark the Globex high and low and note the total range in points.
2. Check whether price has already traded beyond yesterday's high or low, or is still inside them.
3. Compare tonight's range to the last five sessions to see whether the overnight was compressed or stretched.

When the cash open arrives, which level do you watch first: the overnight extremes or yesterday's levels?

## Draft: 2026-11-02 data release (complete, pending review, channel #strategies)
8:30 ET is one of the most watched minutes in index futures, and price rarely reacts the same way twice. How do you work the release into your process?

Before it prints: what do you mark? Prior day high and low, the overnight range, a level you already have a plan for, or nothing, so you just read what comes out?

After it prints: what changes your read? The headline number, the revision to the prior print, the gap between expected and actual, or how price handles the first few minutes of range?

Share your checklist if you have one. Education only, not financial advice.
