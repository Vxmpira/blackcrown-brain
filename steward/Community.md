## Nov 1 draft, pending gate
A journaling habit that teaches more than any indicator setting: when price tests one level, log the level, the timeframe, what you expected, and what would invalidate it, all before you check the result. Then add the outcome in one plain line. Win or loss, the entry measures whether your read was clear, not whether the market agreed. Thirty entries show which levels you actually read well. LodeStone can mark the levels on your chart. The log stays yours. Not financial advice.

## Oct 31 draft, PASS on full text
Before the open, build a quick habit: mark the overnight high and low, then compare them against yesterday's high, low, and close so you can judge it yourself. Is the overnight range sitting inside the prior day's, or has it already pushed past one of those levels? That relationship can frame how the session starts. LodeStone plots the session and prior day levels on your chart, so the check takes seconds. What does your overnight range look like against yesterday's levels this morning?
