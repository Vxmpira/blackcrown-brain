## 11-04 filed text (verbatim, PASSED)
Session levels are one of the cheapest context tools on an index futures chart. Before the cash open, mark the prior day high and low, the overnight high and low, and the prior week high and low. Then watch how price behaves when it reaches them: does it stall, sweep and reverse, or accept through? The levels don't predict anything by themselves. They give you a shared reference for what the market did, so your reads stay consistent day to day. Log what you saw each session. Education only.

Trade review habit: log every trade the same way, win or lose. Record the setup you saw before entry, the level it referenced, your planned stop and target, the actual exit, and one line on whether you followed your own rules. The journal gives you data you can actually measure. Education only.
