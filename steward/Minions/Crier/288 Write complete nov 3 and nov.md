**Nov 3 post**

Pre-open map for index futures: four levels to mark before the bell.

1. Prior day high
2. Prior day low
3. Overnight high and low, the range built while the cash session was closed
4. Prior week high or low, whichever sits closest to current price

Mark them before the open, not during it. Once price is already moving, it is easy to draw levels that fit the move instead of levels that were there before it.

Treat these as reference points, not signals. The value of marking them early is having a clear map to check against as price reaches each level, and logging how price reacts in your journal.

This is education, not trading advice. No level guarantees a reaction.
