Pre-session reference levels: prior day and prior week highs and lows

Before the open, mark four levels on your index futures chart:
Prior day high
Prior day low
Prior week high
Prior week low

Why they matter: these are widely watched reference points. Price returning to them can produce reactions, and a clean break can signal a new range forming. Many traders check whether overnight trade has already taken one of them before the cash open.

Prep checklist:
1. Note which levels sit closest to current price.
2. Note whether overnight trade has swept any of them.
3. Decide in advance what a reaction and a break would look like on your timeframe.

These are reference points, not predictions. Levels get tested, held, and broken, and no single level is a guarantee.

Educational only, not financial advice. Trade your own plan.
