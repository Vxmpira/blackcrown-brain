**Session Levels Primer for Index Futures**

Before the open, many traders mark a few reference prices and watch how price reacts when it reaches them. Common ones include:

- Prior day high and low
- Prior session highs and lows (Asia, London, New York)
- Prior week high and low
- The session open

A level is a price where past activity may have left resting orders, stops, or interest. Traders often watch whether price accepts above or below a level or rejects it. Context matters more than any single line.

Write your levels down before the session starts so your read is not built after the fact.

LodeStone can plot prior day and session levels with a timezone setting so the lines match your market hours. Educational tool, not a signal. Past behavior of a level does not guarantee how it will behave next time.

Educational only, not financial advice.
