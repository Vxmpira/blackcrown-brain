**Reading the overnight range on ES and NQ**

The overnight session sets the first footprint before the cash open. A simple way to read it:

1. Mark the high and low from the 6:00 PM ET globex open through 9:30 AM ET.
2. Check whether price is trading near the middle of that range or pressing one edge.
3. Compare NQ's range width to ES. A wide NQ range with a tighter ES range suggests tech is leading, which is worth noting before the open.
