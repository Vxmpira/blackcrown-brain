**Title:** Watching the opening range test on index futures

**Post:**

Something worth watching on index futures: the opening range often gets tested before the day picks a direction. A common reference point is the prior day high and low, since many traders are watching the same levels. When price holds above a level after an opening drive, the question shifts from whether it breaks to whether it can hold on a retest. That is a structure observation, not a signal.

LodeStone's prior day and week levels plot those references directly on your chart, so you are not redrawing them each morning. Korvus Operator includes LodeStone on TradingView, and the sessions and timezone inputs keep the levels aligned with the session you actually trade.

Question for the room: when an opening range test is failing, what tells you first? Volume, time spent at the level, or something on the tape?
