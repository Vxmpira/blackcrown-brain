**Post:**

Session reading, one repeatable exercise for index futures.

Before the open, write down three numbers: prior day high, prior day low, and prior day close. Most traders already have these on their charts. The useful question is what price does once it trades through them.

Next, note which killzone the move happens in. Timing matters more than most people admit. The same sweep can read very differently at the London open than at 3pm.

When price takes out a prior high or low, check whether it closes back inside the range or keeps running. Log it with the time and the killzone. After five sessions you have a record of your own market, not someone else's story.

If you watch two correlated index futures, look for SMT divergence: one makes a new high or low and the other fails to confirm. Note whether it resolves, and how.

This is a study method, not a signal or a plan. Education only, not financial advice.
