Nov 3: Prior day high and low, what to watch at the open

The prior day high and low are two of the most watched reference points in index futures. They mark where the last session reached its limits, so many traders check whether price is accepting above or below those levels as the new session starts.

Questions worth asking before the open:
- Is price holding above or below the level, or only poking through it?
- Does the reaction hold on a candle close, or fade back into the range?
- Is volume confirming the move?
