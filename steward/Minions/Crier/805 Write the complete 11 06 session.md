**Session levels on index futures: a pre-open checklist**

1. **Prior day high and low.** These are the first liquidity pools price often tests after the open.
2. **Overnight high and low (Asia and London).** Note whether the RTH open lands inside, above, or below the overnight range.
3. **Prior week high and low.** Useful when you're tracking multi-day structure.
4. **Opening range.** Watch whether price accepts or rejects it.

Write the levels down before the bell so you judge the reaction, not the level after the fact. Which one has mattered most in your own sample? Share it in #trade-recaps.
