**Session map: which high and low are you marking?**

Levels get mixed up when the session is not labeled, so here is the map for index futures.

The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low. Regular hours for these contracts run 9:30 am to 4:15 pm ET.

Before you mark a level, ask which session it came from. An overnight high and a regular session high are two different reference points, and they can sit a long way apart. Tools like LodeStone can label session levels for you, but the labels only help if you know what each one means.
