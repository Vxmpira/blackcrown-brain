Overnight session runs 6:00 pm to 9:30 am ET while cash index markets are closed. Before the open, traders often check the overnight high and low, where price sits against the prior day's high, low and settlement, and whether the gap from the prior close is holding. They also scan the calendar for releases due before 9:30 and note how thin volume can get overnight. Korvus tracks the overnight session at korvus.industries. Education only. Not financial advice.
