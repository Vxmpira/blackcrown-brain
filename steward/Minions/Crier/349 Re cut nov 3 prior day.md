Prior day high and prior day low are the two levels most index futures traders check before the open, and they are worth tracking because price often reacts to where it has already been. The useful question is not whether a level will hold, but what price does when it arrives. Did it sweep the level and reverse, accept beyond it, or stall? Mark both levels on your chart, then review how the next session handled each one. Over a few weeks, that review shows how this market behaves around its own recent extremes.
