**Reading a scored headline before you trade the news**

A scored headline gives you impact, direction, and confidence. Before you act on any of them, run the context pass:

1. **Is it new?** Check whether the story repeats something the market already digested.
2. **Was it expected?** If the print matched consensus, a strong score may mean less for price than it looks.
3. **How did the tape react?** Watch what price did in the minutes after release, not just the headline.
4. **What does confidence measure?** It reflects how clearly the event was scored, not how likely price is to move in a given direction.

The score tells you what to look at. Context tells you whether it matters.
