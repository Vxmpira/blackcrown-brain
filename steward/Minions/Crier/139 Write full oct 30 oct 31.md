**Oct 30, Channel: #strategies**

Session levels for ES and NQ, value only.

Before the open, mark the levels that price has already respected. For ES and NQ, the most useful reference points are usually:

1. Prior day high and low
2. Overnight (globex) high and low
3. Prior week high and low
4. The first 15 minute range after the cash open

Mark them on your chart before you look for anything else. Note which ones price swept in the overnight session and which ones it left alone. A level that has been tested several times tells you something different from one touched once.

Write the levels down in your own notes with the date. After a few weeks you can check which ones actually acted as support or resistance on your instrument and which did not. That record is worth more than any single call.

Marking levels is education, not financial advice.

**Oct 31 Post 1, Channel: #presidential-news**

How to read a Presidential Wire item, in four parts.

Each scored item gives you four things. Read them in this order:

1. **Impact:** how much the headline could matter for index futures. A high score on a minor item is a reason to check the source, not to act.
2. **Direction:** the expected lean for ES and NQ if the item plays out as written. Direction is a hypothesis about how the market may react, not a forecast.
3. **Confidence:** how sure the scoring is about the first two parts. Low confidence means the wording is vague or the situation is still moving.
4. **Korvus read:** the written interpretation that ties the three scores together in plain language.

Read the Korvus read last, after you have formed your own view from the first three. Then ask what would prove the read wrong. If you cannot name it, the item is not yet actionable for you.

Scores are education and context, not financial advice.

Pre-open walkthrough of what Observer shows, value only.

Here is how to scan Observer before the bell, top to bottom, in about five minutes:

1. Check the timestamps first. Know how old the newest items are before you read their content.
2. Scan the scored news. Look at impact and confidence together. High impact with low confidence needs a second look.
3. Note what changed overnight compared with the prior session's close.
4. Compare your own marked levels with what the terminal is showing. Disagreement is useful to write down.
5. Write one sentence on what would change your plan for the open.

Panels and layouts can change between updates, so use the labels on your screen rather than memory. The goal is a routine you can repeat each morning, not a signal.

Observer is information, not financial advice.
