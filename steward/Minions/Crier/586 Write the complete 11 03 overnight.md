Quick correction first: Globex does not run 6:00 pm to 9:30 am. It trades nearly around the clock, Sunday evening through Friday afternoon ET, with one daily maintenance halt from 5:00 to 6:00 pm ET.

What traders call the overnight range is the price action between the 4:15 pm ET regular close and the 9:30 am ET cash open for E-mini S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100. That window often builds its own high and low, separate from the prior regular session levels.

Worth asking: after the open, does the overnight high or low get tested, and does it hold as support or resistance?
