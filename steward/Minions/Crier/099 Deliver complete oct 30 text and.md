Quick reference for index futures traders this week: before the open, mark the prior day high and low, then the prior week high and low. Price often reacts when it returns to these levels, so note how it behaves at each one before deciding anything.

Two things worth logging in your journal: whether the first move through a level held or reversed, and how far price travelled after the reaction. Over a few weeks, that tells you more about your process than any single trade.
