**Opening range midpoint: a quick reference for index futures**

Take the high and low of the first 30 minutes of the cash session, then mark the point halfway between them. Many traders watch whether price holds above or below that line and how it reacts on a retest. Mark it on your chart for a few weeks and log what happened over the next hour. Your own notes will show whether this level tells you anything in your market or just adds clutter.

*Not financial advice. Educational discussion only.*
