**Session map for index futures: know which high and low you're marking**

The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low. Regular hours for these contracts run 9:30 am to 4:15 pm ET.

Why it matters: when you mark levels on a chart, know which session printed them. Mixing the two can make a level look more important than it is.
