Session levels are the highs and lows that price printed during specific trading windows, such as the Asian, London, and New York sessions. Traders log them because they often act as reference points: price revisits a prior session high or low, reacts, and sometimes continues. Logging them is simple. Before each session opens, note the high, low, and midpoint of the prior session. During the session, mark any new extremes as they form. At the close, record the final range and whether price swept a prior level before reversing. Keep the notes in one place, dated, so you can review how often each level held over a few weeks. Consistency matters more than the number of levels you track. Start with two or three and expand only when the log shows they add value. LodeStone's session toolkit automates much of this on TradingView, if you prefer to skip the manual marking. Nothing here is a trading recommendation; it is a method for building your own record.
