**Oct 29**

A fixed pre-session watchlist

Pick the same short list every session so your review is comparable day to day:
1. ES and NQ levels from the previous session
2. The 8:30 ET economic releases, if any are scheduled
3. Whether ES and NQ are trading inside or outside the overnight range
4. The one level you would need to see accepted to change your read

Keep it to four items. A longer list turns preparation into noise.

Log what happened to each item at the close. Over a few weeks that log shows you which items carry information for you and which ones you check out of habit.

Education only. This is a process to test, not a signal.

**Oct 30**

Reading the overnight range on ES and NQ

The overnight range is the high and low from the Globex session up to the 9:30 ET cash open.

Note where price opens inside it: near the high, near the low, or in the middle.

Then watch the first break. If price breaks and returns inside the range, the range is still acting as a boundary. If it breaks and holds, the range was a launch point.

Check ES and NQ together. When both break the same way, the move has more agreement. When they split, watch which one holds.

Education only. Ranges describe behavior, they do not predict it.
