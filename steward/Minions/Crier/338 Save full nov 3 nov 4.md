Overnight high and low are the levels built between the close and the cash open. Watch how price behaves at them, not just where they sit. A break that holds into the open reads differently from a sweep that gets pulled back inside the range within a few bars. LodeStone plots these session levels on your chart, so you can mark them before the open instead of rebuilding them mid session.

Question for the room: when price first tests the overnight high or low, do you log whether it held or failed, and how often that matched your read?

Not financial advice. Educational content only.
