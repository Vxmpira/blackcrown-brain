#presidential-news

How to read a Presidential Wire item, in four parts:

1. **Impact:** how much the headline could move index futures if the market treats it as new information. A high number means the news is big. It does not say which way price goes.
2. **Direction:** the lean the item carries for the index: bullish, bearish, or mixed. Read it as a tilt, not a call.
3. **Confidence:** how sure the scoring is about impact and direction. Low confidence on a big headline means waiting for a second source before you weigh it.
4. **Korvus read:** the written take on what the item means in context. Check it against the chart and your own plan.

Scores are inputs to your process, not trade signals. Not financial advice. Tell us in the thread which field you check first and why.
