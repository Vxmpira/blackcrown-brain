Position Sizing: Fixed Dollar Risk

Size is decided before entry, not after.

Rule: pick a dollar amount you can lose on one idea, base it on your account, and keep it fixed. Do not raise it after a win.

Formula:
Contracts = Dollar risk / (Stop in points x Dollar per point)

Illustration (hypothetical):
Account $5,000, risk 1% = $50
MNQ stop 10 points at $2 per point = $20 per contract
$50 / $20 = 2.5, round down to 2 contracts

Why it matters: losing streaks happen to every strategy. Fixed-percentage sizing keeps a bad run survivable, while oversizing can turn an ordinary drawdown into an account-ending one.
