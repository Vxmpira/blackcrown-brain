LodeStone is the chart side of the Korvus stack: a session level toolkit built on ICT concepts for index futures, build V7.5. It has seven feature layers and does not repaint.

The seven layers:
1. Sessions and timezone
2. Prior day and week levels
3. Premium and discount
4. Resting liquidity
5. SMT divergence
6. A restrained volume profile
7. Sweep cleanup

Two versions:
- **Tradovate version:** 149 dollars one time. It ships as a code file to your checkout email, and access cannot be revoked.
- **TradingView version:** $19.99 a month, with the full inputs panel. TradingView access is also included with Korvus Operator.

Buy buttons are in #korvus-indicator.

The tool shows structure on your chart. It does not tell you what to trade. The decisions stay with you.

Not financial advice. LodeStone is a charting tool, not a signal service or a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument. Trading index futures involves substantial risk of loss.
