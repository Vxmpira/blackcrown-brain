Reading the overnight range against yesterday's range

Before the open, compare two numbers: the high to low of the prior regular session, and the high to low of the overnight session. Then ask a few plain questions.

Did overnight trade stay inside yesterday's range, or push past one side of it? If it reached the prior high or low, did it stall there, or run through and hold?

An overnight range that sits well inside the prior day often reflects balance. A break beyond one side that then returns can show where resting liquidity was taken. Neither pattern tells you what comes next. It gives you context for the session you are about to watch.

Log the same comparison each day for a week and you will see how often your read held up.

Education only, not financial advice. Trading carries risk of loss.
