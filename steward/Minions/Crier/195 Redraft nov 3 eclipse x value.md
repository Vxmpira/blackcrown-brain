**Eclipse-X post (Nov 3)**

Session levels worth marking before the New York open: the prior day high and low, plus the Asia range high and low.

Before 9:30 ET, draw them on your chart. Then watch the first 30 minutes. Does price accept above or below a level, or does it sweep through and return inside? Log which one happened and whether it matched your plan.

After a few weeks, that log shows how these levels behaved in the market you actually trade.

Not financial advice.
