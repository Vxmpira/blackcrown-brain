Overnight range on ES and NQ

The overnight session, roughly 6:00 p.m. to 9:30 a.m. ET, builds a high and low while most traders sleep. Many watch how price reacts to those levels at the cash open.

Three questions to ask:

1. Did the open hold inside the overnight range, or break out of it?
2. Did ES and NQ agree on the break? A move in one without the other is worth noting.
3. How much of the range was already used before 9:30?

Keep a log for a week: overnight high, low, and the first 30 minutes of direction. Patterns show up in your own data faster than in any summary.
