**Forecast vs. actual: reading the USD agenda before the print**

The daily USD agenda in #forex-news lists each release with its consensus forecast and, once it prints, the live actual. The gap between those two numbers often matters more than the headline label. A CPI print that matches consensus can move index futures less than one that misses by a tenth. Before the release, look at where price is sitting relative to the prior reaction, since that shapes how much surprise the market can absorb. After the print, watch whether the first move holds over the next few bars or fades. None of this says what to do. It shows you what to measure.
