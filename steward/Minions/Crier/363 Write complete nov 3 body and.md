**Nov 3**

Prior day levels are one of the simplest reference points on an index futures chart: the high and low from the last full session. Chart readers often use them as context for where price has been, and they are worth watching when planning the day. LodeStone plots prior day and prior week levels as part of its session toolkit, so you can see where yesterday's range ended without drawing lines by hand. Use them to inform your own plan, not as a signal. This is education, not financial advice.

**Nov 4**

The overnight session runs from the US close to the next open, and its high and low give you a fixed range to compare against once the cash session starts. LodeStone's session inputs let you set session times for your own timezone, so the overnight range is drawn for you instead of reconstructed from memory at the open. Watch how price behaves when it trades back into that range compared with when it holds outside it, and log what you see. This is education, not financial advice, and nothing here is a trade recommendation.
