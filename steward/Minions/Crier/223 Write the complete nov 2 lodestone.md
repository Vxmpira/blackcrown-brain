LodeStone is a session level toolkit for index futures, built on ICT concepts. On your chart it draws session highs and lows in your chosen timezone, prior day and week levels, premium and discount zones, resting liquidity, SMT divergence between related indexes, a restrained volume profile, and sweep cleanup that tidies levels once price has taken them.

Levels are drawn as each session develops and are not redrawn after the fact, so what you see on the chart is what the bars printed.

There are two versions. The Tradovate version is $149 one time, delivered as a code file to your checkout email. The TradingView version is $19.99 a month, and it is included free with Korvus Operator.
