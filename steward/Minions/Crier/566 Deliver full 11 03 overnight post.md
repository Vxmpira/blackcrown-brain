**Overnight Session: What Happens Between 6:00 PM and 9:30 AM ET**

Index futures trade nearly around the clock, but the stretch from 6:00 PM ET to the 9:30 AM ET cash open is its own environment. It is separate from the prior regular session, so yesterday's regular session highs and lows give you a reference point, not a promise of how overnight price will behave.

How traders use the window:

- Mark the overnight high and low as reference levels heading into the cash open.
- Watch whether price holds or breaks the prior session's range before 9:30.
- Account for thinner liquidity. Moves can be sharper on lower volume, and fills can differ from what a chart suggests.
- Note economic releases and headlines that land while US cash markets are closed.

Korvus tracks overnight headlines and scores them with impact, direction, and confidence, so you can see what is moving. That is context for your own process, not a signal.
