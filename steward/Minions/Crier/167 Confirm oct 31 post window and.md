Session levels are one of the first things worth marking before the open on ES and NQ. Prior day high and low, the prior week range, and the Asia and London highs and lows show where price has already shown interest.

Before the bell, ask yourself: Did yesterday's high get taken overnight? Is price trading at a premium or discount to the day's range? Which levels were swept, and did they hold?

The goal isn't to predict. It's to know which levels are in play before you decide anything.
