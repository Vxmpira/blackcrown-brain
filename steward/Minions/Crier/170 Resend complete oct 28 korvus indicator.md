The levels that matter most before the US open on ES and NQ are usually already on your chart: prior day high and low, the overnight session range, and the prior week's high and low.

Watch how price behaves at those levels once the 9:30 ET open hits. Acceptance above or below a level tells a different story than a quick wick through it.

For NQ, check the gap between the prior close and the overnight high or low, since it often frames the first move.

Mark these before the bell so you are reacting to a plan, not building one under pressure.
