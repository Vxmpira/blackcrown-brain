Prior day high and low are some of the most watched levels in index futures because they show where yesterday's buyers and sellers ran out of steam. Many traders mark them before the open and then watch how price behaves when it reaches them: does it stall, accept beyond, or reject quickly? Tracking that reaction across sessions builds a clearer picture of market structure than the level alone. This is education, not financial advice.
