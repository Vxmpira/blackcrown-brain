Overnight level marking, a quick read for index futures traders.

Mark the high and low of the overnight range before the cash open. For ES and NQ, the window is the Globex session, 6:00 PM ET to 9:30 AM ET, per CME session times. Once it's drawn, you have a reference for how price behaves at the open: does it reclaim one side, sweep one and reverse, or hold outside the range?

Tip: compare today's overnight range to the prior few days. A narrow overnight often means the session hasn't decided yet. Use the same session definition every day so your notes are comparable.

Educational only, not trading advice.
