On scheduled news days, the first question is whether the release is high impact for index futures and which way it leans. Presidential Wire scores each story on impact, direction, and confidence, and adds a written Korvus read, so you can screen the tape before you pick a chart.

Scored news does not tell you what price will do. A high impact print can be priced in, and a low impact print can still move a thin session. Treat the score as a filter for attention, then check your own plan.
