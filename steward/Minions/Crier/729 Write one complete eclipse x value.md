Before a US release hits, write three things on paper: the consensus number, the prior reading, and the one move that would change your read. Do it before the print, not after.

Consensus tells you what the market already expects, so a "beat" only means something relative to it. The prior reading shows whether the trend is accelerating or fading, which often matters more than the headline. The third line is the useful one: name the specific price reaction that would make you revise your view, such as a failed break of a level you flagged. If you cannot name it, you are reacting, not reading.

Review the notes the next morning. Most traders find their first-minute moves were guesses that happened to feel like analysis.
