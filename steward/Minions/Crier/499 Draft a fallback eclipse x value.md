Before the open, write down the prior day high, low, and close for ES and NQ. Then add the prior week high and low. Those five numbers make a simple reference map.

Next, ask where the open sits against them. Is price opening above yesterday's high, below yesterday's low, or inside the range? Each condition calls for a different read, and the open tells you which one you are working in.

If you use LodeStone on TradingView, the prior day and week levels are on the inputs panel, so the lines are drawn for you. Either way, check the levels before you look at an entry.
