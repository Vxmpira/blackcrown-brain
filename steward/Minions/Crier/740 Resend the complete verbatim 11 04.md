How to read a scored headline before you trade on it

A headline tells you something happened. It doesn't tell you what the market will do with it. Three things to check before you act:

1. Impact: is this a market-moving release or background noise?
2. Direction: which way does it lean for index futures, and is that consistent with what price is already doing?
3. Confidence: how sure is the read? Low confidence means wait for the tape to confirm.

The Presidential Wire in #presidential-news scores each item on these three fields. Use them as a filter, not a signal. Your plan still comes from your own levels and risk.
