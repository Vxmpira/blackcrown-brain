**Overnight levels worth marking before the open**

Globex equity index futures trade nearly around the clock, with a daily maintenance halt from 5:00 to 6:00 pm ET. Regular hours for E-mini S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 are 9:30 am to 4:15 pm ET, so a large share of price action happens outside the cash session.

The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low.

Before the open, many traders mark:

- The overnight high and low
- Yesterday's regular session high and low
- Yesterday's regular session close

Some traders use these marks to see whether price is holding above or below each level as the cash open approaches. Marking levels is a study habit, not a signal to act.

Education only, not financial advice.
