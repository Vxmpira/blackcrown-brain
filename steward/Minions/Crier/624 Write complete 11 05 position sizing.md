**Position sizing, education only**

Most blown accounts weren't wrong about direction. They were oversized for the stop they actually needed.

Decide the dollar amount you can lose on one idea before you look at a chart. Then work backward. Stop distance in points times dollar value per point gives your risk per contract. Divide your max loss by that number and you have a ceiling, not a target.

*Illustration:* A $2,000 account with a 1% max risk allows $20 per idea. A 10-point stop on a contract worth $5 per point risks $50, so that setup exceeds the limit even for one contract. Your numbers will differ.

No contract count is prescribed here. What's your max loss per idea? Share it below.
