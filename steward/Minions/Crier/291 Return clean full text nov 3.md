**Nov 3**

Four levels to mark before the open:

1. Prior day high: [PDH]
2. Prior day low: [PDL]
3. Overnight range: high [ON HIGH], low [ON LOW]
4. Prior week high or low, whichever sits nearest current price: [PWH or PWL]

Mark them, then watch how price behaves at each one. These are reference points, not predictions.

**Nov 4**

Reference levels are only useful if you track what price does at them. Before the open, write down the levels you're watching. During the session, log each one: rejected, accepted through, or swept and reclaimed. At the close, review the log against your plan. After a few weeks, the pattern will tell you more about how you read these levels than any single session.
