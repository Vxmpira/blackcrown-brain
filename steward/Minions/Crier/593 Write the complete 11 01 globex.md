**Globex high and low before the open**

Index futures trade nearly around the clock on CME Globex, Sunday 6:00 pm to Friday 5:00 pm ET, with a daily halt from 5:00 to 6:00 pm ET. The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low.

Overnight extremes are prices the market already traded, a reference point, not a signal. Before the open, compare where the overnight range sits against yesterday's levels, then watch whether price holds or rejects those marks once cash trading starts.

Education only, not financial advice.
