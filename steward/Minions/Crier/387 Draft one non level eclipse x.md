Why a stop run looks like a breakout before it fails.

Price pushes through an obvious high or low. Volume picks up, the candle closes strong, and it looks like real conviction. Then it reverses.

Resting stops sit just beyond those points. Price trades into them for liquidity, and the push you read as conviction is often just the fill.

Before you act on the next one, check:
1. Did price hold outside for more than one close?
2. Did follow through come from new participation, or only the stops being taken?
3. Did it stall or reverse fast on the same tape?

Which of these has tripped you up most?
