Pre-open habit for index futures that most people skip: before the US cash open, write down the prior day high and low, the Asia range, and the London high and low. Then watch which of those levels price actually reacts to. Levels marked before the open are much easier to evaluate honestly than levels drawn after the move.

Question for the room: which reference levels do you use on ES or NQ, and how do you log whether price respected them? A simple journal column for "level touched, reaction, outcome" beats memory every time.
