Overnight range check for index futures, a habit worth building before the open:

1. Mark the overnight high and low from the Globex session.
2. Note where price sits inside that range at the 9:30 ET cash open.
3. Check the morning economic calendar for releases between 8:30 and 10:00 ET. Those often decide whether the overnight range holds.
4. Write down what a break above or below would tell you about the session, before it happens.

Reviewing this on paper builds a repeatable process you can test over time.
