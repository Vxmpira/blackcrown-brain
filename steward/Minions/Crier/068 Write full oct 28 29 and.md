**Oct 28: Pinning levels before the cash open on ES and NQ**

Before 9:30 ET, mark five levels on each chart: prior day high and low, prior day close, and overnight high and low. Add prior week high and low if they sit near price.

On ES, draw the overnight high and low separately from the prior day range so you can see which side the session has been defending.

On NQ, check whether the overnight range is wider or narrower than the prior day's range. A wider range suggests the market is repricing. A narrower one suggests it is waiting.

Keep the chart to these few lines. A level earns its place only if you can state what you expect price to do when it trades there.

Education only, not trading advice.

**Oct 29: A fixed pre-session watchlist for index futures**

Use the same list every morning so that changes stand out:

1. Economic calendar: release times, prior values, and forecasts.
2. Overnight bias: is ES above or below its prior close at 8:30 ET?
3. Relative strength: is NQ or ES leading the move?
4. Volatility: which way is VIX moving, and how large is the opening gap?
5. Levels: the five you marked before the open.
6. One sentence: what would make you change your read?

Write the sentence down before the open. Reviewing it later shows whether your read held up or whether the market gave you information you weren't tracking.

Education only, not trading advice.

**Oct 30: Reading the overnight range on ES and NQ**

The overnight range is the high and low from 6 PM to 9:30 ET. Three questions help you read it:

1. Where did price open within that range: near the high, the middle, or the low?
2. How does today's range compare with the average of the last 10 sessions? Unusually large ranges usually mean heavier participation. Small ranges often come before news.
3. Did the cash open take out an overnight extreme, and did it hold there?

On NQ, check whether the overnight move came from one headline or from broad trading. A single headline often fades differently than a steady build.

Education only, not trading advice.
