Session levels, the short version.

Most index futures traders watch the same handful of prices: the prior day high and low, the prior week high and low, and the high and low each session printed before the one you are trading. Marking them before the open turns a blank chart into a map.

Three habits that make them useful:

1. Mark them first, then wait. A level matters when price reaches it, not when you draw it.
2. Note how price reacts. A rejection is information. A clean break is information too.
3. Keep a log. Record which levels mattered each day so you learn how your market behaves.

LodeStone plots these levels on TradingView, with sessions and timezone set in its inputs panel. Educational only, not financial advice.
