**Routine prompt for ES traders: before the open**

Before you look at a chart, write down three things:

1. The prior day's high, low, and close.
2. Where overnight price is trading relative to that range.
3. One condition that would make you stand aside today.

Then check whether your plan still holds once the cash session opens. A written routine turns a chaotic open into a checklist you can actually review later.
