Session levels: a quick way to check your chart before the open.

Mark the Asia, London, and New York session highs and lows on your index futures chart. Note the prior day high, low, and close, plus the prior week high and low. When price returns to one of these levels, write down what happens: a clean reaction, a sweep and reversal, or a slow grind through. Over a few weeks you build your own record of which levels matter on your instrument and session.

LodeStone plots these levels automatically on TradingView if you want them marked the same way every session. Share your set-up and what you saw. Educational only, not financial advice.

**Draft 2: #presidential-news, Oct 31 (REPLACEMENT)**

How to read a Presidential Wire item

Each post is scored news, not a signal. Check four fields:

Impact: how much the headline could move index futures.
Direction: the lean the score implies for the market, bullish or bearish.
Confidence: how sure the scoring is about that lean. Low confidence means treat it as context only.
Korvus read: the written explanation of why the score landed where it did.

Read the Korvus read first, then the score. Compare both against the economic agenda in #forex-news before deciding anything. Scores are automated and can be wrong. This is education, not financial advice.
