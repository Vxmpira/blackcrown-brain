Prior day high and low are two of the first levels many index futures traders mark before the open. Some traders look at whether price returns to yesterday's high or low during the first hour, and how it trades once it gets there. Others check whether price holds beyond the level on a closing basis or slips back into the prior range. Some compare yesterday's range with the overnight range, since a large gap between the two can change how a level reads. Traders who keep a journal often tag each session by which level got tested and what followed, which builds a sample of their own behavior over time. If you want to compare tagging methods with other operators, share yours in the channel. This is education, not financial advice.
