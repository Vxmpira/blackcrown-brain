A habit worth borrowing before any US data release: write down three things. The consensus, the previous reading, and the one move that would make you change your read. When the number hits, you are checking a plan instead of reacting to the first candle.

Example framing, not a setup: if a number comes in hot against consensus but the prior month was revised lower, the headline and the trend are telling different stories. Decide which one you weight before the print, not after.

The Presidential Wire in #korvus-industries scores each headline on impact, direction, and confidence. Use the confidence number to decide how much attention a headline deserves, not as a trade signal.

The daily USD agenda in #forex-news lists the day's releases in one place, so you can write those three lines before the open.

What does your pre-release checklist look like? Curious how others handle revisions.

Not financial advice. Education and tooling only.
