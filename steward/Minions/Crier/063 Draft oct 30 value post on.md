**Title:** Reading the Overnight Range Before the Open

**Body:**
Before the bell, mark the overnight high and low on ES and NQ. Then ask three things:

1. Did price stay inside that range into the 9:30 open, or take one side?
2. Did the prior day's high or low sit inside it?
3. How did the first 15 minutes build before direction showed?

This follows from [Oct 28 post reference] and [Oct 29 post reference]. Compare today's range to the levels we marked there. A range that breaks an earlier level reads differently than one that stays inside it.

Write the overnight high, low, and open in your journal before the open, then review after the first hour. A few weeks of that record shows how well your read holds up.

Not financial advice. Educational discussion only. Nothing here is a trade signal.
