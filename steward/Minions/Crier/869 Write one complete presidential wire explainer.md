Reading the Presidential Wire, field by field:

Impact: how much a headline could move index futures, from low to high.

Direction: which way the score leans for the market, up, down, or mixed.

Confidence: how sure the scoring is about that lean. Low confidence means treat the post as a question to watch, not a read to act on.

Korvus read: the written reasoning behind the scores.

Practical tip: read the Korvus read before the direction. If the reasoning doesn't match the headline, the score deserves less weight than its number suggests.

Not financial advice.
