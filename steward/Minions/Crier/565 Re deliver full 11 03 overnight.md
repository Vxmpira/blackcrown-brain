**Overnight session: what the 6:00 pm to 9:30 am ET window actually is**

Index futures trade through the overnight session, but thinner liquidity changes how price behaves. Fewer participants means wider spreads and faster reactions to headlines, so a level that held during the day can get tested with less volume behind it.

Three things worth watching if you trade the open:

1. Where price sits relative to the prior session's high and low.
2. Whether the overnight range expands or stays compressed into the cash open.
3. Which headlines hit while most desks are closed.
