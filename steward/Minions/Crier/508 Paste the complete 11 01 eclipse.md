The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low.

Those two sets of levels answer different questions. Prior day levels show where the last regular session did its business: where price was accepted, where it was rejected, and where liquidity may still rest from that auction. They are a record of yesterday's decisions.

Overnight levels answer a more immediate question: what happened while the cash market was closed, and did price trade through that liquidity before the open or leave it untouched? A sweep of the overnight high or low at the open reads differently than price holding inside the overnight range. Traders who mark both sets are comparing the reference the market already respected with the range the overnight session built for itself.

Neither set is a signal on its own. They are context, and context is only useful when you decide in advance how you will read it.
