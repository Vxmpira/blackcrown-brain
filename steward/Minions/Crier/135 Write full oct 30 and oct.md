**Oct 30, Post 1**
**Channel: #strategies**

Session levels, how I track them for ES and NQ:

Before the Globex open, mark the prior day high, low, and close. Add the prior week high and low if they sit within reach. These are the levels the next session is most likely to test first.

Then note the session timing: Asia range, London open, and the 9:30 ET cash open. Write down the range each session sets before the next one starts. A level that held across three sessions usually carries more weight than one touched once.

To journal it, log the date, each level in points, the time it was touched, and what price did over the next 15 minutes. Keep this for two weeks before judging anything. Your own log will show your pattern faster than memory will.

**Oct 31, Post 1**
**Channel: #presidential-news**

How to read a Presidential Wire item, step by step:

1. **Time.** Compare the release time to the cash open. An item at 8:30 ET hits a different tape than one at 2 PM.
2. **Impact.** A high score means the market may react. It does not mean it will.
3. **Direction and confidence.** Read direction as a hypothesis. Read confidence as how sure the scoring is, not as a forecast.
4. **The written read.** Check whether it names a specific mechanism, such as rates, earnings, or policy timing. Vague reads deserve less weight.
5. **Price check.** Watch the first five minutes. If price disagrees with the read, that is information too.

Log each item and what happened after it.

Weekly review, about five minutes, no P&L required:
