**Eclipse-X post draft: #korvus-indicator**

Session levels give you a map of where price has already been before you ask where it might go. On index futures, three questions are worth asking of any session high or low:

1. Has it been taken out during a later session, or is it still untouched?
2. Did price reject from it, or accept beyond it?
3. Where does the prior day's range sit relative to it?

LodeStone marks these levels from your session and timezone inputs, alongside prior day and week levels and premium/discount context, so you can review the same questions on your own chart. The levels show where the market has been. How you read them is your decision.

Educational discussion only, not financial advice.
