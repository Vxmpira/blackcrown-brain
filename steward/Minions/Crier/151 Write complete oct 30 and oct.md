**Oct 30, #strategies (105 words)**

Before the open, a short list of levels worth marking on your index futures chart, so the first hour has a map:

1. Prior day high and low
2. Prior week high and low
3. Overnight (Globex) high and low
4. Asia and London session ranges, if you track them
5. Pre-market high and low

Mark them before the bell, not during it. Watch how price reacts when it reaches each one: acceptance beyond a level, a quick rejection, or a sweep and return all tell different stories. Note which levels get respected today and compare notes in the thread afterward. This is education, not trading advice.

**Oct 31, #presidential-news, Post 1 (108 words)**

Each Presidential Wire item carries four parts.

Impact: how much the headline could matter to index futures, from low to high. A high score does not predict price.

Direction: the lean the scoring assigns to the market, not a forecast. Treat it as a hypothesis to test against price.

Confidence: how certain the scoring is about its read, based on the clarity and source of the news. Low confidence means the story is still developing.

Written read: the context behind the scores. Start here, then check whether the scores match what price actually does. Use scores as prompts for your own work; this is education, not trading advice.
