Session level journal, one entry per session. Keep it to five fields so you actually fill it in:

1. Levels in play: prior day high and low, overnight high and low, prior week high and low, today's open.
2. Which level price reached first, and roughly what time.
3. Did it hold, get swept and reclaimed, or get accepted through? Write one word.
4. Time spent within 10 points of the level before the reaction.
5. One sentence on what you expected versus what printed.

After 20 sessions, sort by field 3. You will see which level types behaved for you on index futures and which ones you were guessing about. Track the behavior, not the P&L, first.
