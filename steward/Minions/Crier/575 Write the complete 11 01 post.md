**Session times, and why the overnight high matters for your levels**

The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low. Regular trading hours run 9:30 am to 4:15 pm ET (cited CME contract specifications, capture date pending).

If you mark prior day levels, check which high and low your chart or indicator is referencing. A prior regular session high and an overnight high can sit at different prices, and mixing them up changes what the chart is telling you.
