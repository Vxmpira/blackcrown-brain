Clocks fall back in the US on the first Sunday of November, so for a few days your usual session times may not line up with your wall clock.

A quick way to check which session is live:

1. Pull the session times from the official CME Group trading hours page, not from a screenshot or a forum post.
2. Convert those times to your own time zone with a tool that handles daylight saving, and note which zone the exchange publishes in.
3. Compare the current time against the open and close, and confirm on your platform's clock before you size a trade.
