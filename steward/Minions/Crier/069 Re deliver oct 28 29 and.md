**Post 1, Oct 28**

Three questions before you act on any news item. What changed: is this a new fact or something the market already priced in? Which contract moves first, ES or NQ? How reliable is the source: a primary release outweighs a secondhand summary. The Presidential Wire scores each item for impact, direction, and confidence. Treat those scores as a starting point for your own read, not a signal. Log the item and what price did over the next 15 minutes. Your own record over a few weeks teaches more than any single score.

**Post 2, Oct 29**

Prior day high and low are some of the most watched reference points in index futures, mostly because so many participants watch them. Before the open, mark the prior session high, low, and close on your chart. Note where overnight trade sits relative to those levels. The question is not what to do next. The question is what the market is already respecting. LodeStone plots session and prior day levels on TradingView so you can keep that context on screen without drawing it by hand. Education only, not a trade plan.

**Post 3, Oct 30**

Before a high impact USD release, check three things. First, the forecast versus the prior reading, because the gap is what the market prices. Second, the time, converted to your own timezone so you are not surprised at the open. Third, what else sits in the same window, since two releases close together can blur the read. Eclipse-X posts the daily USD economic agenda with live actuals in #forex-news, so the calendar is in one place. Plan your watch list in advance. Reacting in the first minute after a print is the hardest habit to unlearn.
