Before the open, mark the Asia high and low, the London high and low, and the prior day's high and low. Then watch how price behaves when it reaches them. Does it stall, reject, or run straight through? A poke above a prior high that closes back inside often reads differently than a clean break that holds. Log what you see each session. After a few weeks, you'll have a clearer picture of which levels your market respects and which it mostly ignores. Education only, not financial advice. Nothing here is a signal, and all trading carries risk.
