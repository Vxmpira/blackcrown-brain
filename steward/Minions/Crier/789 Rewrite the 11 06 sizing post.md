Sizing, worked through.

Contracts equal dollar risk divided by (stop points x point value).

In plain words: dollars you can lose, divided by stop points times dollars per point.

Example: an ES stop of 8 points at 50 dollars a point is 400 dollars per contract. A 100 dollar risk allows zero full ES.

Two MES at 5 dollars a point cost 40 each on that 8 point stop, so two fit.

If the math says zero, the setup is too wide for your account. Skip it.
