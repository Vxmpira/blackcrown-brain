**Session levels primer**

Four levels worth marking before the open:

1. **Prior day high and low.** Where the last session stopped is a reference point for today.
2. **Overnight range.** The globex high and low. Watch whether price accepts above or below it.
3. **London open.** Often the first real volume of the day. Note where it starts against the overnight range.
4. **New York open.** Compare it to the London range and prior day levels. Agreement or conflict between them is information.

Habit: write the levels down before the session, then log how price reacts at each one. After a week, patterns start to show.
