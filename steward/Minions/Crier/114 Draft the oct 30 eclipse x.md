**Pre-session checklist: the four prior-day numbers to check before the open**

Before index futures open, most of your context is already on the chart. Start with four numbers from the prior session:

1. Prior day high
2. Prior day low
3. Prior day close
4. Today's open, compared against the prior close (gapped up, gapped down, or inside)

LodeStone plots prior day and week levels alongside the session boundaries, so this checklist takes a glance instead of a hunt. Each morning, ask: where is price relative to these four, and has any of them already been tested overnight?

Educational only, not financial advice. Share your own pre-session checklist in the thread.
