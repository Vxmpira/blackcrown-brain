**Nov 4 | Pre-open level map for index futures**

Three level families to mark before the open:

**Overnight session high and low:** The range built while the cash session was closed, and price often tests it early in the day.

**Prior week high and low:** The larger map, which traders often watch as a boundary when the day's range is still forming.

**Opening range midpoint:** The midpoint of the first range of the session, which some traders use as a bias check, with price holding above or below it.

Educational only, not financial advice.

Which of these do you mark first, and how do you handle a level that fails on the first test?
