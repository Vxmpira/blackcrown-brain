Nov 6: The midpoint of the prior session

Every session leaves a range. The midpoint sits halfway between that session's high and low, and many traders use it to frame whether price is working in the upper or lower half of recent activity.

Questions worth asking before the open:
- Did yesterday close above or below its midpoint?
- Did price trade through the midpoint early, or hold on one side all session?
- How has the market treated this level on the sessions earlier this week?

Logging the answers over several days shows how this level behaves in your market, not just what it did once.

Educational discussion only. Not financial advice.
