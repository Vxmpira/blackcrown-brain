Session levels are where index futures often start to react, so it helps to know which ones you are watching before the open. Prior day high and low, prior week levels, and the overnight range give you a short list of prices to track. LodeStone draws those levels on your chart and does not repaint, so the levels you saw at the close are still there when you return. Korvus runs the news side around the clock, scoring headlines by likely impact on index futures, so you can check whether a level sits near something that matters. Build your list before the bell, then watch how price behaves at each level.
