Pre-session checklist for index futures. Before the open, check three things: the high-impact USD events on the calendar and their exact times, where price sits relative to yesterday's high and low, and whether the overnight range is already wide or still tight. A wide overnight range going into a data release often means part of the move is already priced in. A tight range with no scheduled news usually means the market is still deciding. Write down what you expect before you look at the chart, then compare. Education only, not financial advice.
