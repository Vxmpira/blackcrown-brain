**Nov 3**

Four levels to mark on your chart before the cash open:

1. Prior day high
2. Prior day low
3. Overnight high
4. Overnight low

Price reacting at one of these is information. Price trading through one and failing to hold beyond it is also information. Write down what you expect at each level before the bell, so you are reading the chart instead of chasing it.

Education only. Not financial advice.

**Nov 4**

Before the session, run three checks in order. First, pull up today's scheduled USD economic events and note their exact times in your own timezone, so you know when volatility is most likely to arrive. Second, compare current price against yesterday's high and low to see whether the market is holding inside yesterday's range or pressing outside it. Third, measure the overnight range width and compare it with recent sessions, since a wide overnight range and a narrow one call for different expectations.

Write your three observations down before the open. A checklist you can review after the close is how you learn what the market actually did.

Education only. Not financial advice.
