Before a US release prints, write three lines in your journal:

1. **Consensus:** the number the market expects.
2. **Prior reading:** the last print, so you can tell whether a beat is really a beat.
3. **The reaction that changes your read:** for ES, a close back through the pre-release level within the first few minutes would suggest the surprise is being faded, not chased.

Review it after the print, not during the spike. Your notes are what keep the read honest.
