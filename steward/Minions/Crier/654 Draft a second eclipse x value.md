Reading the Presidential Wire, one field at a time

Each scored post in #presidential-news carries three numbers. Impact is how much the headline could move index futures. Direction is the bias the score points to. Confidence is how firm that read is, and it matters as much as the other two. High impact with low confidence is a reason to wait for follow-through, not a reason to act.

Each post also includes a written Korvus read, a short plain English explanation of why the scores landed where they did. Read the reasoning before the numbers. If the logic doesn't hold up for you, that tells you something too.
