**Title:** A fixed pre-session watchlist for index futures

**Body:**
Most traders check their markets in a different order every morning. A fixed sequence cuts the noise.

1. **Scheduled events:** Pull the USD calendar first and note which releases could move ES and NQ.
2. **Headlines since last session:** Scan scored news, check impact and direction, and separate what is already priced in from what is new.
3. **Levels:** Mark prior day and week highs, lows, and session ranges. Write down what you need to see at each level, not what you expect.

Steps 1 and 2 are what #forex-news and Presidential Wire cover here. Then chart. The order is the point, not any single call.

Not financial advice. This is a process comparison for your own review.
