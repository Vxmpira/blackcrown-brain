**Post 1: Channel: #strategies**

**Prior day levels before the open**

Before the session opens, mark three reference points: the prior day high, the prior day low, and the prior day close. Then note where the overnight range sits against them. Price trading above yesterday's high at the London open reads differently than price holding inside yesterday's range, and that difference is worth journaling before you decide anything.

Session timing depends on your timezone, so confirm the session windows on your own chart before relying on them. LodeStone's session and timezone inputs, along with the prior day and week levels, plot these automatically on TradingView if you'd rather not draw them by hand.

Not financial advice. Levels show where price has been, not where it will go.

Rationale: Moves community engagement by giving members a concrete, repeatable reference-level habit to discuss and test.

**Post 2: Channel: #korvus-industries**

**How we read the wire before the 8:30 prints**

On high impact USD days, our routine is to check the Presidential Wire for scored items first, then the #forex-news agenda for release times and forecasts, then compare the two. Each wire item carries impact, direction, and confidence, plus a written Korvus read. Treat confidence as a filter for where to pay attention, not as a signal.

After each release, note whether the actual matched the agenda forecast and whether the scored direction agreed with what followed. A few weeks of that log shows you where the wire reads hold up for your own process and where they don't.

Not financial advice. Scores are analysis aids, not calls.
