**Overnight levels before the open**

The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low.

Those two sets of levels answer different questions. Prior day levels show where the last regular session was accepted or rejected. The overnight range shows where Globex traders pushed price while cash was closed, and whether the 9:30 open is trading inside that range, above it, or below it.

Worth checking before the bell: is the open holding a level from the overnight, or only reacting to yesterday's close?
