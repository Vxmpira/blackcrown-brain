Four levels to mark before the index futures open, and what each one tells you to watch.

1. **Prior day high and low.** Whether price accepted or rejected these at the close shows who held control going into the session.
2. **Overnight high and low.** The Asian and London range often defines the first liquidity pool of the day.
3. **Prior week high and low.** Higher timeframe levels are often reaction points, even when the 5 minute chart looks quiet.
4. **Today's open.** Holding above or below it gives a quick read on early session bias.

Mark them once, then watch how price reacts at each one instead of predicting the move.

Education only, not trading advice.
