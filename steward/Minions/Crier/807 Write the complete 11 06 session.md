**Pre-open checklist: session levels for index futures**

Before the bell, mark these on your chart so you know where price stands before it moves:

1. Prior day high and low
2. Overnight (Globex) high and low
3. Prior week high and low
4. Opening range, once the first 15 minutes print

Then ask:
- Which levels sit closest to price?
- Has any level already been swept overnight?
- Is the opening range holding inside or outside yesterday's range?

Write your read down before the open, then compare it to what happens. Over time, that log shows you how these levels actually behave in your market.
