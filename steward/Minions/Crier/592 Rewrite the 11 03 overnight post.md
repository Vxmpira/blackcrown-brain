Overnight trading on index futures, explained.

Globex trades nearly around the clock, from Sunday 6:00 pm ET to Friday 5:00 pm ET, with one daily halt from 5:00 to 6:00 pm ET. A useful reference point is the gap between the 4:15 pm ET regular close and the 9:30 am ET open. The cash session is shut during that stretch, but futures keep trading.

Why it matters: overnight liquidity is usually thinner, with fewer participants, and news released outside US hours can produce wider spreads and faster moves. Knowing where the session boundaries fall helps you see which session set a given high or low.
