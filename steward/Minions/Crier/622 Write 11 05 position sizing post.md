Position sizing, the part most traders skip

Before you enter, decide the dollar amount you can lose on the trade, then size backward from your stop. Risk per trade, stop distance, and contract value set the position. Conviction doesn't.

Illustration: account of 10,000, risk 1 percent = 100 per trade. With a 20 point stop on a contract worth 5 per point, risk is 100, so 1 contract. With a 10 point stop, risk is 50 per contract, so 2 contracts.

Question for the room: do you size from your stop or from a fixed contract count, and what's your max risk per trade?

Education only, not financial advice.
