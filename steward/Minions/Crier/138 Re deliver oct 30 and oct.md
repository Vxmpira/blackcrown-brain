**Session levels for ES and NQ: a marking routine**

Before the open, mark five levels on your chart: prior day high and low, Asia session high and low, and London session high and low. Note which ones price has already traded through and which are still untouched.

Untouched levels are where resting orders tend to sit, so they are worth watching for reaction. Write down what you expect at each level before the bell, then compare the outcome to your notes.

Illustration: ES prior day high 5,412, Asia low 5,388. If overnight price trades to 5,388, mark that level as taken and log how price reacted.

Repeat the process for NQ with its own levels, since the two indices don't always sweep the same highs and lows. Session levels are a map to study, not a signal.

**How to read a Presidential Wire item**

Each Presidential Wire entry has four fields. Read them in order.

**Impact:** how much the headline could move index futures if the market treats it as new information. Higher impact means it deserves attention before the open.

**Direction:** the scored lean for ES and NQ: bullish, bearish, or mixed. It describes the read, not where price will go.

**Confidence:** how certain the scoring is. Low confidence usually means the headline is ambiguous or sources disagree.

**Korvus read:** the written explanation and context. Read it before deciding the item matters.

A high impact item with low confidence is a reason to check the primary source, not to act. Not financial advice.
