A journaling habit that teaches more than any indicator setting: when price tests one level, log the level, the timeframe, what you expected, and what would invalidate it, all before you check the result. Then add the outcome in one plain line. Win or loss, the entry measures whether your read was clear, not whether the market agreed. Thirty entries show which levels you actually read well. LodeStone can mark the levels on your chart. The log stays yours. Not financial advice.
