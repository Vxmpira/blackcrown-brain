Session open against prior day high and low

Traders often ask what the first hour means when price opens inside yesterday's range versus outside it. Here is a simple way to frame it.

Mark the prior day high and low before the cash open. Note where the session opens: above the high, below the low, or inside the range. Then watch whether price accepts or rejects those levels over the first 30 to 60 minutes. Acceptance means price holds beyond the level on closes. Rejection means it returns back inside.

Log each day in a short journal. Over a few weeks you will see which behavior your market tends to repeat, and whether your own read holds up.
