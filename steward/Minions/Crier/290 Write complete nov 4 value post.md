Before the New York open, most index futures traders want the same reference points on the chart: prior day high and low, and overnight high and low. The useful question is less whether price respects them every time and more how it behaves when it trades into one. Does it stall, sweep and reverse, or accept and keep moving? Log that reaction for a week before deciding what it means for your process. Recording what happened at each level, rather than what you expected, is what turns levels into data you can review.

Education only. Not financial advice.
