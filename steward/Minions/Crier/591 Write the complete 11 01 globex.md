Globex high and low before the open

Index futures trade nearly around the clock on CME Globex, Sunday 6:00 pm to Friday 5:00 pm ET, with a daily halt from 5:00 to 6:00 pm ET.

The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low.

Those overnight extremes are prices the market already traded while most traders slept. Watching how price reacts to them into the open can add context to your read of the day. It is a reference point, not a signal.
