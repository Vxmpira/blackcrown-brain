**Eclipse-X Education, 11-06 (Position Sizing)**

Position sizing for index futures, quick math. Pick the dollar amount you can lose on one trade. Measure your stop in points. Contracts equal risk divided by stop points times point value. Example: an ES stop of 8 points is 400 dollars per contract. A 100 dollar risk allows zero full ES contracts, but two MES at 40 dollars each. If the math says zero, the setup is too wide for your account. Skip it.
