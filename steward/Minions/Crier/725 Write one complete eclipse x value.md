Before the open, most of what you need is already on the chart: yesterday's high, low, and close. Here is a simple way to read where price sits against that range.

1. Check where overnight trade sits against yesterday's high and low. Holding above the high suggests buyers are defending it. Holding below the low suggests the reverse.
2. Note which half of yesterday's range price is in. A close near the top or bottom can shape how the first hour behaves.
3. Compare yesterday's close with current price. Is the gap still open, or has it been filled?

Illustration: yesterday's range 5,000 to 5,040, close 5,035, overnight trade at 5,028. Price is in the upper half but has slipped below yesterday's close.

Write down what you see before the bell, then compare your read with the first hour.
