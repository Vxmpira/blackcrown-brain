**Position sizing basics: fixed dollar risk per trade**

Before you pick a contract count, decide the dollar amount you accept losing if your stop is hit. Size to that number, not to how confident the setup feels.

**Illustration (hypothetical numbers):** $10,000 account, $100 fixed risk per trade, each point worth $5 per contract.
- 10 point stop: $50 risk per contract, so 2 contracts fit.
- 20 point stop: $100 risk per contract, so 1 contract fits.

Wider stops mean fewer contracts for the same dollar risk. The math stays the same whatever the setup looks like.

Traders who size by feel often find their losses grow on wide-stop days, which is when a fixed rule matters most. Results depend on your edge, your execution, and your discipline.

What per-trade risk rule do you use? Share the number, not the strategy.
