**Try a one-week session journal**

Pick three levels you watch every session: the prior day high, the prior day low, and one level you already trust. Before the open, write them down. Then, for each one, log what price did when it got there: rejected, accepted through, swept and returned, or ignored.

After five sessions, read the log back. Most traders find they overrate some levels and underrate others. The journal won't tell you what to trade, but it will show you which levels actually mean something on your chart and which ones you only remember when they worked.

Keep it simple. A notes app is enough. Bring your best entry to #trade-recaps if you want feedback.
