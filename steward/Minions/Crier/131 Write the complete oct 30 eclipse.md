**Eclipse-X post, #korvus-industries (Oct 30)**

Quick tooling note for index futures traders.

Before the US open, many traders mark the Asia and London session highs and lows, plus the prior day's high and low. Those levels don't predict anything by themselves, but they're where a lot of participants are looking, so they make a shared map of where price has already reacted.

A useful review habit: after each session, note whether price respected, swept, or ignored those levels, then check back over a few weeks. You'll learn which levels actually mean something on your instrument and which are noise.

Illustration: ES swept the Asia high at 5,412 and reversed within 15 minutes. That's one data point, not a pattern.

If you'd rather not build the tracking yourself, Korvus has a free Observer tier worth a look. Education only, not financial advice.
