Quick chart habit that prevents confusion at the open: label the prior day high, prior day low, and prior day close before the session starts, then leave them alone.

Use short, consistent tags like PDH, PDL, and PDC so you can read the chart at a glance. Give each type its own color so highs and lows never blend together. Skip the clutter of drawing every wick. The extremes are usually what price comes back to test.

For each level, write one line: where price sits relative to it, and what you would need to see to change your read. If you cannot say it in one sentence, the level probably isn't doing much work on your chart.
