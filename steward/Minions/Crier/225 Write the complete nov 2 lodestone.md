**LodeStone: session levels for index futures**

If you trade NQ or ES, most of your context lives on the chart. LodeStone draws it in one place: session ranges with timezone handling, prior day and prior week levels, premium and discount, resting liquidity, SMT divergence between related index markets, a restrained volume profile, and sweep cleanup so spent levels stop cluttering the chart.

Zero repainting means levels are drawn from completed session data, so a level you saw printed stays where it printed.

There are two versions. The Tradovate build is 149 dollars one time, delivered as a code file to your checkout email. The TradingView version is 19.99 a month, and it comes free with Korvus Operator. Buy buttons are in #korvus-indicator.
