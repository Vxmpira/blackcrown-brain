Five levels to mark before the cash open on index futures:

1. **Prior day high and low.** Price often returns to them to decide the next move.
2. **Prior week high and low.** A break here usually shifts the bias for the day.
3. **Overnight high and low.** Asia and London ranges, often tested at the open.
4. **Midpoint of the prior range.** Shows whether price trades at a premium or discount.
5. **Unfilled imbalance from the prior session.** Price often revisits these before continuing.

Mark them, then watch how price reacts instead of predicting it.

Education only, not financial advice.
