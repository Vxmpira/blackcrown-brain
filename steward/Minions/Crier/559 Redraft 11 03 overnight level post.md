**Overnight levels: the education version**

Globex is not a 6:00 pm to 9:30 am session. Equity index futures trade nearly around the clock from Sunday evening through Friday afternoon ET, with a daily maintenance halt. Check the current hours in the CME contract spec for E-mini S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 before relying on them. [CME SPEC CAPTURE DATE: ____]

What's worth tracking is the overnight session, 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open. It often prints its own high and low. Marking those levels before the open gives you a reference for how price behaves once cash starts.

Question for the room: do you mark overnight highs and lows before the open, or do you wait for cash to set the levels?
