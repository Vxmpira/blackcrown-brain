Quick tip for the Presidential Wire: read each scored item in three passes before you react.

1. **Impact:** How much could this move the index futures session? A loud headline with low impact rarely deserves your attention.
2. **Direction:** Does the score lean up, down, or mixed? A mixed read is information too.
3. **Confidence:** A high-impact item with low confidence is a reason to watch the next print, not a reason to act.

Then read the written Korvus read last, and check whether it matches what your own chart is showing. When they disagree, that's worth a second look before you decide anything.
