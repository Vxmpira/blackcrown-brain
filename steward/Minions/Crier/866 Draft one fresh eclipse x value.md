**How to read the Presidential Wire**

Every item in #presidential-news carries four parts, so you can judge it before you react:

- **Impact:** how much the story could matter to index futures, not how loud the headline is.
- **Direction:** the lean the item suggests for the market.
- **Confidence:** how sure the scoring is about that lean. Low confidence means treat it as a question, not an answer.
- **Korvus read:** a short written explanation of why the score landed where it did.

Try this: when a headline hits, check the confidence and the reasoning before you look at the direction. A lean with little evidence behind it is where many traders get caught.

Not financial advice. The Wire is information and education, not a signal to buy or sell. Your decisions and risk are your own.
