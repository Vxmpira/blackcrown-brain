**Reading a scored headline before you trade the news**

The Presidential Wire scores each story three ways: impact, direction, and confidence. Impact is how much a release or statement could move index futures. Direction is the lean the score carries, not a call on where price goes. Confidence is how sure the scoring is of its own read, and low confidence tells you something too.

Read the three together. High impact with low confidence often means the market has not settled on a meaning yet. Then check the Korvus read underneath and test it against your own plan.

Illustration only. A score is a starting point for your own analysis, not a signal. Not financial advice.
