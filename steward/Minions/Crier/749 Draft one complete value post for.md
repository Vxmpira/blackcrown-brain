Quick habit for ES and NQ traders: before the open, mark the prior day high and low, then the prior week high and low. On a quiet morning, price often reacts first at those levels. Note whether price trades through them, holds, or sweeps and reverses. Log it for a week. You will know which levels actually matter on your instrument before you risk a contract on them.
