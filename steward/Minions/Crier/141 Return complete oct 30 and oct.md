**Oct 30, #strategies**

Session levels for ES and NQ, and how to use them without guessing. Start with the prior day high and low, then mark the overnight high and low from the Globex session. Add the prior week high and low if price is trading near them, since more desks watch those levels. Before the open, note where price sits relative to each level: above, below, or inside. Write your plan as a condition, such as what you want to see if price trades back into the overnight range, rather than a prediction. Check ES and NQ separately, because the two can respond differently to the same catalyst. After the session, log which levels held and which broke. Over a few weeks that log shows which levels actually matter for your instrument and your hours. Education only, not financial advice.

**Oct 31, #presidential-news**

How to read a Presidential Wire item in four steps. First, read the headline for what happened, not what it might mean. Second, check the impact score to decide whether the item belongs on your screen during your session. Third, read direction and confidence together. A strong direction with low confidence is a reason to wait for confirmation in price, not a signal to act. Fourth, read the written Korvus read last, and compare it against what the market is actually doing. The read is an interpretation to test, not an instruction. Note each item's time against your own chart so you can see how price reacted in the minutes that followed. Treat the score as one input among several. If a read surprises you, bring the question here.

**Oct 31, #korvus-industries**

Pre-open checklist for Korvus Observer, the free tier. Before the bell, open Observer and scan the latest items first, since overnight developments usually set the opening tone. Note the impact and direction of anything new, then cross-check it against the levels you marked in #strategies. Focus on what changed since the last session rather than re-reading the full feed. Keep a short list of three things you want answered before the open, and mark each one answered or open once the session starts. Treat anything you cannot verify as unconfirmed until price or a second source confirms it. Observer is information and education only, not financial advice. Post what you noticed in this channel so members can compare their checklists.
