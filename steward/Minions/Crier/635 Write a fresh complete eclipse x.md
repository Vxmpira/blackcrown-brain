**Session levels before the open**

Before the cash session starts, mark the Asia range, the London range, and the prior day high and low. These levels give you a map for the morning, so you can react to price instead of predicting it.

Three questions to ask each session:

1. Did price sweep a prior session high or low, or stay inside the range?
2. Where did price accept after the sweep?
3. Does the New York open respect or reject the London range?

If you use LodeStone on TradingView, the session and prior day level inputs draw these lines and let you set the timezone for your own sessions.

Educational only, not financial advice.
