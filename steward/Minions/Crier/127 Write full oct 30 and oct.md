**REPLACEMENT** | Oct 30 | #strategies | Eclipse-X

Session levels give your chart context before the open. Before the cash session on index futures, run this checklist:

1. Mark the prior day high and low.
2. Note the Asia and London ranges so you know where price has already traded.
3. Check whether the overnight high or low was taken before the open.
4. Write down what a break and hold would mean for your plan, and what a failed break would mean.

The goal is not to predict. It is to decide in advance which levels matter, so you react to price instead of guessing. LodeStone draws these session levels on TradingView if you want them automated, but the checklist works on any chart. Education only, not financial advice.

**REPLACEMENT** | Oct 31 | #presidential-news | Eclipse-X

Presidential Wire posts scored news items, and the useful part is the reasoning, not the headline. When one lands, read it in this order:

Impact: how much could this move index futures if it plays out?
Direction: which way does the scored read lean?
Confidence: how sure is the read, and what would make it wrong?
Korvus read: the written context explaining why the score landed where it did.

Then check the chart. Did price respond to the item, or ignore it? High impact with no price reaction tells you something too. Scores are a starting point for your own process, not a signal to act on. Education only, not financial advice.
