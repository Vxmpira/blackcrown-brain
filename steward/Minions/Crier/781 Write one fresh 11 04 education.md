Education of the day: what is a prior day high and low? They are the highest and lowest prices from the previous session. Many index futures traders mark them on the chart as reference points before the open, then study how price has behaved around them over time. Historical behavior is not a guarantee of future behavior, so treat these levels as something to observe and test on your own charts, not as signals.
