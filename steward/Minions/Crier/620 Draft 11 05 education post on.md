**Position sizing basics**

Most traders spend hours on entries and minutes on size. Size decides how much one wrong idea costs you, so it deserves the same attention.

A simple way to start:
1. Set the dollar amount you are willing to lose on one trade before you enter.
2. Measure your stop distance in points.
3. Divide the first number by the cost of your stop.

Illustrative: a $100 max loss with a 10 point stop on MES (about $5 per point per contract) allows 2 contracts. $5 x 10 points x 2 = $100.

Recheck the math whenever your stop changes, not only when your account does.

Question for the room: what do you size against, a fixed dollar amount, a percent of account, or something else?

Education only, not financial advice.
