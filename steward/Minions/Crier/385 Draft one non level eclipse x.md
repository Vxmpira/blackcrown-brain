**Post text:**

Sweep then reclaim gets misread constantly on index futures. Price takes out an older high, clears the stops above it, and closes back inside. Before you trust the reversal read, check three things. Did the sweep hold a close beyond the high, or just poke through? Did the move back inside come with range expansion or fading volume? Does the next push fail near the swept area or drift away from it? Each answer changes the read. None of this is a signal on its own, just a way to judge what the auction actually did.
