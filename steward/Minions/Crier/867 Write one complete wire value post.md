**Reading the Presidential Wire, field by field**

Every Wire item carries four fields. Here's how to read them.

**Impact:** how much weight the headline could carry for index futures. High impact means the story touches a major market lever. It says nothing about direction.

**Direction:** the scored lean of the headline for the index: bullish, bearish, or mixed. Read it as a reading of the news, not a forecast.

**Confidence:** how sure the scoring is about impact and direction. Low confidence usually means the story is still developing, so check the source before you act on it.

**Korvus read:** a written take that ties the other three together in plain language. Use it as context for your own plan.
