Overnight vs. regular session levels on index futures

The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low. Regular trading hours for these contracts run 9:30 am to 4:15 pm ET.

Why it matters for your chart: a level that held in the regular session may sit inside the overnight range, and the overnight high or low often becomes a reference point once the cash open arrives. Check both ranges before the bell so you know which levels are fresh and which are already in play.
