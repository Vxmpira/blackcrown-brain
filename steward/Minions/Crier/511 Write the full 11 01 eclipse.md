Quick one for index futures traders: before the open, where do you mark the prior day high and low, and the overnight range?

Here's how we read it at Korvus. Each session leaves levels that price tends to revisit. The useful question isn't whether a level holds, it's what price does when it trades through one and comes back. A sweep of the prior day high that closes back inside the range reads differently than a clean break that holds.

Illustration: prior day high 5,412. Overnight range 5,398 to 5,419. Price trades 5,414, then closes back at 5,406. That's a reference point to log, not a signal.

LodeStone plots the session and prior day and week levels on your chart, so you're not rebuilding them by hand. The Korvus wire logs scored headlines with a written read, so you can check whether news hit during a sweep or after it.

How do you mark the overnight range before the bell, and do you change your read when a level gets swept?
