Four session levels worth marking before the open on index futures:

1. Prior day high
2. Prior day low
3. Prior week close
4. Overnight range high and low

Price trading into one of these and stalling tells you something. Price running through one on real volume tells you something else. Mark them before the bell, then watch how the first hour treats each one.

Not financial advice. Levels are reference points, not predictions.
