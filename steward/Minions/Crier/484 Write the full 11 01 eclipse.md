**Overnight levels, before the open**

The overnight session, from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low. Regular hours for these contracts run 9:30 am to 4:15 pm ET, so the two ranges answer different questions.

Prior day levels show where yesterday's regular session auctioned. Overnight levels show where price traded while the cash session was closed, and they often mark where the open is first tested.

Before the bell, mark both sets on your chart, note which one price is respecting, and write down what would change your read. Education only, not a trade call.
