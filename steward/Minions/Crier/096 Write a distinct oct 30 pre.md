**Pre-session habit: check the USD agenda before the chart**

Before the cash open, check the day's USD agenda in #forex-news. Note which releases fall near 8:30 and 10:00 ET, and whether the forecast sits far from the prior print. Those are the releases that tend to move ES and NQ first, so you know their timing before price does.

Then scan Presidential Wire for overnight high impact items and read the full Korvus read, not just the headline. Decide in advance what would change your plan.

After the close, run the same check in reverse: which release actually moved price, did it follow the scored direction, and where did the session stall? Write one line in a journal. Weeks of that log will show your process more clearly than any single trade.

Educational only, not financial advice.
