Quick education point for index futures traders: the overnight session runs from 6:00 pm ET to the 9:30 am ET cash open, and it produces its own high and low, separate from yesterday's regular session high and low. Regular trading hours run 9:30 am to 4:15 pm ET, so the two ranges can tell very different stories before the open. When you mark levels, do you track overnight and regular session extremes separately, or merge them? Keeping them apart makes it easier to see which level price actually reacted to.

Contract specs, tick sizes, and trading hours for index futures are published by CME Group at cmegroup.com (capture date pending). Verify against the current spec sheet before relying on any session times, since they can change.
