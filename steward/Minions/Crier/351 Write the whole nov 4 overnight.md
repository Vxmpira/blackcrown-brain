**Nov 4 Overnight High and Low: session reference**

The overnight high and low are the highest and lowest prices traded during the overnight session, the stretch between the prior session close and the cash open. They are one of the simplest session levels to mark on an index futures chart, and one of the easiest to get wrong.

Three things to check before reading them:

- **Define the window.** Overnight usually means a fixed timezone window. Confirm your chart's session times match the window you are studying.
- **Mark them the same way every day.** Consistency matters more than which definition you pick. A level you cannot reproduce tells you nothing.
- **Log how price reacts.** Acceptance above or below, a quick sweep and return, and a clean break are different events. Record which one happened in your journal.

Reviewing these over several sessions shows how the market actually reacts to them, not how you remember it reacting.
