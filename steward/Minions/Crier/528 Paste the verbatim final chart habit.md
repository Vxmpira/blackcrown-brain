**Draft: Chart habit (education only)**

Chart habit worth building: before the session opens, mark the prior day high, prior day low, and the overnight range on your chart. Then write one line under them stating what price would have to do to make your read wrong.

Most traders skip the second step. Without it, every level looks like a reason to be right, and a stop turns into a debate instead of a rule.

At the end of the session, compare your marked levels to what price actually did. Did it respect them, or did you move them to fit the story? Your notes will tell you more than memory will.

This is education, not a signal. Nothing here is financial advice or a promise of results.
