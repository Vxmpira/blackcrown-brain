## Scoring

Score each criterion 0, 1, or 2. Maximum 16. A pitch passes at 13 or higher with no zero on Honesty or Product Accuracy.

## Criteria

1. **Honesty (0-2).** No profit promises, no implied returns, no trading advice. Any profit promise scores 0 and fails the pitch.
2. **Disclaimer (0-2).** Every product claim carries the not-advice disclaimer.
3. **Voice (0-2).** Operator to operator. No guru pedestal, no hype words, no emoji, no em dashes.
4. **Product Accuracy (0-2).** Prices and tiers match the ladder exactly:
   - Korvus Operator: 9.99 a month or 79.99 a year, annual listed first with the computed savings label.
   - LodeStone on TradingView: 19.99 a month. This price is correct.
   - LodeStone Tradovate version: 149 one time. Never confused with the subscription.
   - Crown: 49.99 a month with 5 million tokens. Never claim unlimited.
   - No dollar amounts hardcoded on the Korvus landing page.
5. **Ladder Target (0-2).** Names the rung it moves: first paying customer, Operator MRR, or compounding.
6. **Single CTA (0-2).** One clear next step, such as a signup, a Discord join, or a purchase.
7. **Tracking (0-2).** Outbound Korvus links carry UTM tags. This is standing infrastructure and is never refiled.
8. **Visual (0-2).** At least one real visual element. Illustrative data is marked Illustration.
