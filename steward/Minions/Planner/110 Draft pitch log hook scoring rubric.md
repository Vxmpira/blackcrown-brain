# Pitch Log Hook Scoring Rubric

Scope: korvus.industries, visit-to-click stage. Score each hook 0 to 2 per criterion, 10 maximum. Log the score beside the UTM-tagged annual Operator link so clicks can be read against scores. Click rates are not instrumented yet for hook-level scores; file that job if Herald needs it.

**1. Specific promise**
- 0: vague ("level up your trading")
- 1: names the category but not the value
- 2: states what Korvus does for index futures traders in one line

**2. Honest framing**
- 0: implies profit, returns, or signals
- 1: neutral but missing the not-advice disclaimer on a product claim
- 2: no return language, disclaimer present wherever a claim appears

**3. Operator voice**
- 0: guru tone or hype words
- 1: mixed
- 2: plain, peer to peer, speaks from inside the arena

**4. Next step clarity**
- 0: no action
- 1: action implied
- 2: one clear action pointing to the annual Operator CTA

**5. Proof element**
- 0: text only
- 1: generic visual
- 2: concrete visual, such as a feature chip set or a data mock marked Illustration

**Bands:** 8 to 10 ship, 5 to 7 revise, 0 to 4 rewrite.
