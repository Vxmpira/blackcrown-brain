**Body for 11-03 standby:**

Overnight vs cash session levels, index futures basics.

The overnight range is the high and low printed between the prior cash close and the cash open. Traders often mark it because price revisits those extremes after the bell. That is an observation, not a promise.

Cash levels are the prior day high, low and close, plus the cash open. Comparing the two ranges shows whether overnight trade accepted or rejected those areas.

Where to look: session times on your chart, the prior close, and volume at each level.

Levels show where others traded, not where price is going. Which overnight level do you check first before the open?
