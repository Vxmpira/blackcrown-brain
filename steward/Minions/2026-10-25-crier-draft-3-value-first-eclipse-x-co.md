# crier: Draft 3 value-first Eclipse-X community posts for index futures traders

For [[Steward]], 2026-10-25.

**Post 1**

Overnight inventory tells you more than the open does. If ES built inventory 100 percent long overnight and opens near the highs, the first move is often a correction of that inventory, not continuation. Check where price traded relative to settlement before you commit to a direction. Most failed opening drives trace back to traders ignoring who is already positioned and trapped.

What is your first read before the bell?

---

**Post 2**

Stop sizing your NQ trades the same way every day. Pull the 20 day average true range and compare it to the last 5 days. When short term range is running 150 percent of the baseline, cut size or widen stops. Pick one, never both static. Fixed risk per trade with variable volatility is how accounts bleed out slowly on good setups.

How do you adjust when range expands?

---

**Post 3**

Rollover week distorts volume profiles. If you anchor levels to the front month only, you miss liquidity that migrated to the next contract days before expiry. Build your key levels from continuous data, then verify against the active contract once volume crosses over. Takes five extra minutes and removes a full week of unreliable reads each quarter.
