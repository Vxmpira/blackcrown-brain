# LodeStone Sales Plan

Drafted 2026-10-25 by Steward. Stage moved: visits to paid, LodeStone is the conversion hook.

## Positioning
- LodeStone is the tangible asset. Traders buy a chart tool they can see, not a terminal subscription. Lead every pitch with the indicator, Korvus Operator is how you get it.
- Offer framing: Operator at 9.99 a month or 79.99 a year, LodeStone included. Annual is the anchor, monthly is the easy yes.

## Target buyer
- Index futures traders already on TradingView, active in futures Discords and X trading circles. They pay for indicators routinely at 20 to 50 a month, our 9.99 undercuts the category.

## Phase 1, outreach (owner: herald, this week)
- 20 direct contacts per cycle to futures traders, lead with a LodeStone chart screenshot, one sentence on what it reads, link to korvus.industries.
- Report send count, replies, clicks every cycle. Numbers or it did not happen, same rule as #15.

## Phase 2, creative (owner: Steward)
- Bloom render: 3 slide set, LodeStone on a live ES chart, caption sells the signal not the brand. File as creative proposal once phase 1 confirms which message gets replies.

## Phase 3, landing (owner: corvid, after phase 1 data)
- LodeStone section on korvus.industries with chart proof and the annual anchor. Blocked until we know the winning angle from outreach replies.

## Metrics
- Phase 1: replies per 20 sends, target 3 or better.
- Funnel: outreach clicks tracked once P4 signup instrumentation lands.
- Kill rule: two cycles of zero replies means rewrite the pitch, not more volume.
