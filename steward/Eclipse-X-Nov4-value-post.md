Title: Reading a release day without guessing

Most traders lose their edge on release days in the first two minutes, not the first two hours. Here is the checklist we run before any USD print. It costs nothing to use.

1. Know the time. The daily USD agenda in #forex-news lists each release and its scheduled time in ET. Set your alert before the open, not at the print.

2. Write the prior and the consensus down. Whatever the print does, you want the number you were anchored to on paper before it lands.

3. Mark your levels first. Prior day and prior week highs and lows, plus the session range you care about. If you use LodeStone, these are the session levels it draws. Decide in advance which level a reaction has to hold or break.

4. Set your size before the release, not after. A surprise is the normal case on a release day, and spread and fills can move against you for a few seconds.

5. Treat the first move as information, not a signal. A spike into a level is a question. Watch whether price accepts there or returns.

6. Log it. One line per release: time, print, reaction, what you did. After a month the log tells you more than any forecast.
