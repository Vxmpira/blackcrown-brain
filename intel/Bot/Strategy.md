# Strategy v3-all-sessions

> Generated by Korvus Intel. Do not edit by hand; the next sync overwrites this note.

**Paper only.** No broker connection, no real orders.

- **Trigger:** a universe filing scored impact med or high, confidence at least 60%, seen in the last 18 hours. Bullish read -> **long**. Bearish read -> **short**. Neutral -> no trade.
- **Confirm (long):** price above the reference price at the time the filing was seen, and up less than 8% on the day.
- **Confirm (short):** price below the reference price, and down less than 8% on the day (stays clear of the Rule 201 short-sale restriction at -10%).
- **Window:** around the clock, Sunday 6:00 PM ET to Friday 5:00 PM ET, except the 5:00 to 6:00 PM ET maintenance hour. Outside 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM it only acts on a trade printed in the last 15 minutes.
- **Size:** $10,000 paper notional, at most 5 open, one entry per symbol per day.
- **Exit:** 1.5% in our favor (target), 1.0% against (stop), or flat at 4:55 PM ET, before the maintenance hour. Same distances long and short.
- **Fills:** last quote with 0.05% slippage against us (0.15% outside the regular session), quotes polled every 60s. Real fills would be worse.
- **Shorts on paper** assume shares can be borrowed with no borrow fee. Real shorts need a margin account and an available borrow, and some names charge a daily fee.

Changing any rule ships as a new version with a fresh [[intel/Bot/Scoreboard|Scoreboard]]; history is never rewritten.
